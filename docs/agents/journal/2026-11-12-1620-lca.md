### B1 review: retention, data passed to teams, the team's commitment

**R-1 · O-6 (retention of rejected referrals). Gate for B1.1.**
- *Settled:* Δ5 is PLANNED. Phase A holds only synthetic data. Under DSG Art. 6 Abs. 4 [L1], personal data that are no longer needed for their purpose are destroyed or anonymised. A rejected referral that never became a case has no care purpose left. In D-9 (g2), the PO already decided that unconverted referrals expire and are anonymised before real data. Anonymised means the free-text fields `title`, `need_summary` and `notes` are removed, not only the name. The period is to be set together with D-4's final rule.
- *UNKNOWN:* the period itself. Whether a rejected referral belongs to the *Patientendokumentation* under GesG BS § 29 [BS1] (and the BL equivalent, not read). If it does, the cantonal record period applies; if not, only the purpose applies (proof of the rejection, a duplicate link, a complaint).
- **Verdict:**
  - (a) For B1.1 in Phase A, «nothing is deleted» is **compliant under every plausible interpretation** (synthetic data). **O-6 is not a blocker for B1.1.**
  - (b) As a permanent rule for real data, «never deleted» conflicts with Art. 6 Abs. 4 and D-9 g2. This is **unresolved: needs legal adviser and owner.** It is already D-9/5 and D-4 in the catalogue. The exact question: «How long may a private home-care provider in BS/BL keep a referral it rejected or that was withdrawn, and is it part of the Patientendokumentation?»
- **Conditions for B1.1, so that later anonymisation stays possible** (PLANNED):
  - **(R1-a)** Wording: «In Phase A wird nichts gelöscht». Not «Zeilen werden nie gelöscht».
  - **(R1-b)** The append-only audit of a rejection stores status, reason code, time, actor and a link. It does **not** copy the referral's free text or the «Anderer Grund» remark. The remark stays in the referral row, where it can later be blanked together with `title` and `need_summary`. Test: pgTAP, the audit row of a rejection holds no free-text column.
  - **(R1-c)** The rejection reasons are worded without client data: «Duplikat», «ausserhalb der Region», …. The «Anderer Grund» help text says «ohne Namen und Gesundheitsangaben».

**R-2 · O-1 / Δ4 (title on the team offer).**
- *Settled (IMPLEMENTED):* the free-text referral title becomes `cases.bedarf` and then the offer title «Teamsuche: <Titel>». It is shown to every team of the region (0088; 0034:193). Under FE-040, GM-1 teams are separate providers. Passing a client's identifying or health data to providers that do not care for her is a disclosure of sensitive data to third parties. Without a justification, that is a violation (DSG Art. 30 Abs. 2 lit. c, Art. 31 Abs. 1 [L1]). For a referrer bound by professional secrecy it also falls under StGB 321 [L11].
- *Reading:* a help text («ohne Namen und Adresse») informs the person typing; it does not prevent the disclosure. A diagnosis or a rare service in a small region can identify a person without any name.
- **Verdict:**
  - (a) On synthetic data, the hint (Δ4 as specified) is **compliant under every plausible interpretation.**
  - (b) For real data, the most protective option is **compliant under every plausible interpretation for the step NAVORA controls**: the offer shows only structured data (services from the list, region, scope, start, urgency, order type) and a title generated from them, never free text. With the hint alone, compliance depends on every person typing correctly. That is **not** compliant under every plausible interpretation.
- *Recommendation (to `po`):* keep the hint in B1.1. Before real data, move the offer to structured services; this belongs in B1.1b with the referrer's text.

**R-4 · Insurer, insurance type, residence per order type** (3.1).

**R-6 · Meaning of «Versorgung zugesagt»** (§5).
- *Settled from the text:*
  - A contract needs mutual assent, express or tacit ([B2] Art. 1). A care mandate obliges the agent to perform the services «vertragsgemäss» ([B1] Art. 394 Abs. 1).
  - A mandate can be ended by either side at any time, but ending it «zur Unzeit» makes the withdrawing side liable for the damage caused ([B1] Art. 404). This fits the PO's rule that a withdrawal after the commitment is a handover or an exception (B1.3), never a silent drop.
  - **Risk to name:** under [B1] Art. 395, a mandate that is not rejected at once counts as accepted when the agent does such business commercially («gewerbsmässig»). Whether an offer sent to several teams at once is such a mandate, or only an invitation, is UNKNOWN. The «Wir prüfen» signal must not be worded or built so that it could be read as acceptance.
- *UNKNOWN:*
  - who the parties are: the client, or her representative, and the team. The team's legal form is open (FE-070 (1)). A hospital or home as the client is the D-44 constellation.
  - whether pressing «zusagen» in NAVORA is itself the declaration, or only announces one.
- **Verdict:** **unresolved: needs legal adviser and owner.**
  - The exact question: «For a self-organised care team without a decided legal form, does a team member's commitment entered in a software platform conclude a care mandate (OR Art. 394), with whom (client, representative, home or hospital), and can an unanswered offer to several teams count as accepted under OR Art. 395?»
  - Until answered, the PO's wording «Zusage des Teams» is the safe side. **(R6-a):** «Wir prüfen» carries the words «keine Zusage». **(R6-b):** the commitment screen names the team, never NAVORA, as the one committing.
  - This question goes into the catalogue once it is signed in a package (D-44).
