### (2) The rest-time reference point is the end of block 2

- The reading is consistent. ArG Art. 15a Abs. 1 requires a daily rest of at least 11 *consecutive* hours, so the gap between the two blocks of one day is not rest (sources read 2026-11-26, listed below).

### (3) The busy pause is not working time (510 minutes)

- The reading is consistent, on one condition, which is unchanged from #1790. ArG Art. 15 Abs. 2 and ArGV 1 Art. 13 and 15 count a pause as working time if the person may not leave the workplace or is on call on site.
- In NAVORA, «busy» is a planning lock only: the platform offers no other order for that span. It is not a duty to stay on site or to be on call. On this basis, not counting the 4 h 30 min (510 minutes worked) and not billing it is the consistent reading.
- **Real data (OPEN, owner or Phase B home):** the home must confirm that the person is free during the gap. Otherwise the gap is working time, and the minutes, Pensum and drafts come out too low. The UI must never say or suggest that she has to stay available during the pause (this is a proposal for `uxa`).
- Outside scope, unchanged: a block longer than 5½ h still needs its own pause (ArGV 1 Art. 18 Abs. 2). Block 1 here (07:00–11:30) is 4½ h and block 2 (16:00–20:00) is 4 h, so it does not apply to the example. It applies to other splits.
