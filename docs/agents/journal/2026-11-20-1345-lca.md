### BK-f-F3 (PO observation, view requested; not a merge condition)

`team_prices_read` lets every member of an organisation read every team's price. Phase A (one simulated organisation): harmless. Before **several economically independent teams** share one organisation, the most protective option is: a team reads only its own price; other teams see none (the client sees a price only on its own sent invoice). Reason: if the teams are independent undertakings, a shared view of each other's prices is an exchange of competition-sensitive information (WEKO Merkblatt, above) and could support price alignment. If the teams are cells of one cooperative (one undertaking), the question may not arise. Which applies is **unresolved: needs legal adviser** (question: «Are team cells within one care cooperative separate undertakings under KG Art. 2 and 5, so that a platform view of each other's hourly prices is an exchange of competition-sensitive information?»).
