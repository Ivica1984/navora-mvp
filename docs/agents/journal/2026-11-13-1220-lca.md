### 3 · Confirmed attributes as staff data (BK-2, BK-3, BK-4)

- Attributes such as «Demenzerfahrung bestätigt», language, qualification level, prior knowledge of an institution are personal data of the team member. Where the team (or its legal entity) is employer, OR Art. 328b [S4] limits processing to suitability for the work or the contract: **experience, qualification and language for an order are suitability data** and fit.
- **Protective rules:** (A-a) attributes are **work-related only**; never health, vaccination, pregnancy, absence or sickness reasons, union or religion, never «belastbar»-type judgements; (A-b) the client's offer card names the **team** and the attribute («im Team bestätigt: Demenzerfahrung»), **not a member's name**, until the team has taken the order and assigned the carer; (A-c) a «confirmed» attribute records who confirmed it and when (audit), and the member can see and contest her own attributes (DSG access and correction); (A-d) the AI ranking (BK-3) reads attributes only on synthetic data (D-35 rule A).
