### L-15 · NAVORA's role when rules forward a request to teams

- **Settled (on the sources read):**
  - AVG placement aims at an **employment contract** (OR Art. 319 ff.). Bringing two parties together for a **mandate or a contract for work** is **not** placement in the AVG sense ([V1] p. 20; [V2] Art. 1 speaks of job seekers and employers).
  - Where it *is* employment placement, doing it through a website or an app is always placement ([V1] p. 22). A rule engine is not an exception.
  - Whether the team really contracts on its own account is judged on the economic facts, not on the contract's words ([V1] p. 75; BGE 149 V 57, D-29). The indicators of staff lending are the client's instruction right, integration, the client's tools and billing by hours without a fixed price ([V1] p. 76; [V2] Art. 26 Abs. 2).
- **Reading:** the legal role depends on **what contract the team concludes and how it really works**, much more than on how NAVORA routes the request.
  - Team concludes a care mandate in its own name, plans and instructs its own members, bears its own risk, own costs: on [V1] p. 20 the forwarding is **not** AVG placement, whether the requester chooses, NAVORA proposes, or NAVORA forwards.
  - Team members are in substance employees (of a carrier, D-12 B, or of nobody, the legal form being open, FE-070 (1)), or the home instructs them: then a lending or employment relation exists somewhere ([V2] Art. 26), and a platform that brings the two sides together risks being a placement agent under AVG Art. 2 (licence). For homes this is the open Q-44a.
  - **The three designs** («Auftraggeber wählt selbst», «Plattform schlägt vor», «Plattform leitet weiter») do not change the AVG test on the sources read: [V1] p. 21 counts even letting parties find each other through a search function. They change **NAVORA's own duty of care**: the more NAVORA selects, the more a selection error is NAVORA's (D-29 point 4, «the platform's own check duty cannot be shifted by AGB where it assigns»). That is a liability question, UNKNOWN in its extent.
- **Note on the PO measure «keine Vermittlung»** (Lieferung 2 §2.2): the share of requests closed «ohne eine menschliche Handlung bei NAVORA» is a good product measure, but it has no legal meaning. An automated forwarding is not less «Vermittlung» in law than a human one ([V1] p. 22). **Proposal:** call it «ohne menschliche Disposition» or similar, so no document reads it as a legal classification.
- **UNKNOWN:** the team's legal form (FE-070 (1), not decided); whether a home that orders a team (KON-1) keeps instruction rights through its *pflegefachverantwortliche Person* (Q-44a); whether SECO's p. 20 reading holds for care work integrated in a home; liability of the selecting platform; OR Art. 412 ff. ### CGR-195 (3) · Responsibility during a hand-over and for an inactive team's case

- **Settled from the text** ([B3], [B1]):
  - A mandate is performed personally. Transfer to a third party is allowed only with authority, by necessity of the circumstances, or where substitution is customary (Art. 398 Abs. 3). A team that passes the care to another team **without** authority stays liable for the other team's acts as its own (Art. 399 Abs. 1); with authority, only for care in choosing and instructing it (Abs. 2).
  - Either side may end a mandate at any time, but the side ending it «zur Unzeit» pays the damage it causes (Art. 404). For an ongoing care relation, stopping before another provider takes over is the typical risk of «Unzeit».
  - So **in contract law**, the existing mandate does not end because a team becomes inactive in NAVORA or asks for a hand-over. It ends by notice, by agreement, or because the client accepts a new team. This supports `cra`'s safety condition «the old team stays responsible until a new team accepts» **as far as the contract reaches**.
- **Limits (UNKNOWN):**
  - who the parties are (client, representative, home, hospital; D-44) and the team's legal form (FE-070 (1)). A team that has dissolved may have no one who can perform;
  - professional and cantonal duties of continuity for the licence holder (BS GesG, BL law: not read for this question);
  - whether the client must agree to the new team. Under Art. 398 Abs. 3 a substitution needs authority; a new team is also a new recipient of health data (StGB 321, DSG Art. 30-31; D-9 questions 2 and 4).
- **Verdict on the wording (UXR-183, 0125 next step):**
  - The data-only wording `cra` requires is **compliant under every plausible interpretation**: «Das bisherige Team ist weiterhin eingetragen. Eine Übergabe ist in der App noch nicht möglich. Klären, welches Team übernimmt; nie automatisch zuweisen.» (PROPOSED, `uxa` owns the words.)
  - «Das bisherige Team bleibt verantwortlich» as a NAVORA text is **not** compliant under every plausible interpretation: NAVORA cannot allocate a legal responsibility, and for an inactive or dissolved team it may be untrue. **Do not use it.** Same reasoning as R-7 (16:20).
