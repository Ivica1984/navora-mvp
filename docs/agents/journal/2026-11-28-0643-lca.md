### (a) The monthly target is an input of the home, not a conversion

- IMPLEMENTED (code read): `institution_monthly_targets` holds the minutes as the home enters them. The comparison value (Pensum × Wochensoll × days ÷ 7) is used only for the warning and never overwrites the target. Only the lead writes. The shown value is compared under the person lock, so a change is never silent.
- Legal reading: this fits a contractual monthly target. The binding hours come from the employment contract (or a collective agreement or staff regulation), not from NAVORA's arithmetic. A conversion could deviate from the contract. Entering the value and showing a deviation is the more protective design.
- **Real data (OPEN, owner or Phase B home):** who the employer is and which document fixes the monthly hours (individual contract, collective agreement, cantonal staff law for a public home) is UNKNOWN (FE-082 to FE-084, D-12, D-25). The home must name that source, and the home stays responsible for the value. Hours above the contractual hours are overtime. NAVORA neither records nor pays it (payroll is out of scope, FE-078 (1)). The UI must not suggest that NAVORA has checked the value against the contract.

### (b) W1: a hard weekly maximum is needed before real data

- IMPLEMENTED (code read): with a target, R-m replaces R-h for that month. The week is then limited only by rest time (`min_rest_hours`) and days in a row (`max_consecutive_days`), both home parameters. There is no weekly maximum in the schema (ADR-231, W1). With six days in a row and long shifts, a week can be planned well above 50 h.
- Sources (read 2026-11-28): the weekly maximum is 45 h for industrial, office, technical and large-retail staff and **50 h for all other employees** (ArG Art. 9, as summarised on the SECO page). Overtime is allowed only **exceptionally**, at most 2 h a day, with yearly caps of 140 h at the 50-h maximum. For homes, ArGV 2 Art. 16 Abs. 2 (SECO Wegleitung, July 2023) names Alters- and Pflegeheime. Art. 16 makes ArGV 2 Art. 7 Abs. 2 applicable: up to 7 days in a row if 83 h of rest follow, **the 50 h averaged over two weeks**, and day and evening work capped at 9 h a day. ArGV 2 Art. 15 (hospitals and clinics) needs regular medical care, so it does not normally cover a nursing home (SECO Wegleitung, December 2017).
- Reading: for the simulation, the marker «Arbeitszeitgesetz nicht abschliessend geprüft» is enough. **Before real data, a hard weekly maximum is a precondition** (follow-up F-2). For real data, «leer = aus» is not protective enough. When no value is set, the check should fail closed or at least warn visibly, and NAVORA should not ship a number. The value (50 h, or a stricter contract value) and the averaging period (one or two weeks) depend on whether ArG and ArGV 2 Art. 16 apply. That is UNKNOWN: it depends on the employer status and the legal form of the Phase B home (ArG Art. 2, as read 2026-11-26). The daily 9-h cap for day and evening work (ArGV 2 Art. 7 Abs. 2) is a further real-data question. It belongs with R-j from #1790.
- **Verdict for real data: unresolved: needs owner and legal adviser.** The adviser question: «Gilt für Pflegepersonal, das ein selbstorganisiertes Team (Genossenschaft) in einem Basler Pflegeheim einsetzt, das ArG mit ArGV 2 Art. 16, oder kantonales Personalrecht?
