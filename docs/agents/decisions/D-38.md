# D-38 — Transfer terms of the partnership letter of intent

## Decision summary
D-12 is decided (owner 2026-10-27): B as an operating-model partnership with a licensed, OKP-admitted Zurich provider, a 12-month sunset, and the team-owned provider (C) prepared from month 0. The terms that make the sunset real are written nowhere. This package proposes the terms the partnership letter of intent (LOI) should contain **before the owner signs one**. Raised by `csa` as STR-15 (issue #539, high).

**Preliminary recommendation (Low, inputs open):** the LOI carries terms T1-T10 below. T1-T4 are protective terms `lca` already proposed (D-12, 13:52). T5-T7 and T9 wait for `lca` and the Zurich legal adviser. T8 and T10 are the owner's (pricing, partnership). Owner approval is required in any case.

## Context and problem statement
- **IMPLEMENTED:** nothing. No partner is named; no LOI exists; NAVORA holds synthetic data only.
- **Why it matters (care model):** the sunset is how a self-organised team becomes its own provider, keeps its clients' continuity and earns the provider margin instead of a salary inside a hierarchy (value order 2, 3, 4). Without written terms the transfer depends on the partner's goodwill, and NAVORA drifts into being the partner's supplier (`csa` mandate drift).
- **What is already settled on the sources `lca` read (D-12, 13:52, advisory):**
  1. A licence and an OKP admission are not transferred; the new provider needs its own licence, admission and ZSR.
  2. Clients move only by their own free choice among admitted providers (KVG Art. 41 Abs. 1, secondary source).
  3. Records stay with the partner (controller; retention). A copy to C needs the client's consent as a rule; NAVORA may not move data between organisations on its own.
  4. During employment the nurses owe loyalty to the partner; preparing C is plausibly allowed, approaching clients or colleagues to leave is not.
- **Timing (`csa` estimate, STRATEGY §4):** two procedures of about 8 weeks each after a complete file; the C file must be complete by about month 7 to be admitted within the sunset.
- **Related:** D-11 (canton and municipalities), D-14 (fee), D-18 (tariff contract), D-22 (orders), D-4 (retention), D-29 (qualification verification), D-35 (AI path, data minimisation), LEAVE1 (access ends when employment ends).
- **Safe default in force:** the D-12 decision stands; nothing is transferred; synthetic data only.

**Proposed terms (option B):**

| # | Term | Source | Who answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | The partner consents in writing to the team preparing its own provider (C) from month 0; no approach to clients or colleagues before C is admitted | `lca` D-12 note 1 | settled as a term (`lca`) |
| T2 | At the sunset NAVORA returns the partner's data as a usable export, or keeps them read-only for the partner's retention period, at the partner's choice | `lca` D-12 note 2 | settled as a term (`lca`); period with D-4 |
| T3 | No data move between organisations in NAVORA without the partner's instruction and the client's recorded consent | `lca` D-12 note 3 | settled as a term (`lca`) |
| T4 | One cut-over date per client; each visit belongs to exactly one provider | `lca` D-12 note 4 | settled as a term (`lca`) |
| T5 | Client information at intake states that the team may later form its own provider and that the client then chooses freely | STR-15 (1) | `lca`: may the information name a planned change of provider in advance? UNKNOWN |
| T6 | C applies for its own tariff contract (association route or direct); NAVORA's billing reference data are keyed per provider contract, not per region only | STR-15 (2), STR-6 | `lca` (route); `eng` (data model) |
| T7 | At least one pilot municipality where a new provider's residual-financing or service-mandate path is known | STR-15 (3), STR-1, D-11 | Zurich legal adviser; owner |
| T8 | NAVORA's fee is agreed per pilot team and moves unchanged to C; no exclusivity and no organisation-wide licence with the partner | STR-15 (4), STR-2, D-14 | owner (pricing) |
| T9 | Team members' employment: transfer as a business part (OR Art. 333) or new contracts; no contractual restriction on joining C beyond the statutory duty of loyalty | STR-15 (5); `lca` D-12 question (a) | Zurich legal adviser; `lca` first |
| T10 | What the partner receives for incubating a team that leaves | STR-15 (6) | owner (partnership) |
| T11 | Orders and needs assessments at cut-over: carried over or renewed before C's first visit | `lca` D-12 question (b); D-22 | Zurich legal adviser; `cra` on continuity |
