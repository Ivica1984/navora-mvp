# D-20 — Who admits members to a team

## Decision summary
Today only an organisation administrator invites members and adds them to a team (D-5, ADM1). The row proposes that **teams invite and admit colleagues themselves** by a team decision (as J2 decides on cases), while NAVORA verifies identity and qualification before the qualification is active (D-29). That removes a central hierarchy step from a self-organised model (value order 4, team autonomy; 5, less administration). PO working view: **team admission by team decision, qualification active only after D-29's confirmation, the administrator keeps removal for cause and an audit view**. The PO may decide it if `lca` finds no employment-law, contract or liability limit (who is the employer and who is accountable, D-12) and Engineering shows it is reversible. Inputs requested: Engineering and `lca`; `cra` only on one point (qualification mix in a team, question 3).

## Context and problem statement
- IMPLEMENTED (ADM1, 0035-0037, 0059): `invite_member` (role `admin`, `coach`, `operations` or `nurse`; team and qualification together), `add_team_member` and `remove_team_member` require `private.is_org_admin` (0035:302-333); removal refused while the person is primary nurse or deputy of an open case (J6); audit rows for each command.
- D-26 (package `inputs-requested`): the invitation e-mail needs the service-role key on a server path; a team-level invite reuses the same path.
- D-29 (`awaiting-approval: owner`): how a qualification is verified before it is active (option B: evidence recorded by someone the licence holder authorised). A team decision cannot replace that check.
- Challenge-review row: "Central administration admits members to teams | doubtful".
- R-11 (risk register, S1): professional accountability without line management unassigned.
- Safe default in force: admin-only admission (D-5).

**Legal check (step 4, po 2026-10-24):** yes. Who may hire or admit a person into care work is tied to the employer and the licence holder (D-12): an admission by colleagues must not create an employment relationship, a contract or a professional responsibility nobody signed for. So `lca` is asked.

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A (as built) | admin invites and admits | one accountable step | a hierarchy step per hire; the team waits for an admin |
| B | the team proposes, the admin confirms | team choice visible | still two steps, admin remains a gate |
| C | the team admits by team decision (J2 rule: a quorum of present members, objections recorded); NAVORA activates the qualification only after D-29's confirmation; the admin can remove for cause and sees every admission | team autonomy; no admin step for a normal hire; qualification safety kept | needs D-26 (invite path), D-29, and `lca` on the employer question |
| D | C, but the employment contract stays with the licence holder and the team's admission is a recommendation the licence holder executes | separates team choice from the legal act | if the licence holder always executes, it is B again |

## Legal/compliance impact

**1. Can a team decision admit a person without creating an employment relationship?**
- An employment contract binds the **employer** and the employee ([L6] Art. 319 Abs. 1). It needs no written form and can arise tacitly once work is done for pay (OR Art. 320 Abs. 2, as summarised by [L7]).
- **Consequence:** if a team admission lets a person start working for the organisation, a contract with the employer can arise without anyone at the employer having reviewed it (pay D-14, working time D-23). The team members are not the employer. Whether they can bind it depends on authority the employer gave them (OR Art. 32 ff., not read: UNKNOWN).
- So C does not avoid an employment relationship; it can create one nobody signed for. That is the risk the PO named. It goes away if three acts are kept apart: (i) **the contract** (employer, outside NAVORA or recorded in NAVORA as a fact by a person the employer authorised); (ii) **team membership** (may be a team decision); (iii) **qualification activation** (D-29). Membership should only take effect for a person whose contract fact is recorded.

**2. Does the licence holder's accountability allow the team to choose?** For ZH (orientation until D-11): one nursing lead, a single person with a ZH practice licence, and a deputy ([L3] 5.1); a *Standortleitung Pflege* per site supervises staff without a licence (5.2); tasks only within training, and assistants only by individual delegation (5.4); staffing in proportion to the operating concept, with a staffing plan by qualification and function (5.3, 7.4); the operator sets up suitable organisational arrangements (3.2); changes of the overall or nursing leadership and of a *Standortleitung* are reported in advance for approval (11). **Who selects staff is not regulated** in the text read. The payer contract sets staff qualifications in Anhang 4a, and "die Verantwortung für den Pflegeprozess liegt immer bei einem/r Absolventen/in der Tertiärstufe" ([L2] Art. 18 Abs. 1; Anhang 4a).
- **So:** nothing read forbids the team from choosing a colleague. What must stay with the licence holder: verifying qualifications (D-29), supervision and delegation, and keeping the staffing plan in proportion. Protective conditions for C: the qualification stays inactive until D-29; a team command can never add, remove or vacate a **lead role** (nursing lead, deputy, *Standortleitung*), because those changes need cantonal approval; the nursing lead or *Standortleitung* sees every admission and can remove for cause; nobody plans visits before the contract fact is recorded.

**3. Data protection.** "Der Arbeitgeber darf Daten über den Arbeitnehmer nur bearbeiten, soweit sie dessen Eignung für das Arbeitsverhältnis betreffen oder zur Durchführung des Arbeitsvertrages erforderlich sind" ([L5]). Whether this also covers candidates before hiring is UNKNOWN on the text read (it speaks of the *Arbeitnehmer*). Under DSG minimisation (as cited in D-9) the result is the same in either case. Team members who see candidate data act for the employer. Protective shape: the team sees only what the decision needs (name, the qualification claimed, availability; no references, health data, pay or private contact data beyond the invite); an objection is recorded as a reason code, never as free-text judgements about a person; a declined candidate's data is deleted after the decision (period: D-4).

**4. What depends on other decisions:** D-12 (who the employer and licence holder is, and whether its internal rules can give teams authority to hire); D-11 (canton: only ZH read); D-29 (qualification check); D-26 (invite e-mail, information duty); D-23, D-14 (terms a tacit contract would carry).

**Verdicts:**
- **A (as built):** compliant under every plausible interpretation on the sources read.
- **D, sharpened** (the team decides membership; the employer's only step is to record the contract fact, once, by an authorised person; membership takes effect only then; qualification only after D-29; lead roles never changeable by a team; candidate data minimal): **compliant under every plausible interpretation on the federal and ZH sources read**; other cantons UNKNOWN, but D removes no act of the licence holder.
- **C as written** (team admission is enough): **unresolved: needs owner** (organisational policy, D-12) **and legal adviser.** Question: "Can members of a home-care team, under a written internal rule of the employer, conclude employment contracts or admit people to work on the employer's behalf, and what must that authority contain so that the licence holder still meets its duties under the cantonal operating licence (qualified staff, supervision, staffing plan)?"

**Note to po:** D sharpened keeps the team's choice and reduces the employer's part to recording one fact; it is not B, because the employer does not judge the candidate. It also answers part of R-11: lead roles stay named and outside team commands (same pattern as D-25, Engineering question 3).

## Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| An unqualified person works with clients | qualification inactive until D-29 confirmation; planning refuses an inactive qualification |
| A team admission creates an unsigned employment relationship | D variant or a contract step by the licence holder, per `lca` |
| A team excludes a colleague unfairly | objections recorded; admin removal for cause and an audit view; coach called on dispute |

## Recommended default for NAVORA
PROVISIONAL: **A as built** until the inputs are in. Target **C** (or **D** if `lca` finds the employer must act), built as an ADM1 follow-up after D-26 and D-29.
