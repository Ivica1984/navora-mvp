# D-29 — How a member's qualification is verified before it is active

## Decision summary
Before a member's qualification (registered nurse, care assistant) counts for planning, signing and billing, what evidence is recorded (a GLN, an entry in the national register of health professions NAREG or of medical professions MedReg, a diploma, a cantonal practice licence), who checks it, how it expires and is re-checked, and what happens on expiry. **No recommendation yet.** Expected route: `owner` (regulatory liability of the licensed organisation, D-12), after `lca` names the legal requirements and `cra` names which qualification gates which care. The PO proposes no rule on which qualification may perform which service.

## Context and problem statement
**Blocks:** real clients (with D-3, D-9, D-1). **Finding:** CGR-16 (open, advisory now, blocking before real data): "A qualification is a label set by an administrator, with no evidence of registration and no expiry" (grep: no GLN, NAREG or MedReg fields).

**What NAVORA does today (IMPLEMENTED, read in the repository by the PO; the PO did not run the tests):**
- `team_memberships.qualification` is `registered_nurse` or `care_assistant`, exactly one per member, changed by command only by an admin with a mandatory reason, audited, readable by the team (F0 remainder, ADR-034, #38).
- The qualification gates: category B planning and performing (J3, F1-F4), signature rules (F6/F7, D-10 open), matching hard filter "verified qualification" in name only (0011 comment; no verification data exists).
- No GLN, register number, licence, diploma, expiry date or re-check exists in the schema (CGR-16 grep).
- The pilot runs on synthetic data only (this row's default).

**Safe default in force:** a qualification is a label; synthetic data only; no real client until this package is decided.

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Admin attests with evidence reference | admin records the evidence type and identifier (e.g. GLN, NAREG entry), the date checked and an expiry/re-check date; qualification is active only with it | simple; auditable | manual admin work for every member; relies on one person |
| B Self-declaration plus register check | the member enters her GLN; a second person (any registered nurse of the team or an admin) confirms the register entry; re-check on a schedule | team autonomy; less central admin | two-person rule cost; register lookup manual |
| C **Automated register check (PO challenge proposal)** | the member enters her GLN; NAVORA checks the public register automatically at activation and on a schedule; a mismatch or removal suspends the qualification and raises an exception | no administrator in the loop; continuous, not annual | UNKNOWN whether NAREG offers an interface and terms permit automated lookups; care assistants may not be in a public register |
| D Organisation's HR process outside NAVORA | NAVORA trusts an HR attestation | no build | evidence outside the audit trail; hierarchy remains |

**(1) Which professions are registered, and where**
- Settled [L3]: the register (operated by the SRK, Art. 2) "enthält Daten zu den Personen der Gesundheitsberufe nach Artikel 2 Absatz 1 GesBG" (Art. 1 Abs. 2). The SRK enters personal and education data (Art. 4 Abs. 1). The cantonal authorities enter licence data, i.e. the Berufsausübungsbewilligung and measures (Art. 5). Art. 7 refers to the diploma "dipl. Pflegefachfrau HF / Pflegefachmann HF", so holders of the HF nursing diploma are within the register.
- UNKNOWN: the exact list in GesBG Art. 2 Abs. 1 (not read). [L7] adds a lit. h (APN), which suggests the current list runs to lit. g. Also UNKNOWN: whether entry follows automatically from the diploma or needs the person's own registration, and whether foreign diplomas need recognition first. `MedReg` is for medical professions; it applies to NAVORA only for the doctors (D-22: GLN/ZSR of the ordering doctor).

**(2) Must the organisation verify, and how often**
- Settled (cantonal example quoting federal law [L8]): an OKP-admitted home-care organisation must have "das erforderliche Fachpersonal, das eine dem Tätigkeitsbereich entsprechende Ausbildung hat" (KVV Art. 51, as quoted). The canton checks this at licensing, using a staffing plan by training and an approved care management. The organisation therefore must know and be able to show each member's training.
- Settled [L4]: individual licensing (GesBG Art. 11) concerns work "in eigener fachlicher Verantwortung".
- UNKNOWN: (i) whether employed nurses working under the organisation's care management need an individual licence in the pilot canton (cantonal, D-11); (ii) any duty, method or frequency for the organisation to check register entries; the cantonal instructions that [L8] refers to were not read; (iii) whether the canton or the payers require evidence per person (copies of diplomas, GLN).

**(3) Care assistants**
- UNKNOWN. No source read puts FaGe EFZ, FaBe, Pflegehelfer/in SRK or AGS EBA into the GesBG register, and [L9] does not show the full NAREG annex. [L8] counts them by training in the staffing plan. Their likely evidence is the diploma or certificate itself, but that is a reading, not a rule.

**(4) Automated register lookups (option C)**

An interface to the public data is granted to data suppliers and to bodies with legal or public-interest tasks; "Das BAG entscheidet auf schriftlichen Antrag hin über den Zugang" (Art. 12 Abs. 1, 3).
- UNKNOWN: whether a private home-care organisation or NAVORA qualifies for interface access (Art. 12); whether the SRK's terms of use allow automated queries of the public web search; whether the data needed (status, licence measures) is among the data public "im Abrufverfahren". **Option C: unresolved: needs the owner to ask the BAG/SRK (written application, Art. 12 Abs. 3) and a legal adviser.** It also covers only registered professions, so care assistants would still need A or B (see 3).

**(5) Data protection for registration data**
- Settled [L1]: proportionality, purpose limitation and accuracy apply (Art. 6 Abs. 2-5). Accuracy points to a re-check: register data can change, since licence measures are entered by cantons [L3] Art. 5, and entries can be deleted (GesBG Art. 27, title only read in the lexaris table of contents).
- UNKNOWN: the employment-law limit on processing employee data (OR Art. 328b, not read).
- Protective reading: store the evidence type, the identifier (GLN or register number), the date checked, the person who checked it and the re-check date. Do not store copies of diplomas unless the canton requires them. Show the team only the qualification label (as today); register identifiers only to the people who verify.

**Answer to `cra`: can a qualification or licence lapse?**
- **The diploma:** no source read says a nursing diploma (HF/FH) expires. UNKNOWN in law; on the sources read, the diploma entry in the register is the stable part.
- **The practice licence (Berufsausübungsbewilligung):** yes, it can end or be limited. Federal law has articles on restriction and conditions (GesBG Art. 13), withdrawal (Art. 14), disciplinary measures including a practice ban (Art. 19, 21) and deletion of register entries (Art. 27) [L13]; I read only their titles. The cantonal example [L14] withdraws or restricts a licence whose conditions are no longer met, and ties it to periodic renewal from age 70 and to resumed practice within five years. A licence is cantonal and valid only in the canton of work, including the canton of a home visit [L12]. Withdrawal or refusal is entered in the register [L11], [L12].
- **Whether an employed nurse needs a licence at all:** only when she works in her own professional responsibility, i.e. not under the supervision of a nurse [L11]; employed managers with professional responsibility need one. Cantons decide case by case and some go further [L11], [L12]. That is adviser question 1 (02:52), now narrowed: in a self-organised team without a supervising nurse, a plausible reading is that the team's registered nurses work in their own professional responsibility; whether the organisation's care management counts as supervision is the question.
- **Settled [L11]:** the employer must ultimately ensure that its employed health professionals are qualified. The source does not say how or how often.
