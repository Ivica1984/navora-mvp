# D-1 — Live database: real people? key rotation, leaked-password protection

## Decision summary
Three things about the live Supabase project that the repository describes but does not manage: (1) a **fact only the owner knows**: do the 6 cases and 3 care notes (and the referral, matching and quality rows) concern real people? (2) **rotate** the anon/JWT key (SR-12); (3) **enable leaked-password protection**. The PO's first triage: (2) and (3) are protective and cheap, but they are changes to a live system, which only the owner may execute (COORDINATION rule 5; agents never touch the live database). (1) decides whether a data-protection assessment is needed and, through D-7, whether the repository may stay public. **Preliminary route: `owner`** (facts only the owner knows; live system). Inputs requested: Engineering, Legal & Compliance. No clinical input: no care rule is involved.

## Context and problem statement
- **IMPLEMENTED / VERIFIED (2026-10-21, `docs/ops/live/CONTAINMENT_REPORT_C1_2026-10-21.md`):** before containment, `{public}` policies with `true` let anyone holding the public anon key read, create and modify `cases`, `care_visits` and `teams` (SR-01). Containment C-1 was executed by the owner: anon access denied on 10 of 10 tables, verified. Row counts only (contents not read by any agent): cases 6, care_visits 3, teams 3, care_notes 3, orders 10 (SR-26), partner_requests 2, matching_requests 4, match_candidates 12, quality_events 3.
- **Open since then (backlog O-E):** whether the rows are real people; the anon key is not rotated (SR-12: it grants nothing on `public` now, but it stays a valid credential); leaked-password protection is off (Supabase security advisor, WARN). Edge logs reached back only 24 h, so earlier access cannot be ruled out (report, line 21).
- **Links:** D-7 (`lca` 11:55: staying public is compliant under every interpretation only once D-1 shows the live data are synthetic or deleted); SR-26 (the same live project holds a foreign table with e-mail addresses); D-4 and D-9 real-data gates; the rule "no real patient data in any environment until SR-01 (live) and SR-22 are closed" (SECURITY_RISKS.md).
- **Safe default in force:** containment C-1 stays; no build, dev server or agent against the live database (SR-05, SR-12); the repository's schema is the target (ADR-022), the live project is not migrated by this repository.
- **Care-model relevance:** trust. A care operating system that cannot say whether it once exposed client data cannot ask teams and clients for theirs.

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Owner confirms the rows are synthetic (test data the owner entered); rotate the key and enable leaked-password protection now; then the live project may be emptied or retired | closes SR-12 and the advisor warning; unlocks D-7 "stay public" | only the owner can confirm; nothing else changes |
| B | Owner says the rows are, or may be, real people: rotate and enable now; keep the rows untouched (evidence); a legal adviser assesses whether the past exposure is a breach to be reported (DSG Art. 24) and whether affected persons must be informed; the repository's live identifiers and reports are kept out of public view until SR-12 is closed | the protective path when the fact is not certain | needs a legal adviser; time pressure if a report is due |
| C | Only rotate and enable; leave the fact open | quick | D-7 and the possible notification stay open indefinitely |
| D | Status quo (contained, not rotated) | nothing to do | a valid credential to a database that may hold health data stays live |

## Engineering impact
*requested 2026-10-24 (po).* Asked: what rotating the anon/JWT key breaks (the repository no longer ships it to browsers since T8; does any deployed host or other app still use it?); the exact steps for the owner (Project Settings → API; Auth → leaked-password protection) and how to verify them read-only afterwards; whether `orders` (SR-26) or another app depends on the same key; whether the rows' creation timestamps (read without contents) help the owner answer the fact question.

## Clinical impact
not needed: no care, documentation, quality or safety rule is decided. If the rows are real, the care notes are health data; that is `lca`'s question.

## Legal/compliance impact
*requested 2026-10-24 (po).* Involved because it touches data protection (possible breach of data security of health data), the notification duty, controllership and the D-7 link. Asked: (1) if the rows may concern real people, what DSG Art. 5 lit. h and Art. 24 require, by whom (controller: UNKNOWN, D-12), and within what time; whether informing affected persons is required; (2) whether "rotate and enable now, keep the rows untouched until assessed" is compliant under every plausible interpretation; (3) whether deleting the rows before an assessment could conflict with any duty (evidence, retention, D-4).

## Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| The rows are real and a reporting duty runs while the question waits | ask the fact first, alone and in one line; B as the default path when the answer is "not sure" |
| The old key is used against another table or a future policy mistake | rotate now (all options except D) |
| Rows deleted before a legal assessment | no deletion until the owner confirms synthetic, or the adviser clears it |
| An agent touches the live project to "help" | rule 5 stays: the owner executes; `eng` writes steps and read-only checks only |

## Recommended default for NAVORA
Pending inputs. **PO working view, not a decision:** rotate the key and enable leaked-password protection **now, whatever the answer** (both protective, and the second only blocks known-leaked passwords); answer the fact question; **A** if synthetic, **B** if real or uncertain. Until the owner answers: containment C-1, no agent access, and treat the live data as possibly real (D-7 cannot stay public on that basis).
