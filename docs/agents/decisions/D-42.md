# D-42 — Phase B pilot agreement with a nursing home

## Decision summary
The founder (2026-11-06, points 15 and 16; FE-021, FE-026) wants `lca` to review the contract draft for the later real pilot. By current direction the real pilot partner is a nursing home (Phase B). This package prepares the **minimum content** of that pilot agreement before any talks. Nothing is signed or sent; no partner is contacted (owner only). ## Context and problem statement
- The earlier draft terms (D-38, T1-T18) were written for a home-care provider partner in Zurich under D-12 B (sunset to a team-owned provider). Whether that goal applies to a nursing home is founder question O-5.
- Phase A uses a simulated nursing home; no contract is needed for it.

## Clinical impact
in 2026-11-06 13:30 (`cra`, `CLINICAL_GOVERNANCE.md` section 13:30, PR #911; read on its branch by po 15:00). Advisory; contract wording is `lca`'s and the owner's. Clauses as taken in:
1. Clinical responsibility stays with the licence holder (the home and its responsible nursing lead); NAVORA is a tool, not the care provider; no NAVORA agent approves a clinical rule.
2. A named Clinical Owner on the NAVORA side (G-1, D-27) and a named nursing lead of the home, both before the first real resident; each clinical rule in use approved in writing by the Clinical Owner and accepted by the nursing lead, with version and date.
3. Waiving a clinical gate (S1 item, blocking CGR finding, rule in use): only the Clinical Owner with the home's nursing lead and the owner, in writing, with reason and compensating control; never the PO, Engineering or the owner alone (CGR-117).
4. No NAVORA-derived care-need level, tariff level or clinical score is used for billing or care decisions in the pilot; levels are entered by the home's staff; billing in shadow mode until the billing owner releases it (CGR-142 (3), LT-2).
5. Ward functions NAVORA does not monitor (CGR-142 (1)) listed in an annex, so the home keeps its own controls for them.
6. Incident duty both ways within a period the parties set (UNKNOWN); a stop right for the home's nursing lead on any NAVORA function, effective at once.
7. Change control: no change to a clinical rule, safety signal or billing gate reaches the pilot without the Clinical Owner's sign-off and notice to the home beforehand.
8. Exit: the home's records stay complete and readable without NAVORA (export of signed reports, versions and audit trail).
