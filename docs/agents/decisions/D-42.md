# D-42 — Phase B pilot agreement with a nursing home

## Decision summary
The founder (2026-11-06, points 15 and 16; FE-021, FE-026) wants `lca` to review the contract draft for the later real pilot. By current direction the real pilot partner is a nursing home (Phase B). This package prepares the **minimum content** of that pilot agreement before any talks. Nothing is signed or sent; no partner is contacted (owner only). Preliminary route: `owner` (partnership, contract, liability).

## Context and problem statement
- The earlier draft terms (D-38, T1-T18) were written for a home-care provider partner in Zurich under D-12 B (sunset to a team-owned provider). Whether that goal applies to a nursing home is founder question O-5.
- Phase A uses a simulated nursing home; no contract is needed for it.

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Reuse D-38 T1-T18 unchanged | prepared | written for home care and a sunset model |
| B | New minimum content for a nursing-home pilot: roles (licence holder stays responsible), processor agreement and data protection, scope (wards, functions, what is excluded: D-39, D-40 limits, D-35 rule A), clinical governance (Clinical Owner, gates G-1 to G-25), shadow-mode billing, exit and data return, fees (D-14 open) | fits Phase B | needs `lca`, `cra` work |

## Engineering impact
requested 2026-11-06 (`eng`): technical annexes (hosting region, backups, restore, incident duties, data export on exit).

## Clinical impact
requested 2026-11-06 (`cra`): clinical governance clauses (who approves clinical rules, waiving a clinical gate only by the Clinical Owner with the owner, CGR-117).

## Legal/compliance impact
requested 2026-11-06 (`lca`): minimum legal content under federal law and the law of Basel-Stadt and Basel-Landschaft (processor role, professional secrecy, liability, licence holder's duties, retention, exit); which questions need an external adviser (FE-026).

## Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Talks start before the content is clear | no partner contact before this package is escalated |
| Home-care terms copied into a nursing-home contract | option B written for nursing homes |

## Recommended default for NAVORA
PROVISIONAL: option B, drafted once the inputs are in. Default until then: no contract draft leaves the repository.
