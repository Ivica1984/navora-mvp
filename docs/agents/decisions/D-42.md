**Minimum legal content of a Phase B nursing-home pilot agreement (option B):**
1. **Roles.** The home is the licence holder and the **controller**; NAVORA is a **processor** acting only on the home's instructions ([S1] Art. 9 Abs. 1; [V1]); NAVORA is not a care provider and holds none of the home's licensed functions. The home's professional organisation and supervision stay with its management ([BS1] § 36 Abs. 1; [BL1] §§ 1, 4), so every clinical rule in use needs the home's adoption (`cra` clause 2; D-43 option D). *Settled on the texts read.*
2. **Processing agreement (Art. 9 DSG).** Purpose and data categories (health data, sensitive, [S1] Art. 5 lit. c Ziff. 2); data security measures (Art. 8) as a technical annex (Engineering items 1-4); sub-processors named with their country and the home's prior consent to changes (protective drafting; the Art. 9 paragraph on sub-processing not re-read); instruction right; return and deletion at the end with a confirmation ([V1]); audit right. *Settled as to the duty; wording for the adviser.*
3. **Professional secrecy.** NAVORA staff and sub-processors who can see resident data bound expressly to StGB Art. 321 and § 26 GesG BS (BL equivalent UNKNOWN) as the home's *Hilfspersonen*, with a confidentiality undertaking per person. [K1] covers *Hilfspersonen* but says nothing on IT providers: **UNKNOWN** whether an IT processor qualifies; the same question is open in D-41 (9).
4. **Cross-border and hosting.** The agreement names the hosting region and every transfer (D-2 [H3], [H4]); the choice is D-2 (`awaiting-approval: owner`). *OPEN until D-2.*
5. **Breach duty.** NAVORA reports any breach to the home, not to the FDPIC ([S1] Art. 24 Abs. 3; [S2] Ziff. 1.2); the home decides on the FDPIC report, which is due "so rasch als möglich" with no fixed deadline (Art. 24 Abs. 1), so the processor's notice must be faster than that. The contract should fix a concrete period and contact point (a product choice, not a legal minimum); the incident runbook (Engineering item 3) is a precondition. If the home is a public-law body, the cantonal IDG reporting rules apply instead ([K1] Ziff. 8.2): **UNKNOWN** until the partner is known.
6. **Licence-holder duties not moved.** Staffing and qualification mix stay the home's ([BS1] § 37 Abs. 2; [BL1] §§ 4-5); presence of the PDL or deputy ([BS1] § 22). If the pilot changes how a ward is organised (self-organised team), the home may have to report it: BL "unverzüglich und unaufgefordert" for licence-relevant facts ([BL1] § 8); BS change of PDL/deputy two months ahead ([BS2]); whether a ward reorganisation itself is reportable in BS or BL is **UNKNOWN**. The contract makes this the home's duty and lets NAVORA stop the pilot until it is met.
7. **Retention and exit.** The home keeps its records for its legal retention period; NAVORA returns a complete, readable export (signed reports, versions, audit trail; `cra` clause 8, Engineering item 4) and then deletes. The BS/BL retention period for nursing-home records is **UNKNOWN** (GesG BS/BL not read; D-4 re-check outstanding).
8. **Billing.** Shadow mode only; no NAVORA-derived level used for billing (`cra` clause 4; D-18, D-22: a NAVORA-computed level not compliant). *Consistent with my D-18/D-22 verdicts.*
9. **AI and medical-device scope.** D-35 rule A unless D-41 is decided otherwise; an intended-purpose clause matching the D-34 statement, so the contract does not promise a function the D-34 assessment excludes.
10. **Liability and insurance.** The home keeps its licence-required liability cover ([BS2]); NAVORA's liability, cap and insurance for software faults are **UNKNOWN** (OR not read) and commercial (owner).
11. **Stop right and change control** (`cra` clauses 3, 6, 7): no legal obstacle found; recommended as drafted.
12. **Fees:** D-14 open; no fee as a share of care revenue (D-44 L-10).

**Verdicts:**
- **A (reuse D-38 T1-T18 unchanged): not suitable** on the sources: written for a Zurich home-care provider under the D-12 sunset model; the BS/BL nursing-home duties above (§ 36 BS, APV §§ 1, 4, 5, 8 BL) and home financing are not in it. Not a compliance verdict on the terms themselves.
- **B, the content list above as a drafting basis:** **compliant under every plausible interpretation** as a list (it adds duties, removes none).
- **B, a contract to be signed: unresolved: needs legal adviser** before Phase B, on three named questions (FE-026): (Q-42a) "Can the employees and sub-processors of a software processor be bound as *Hilfspersonen* of a BS/BL nursing home under StGB Art. 321 and § 26 GesG BS (and the BL equivalent), and what must the agreement say for that?" (Q-42c) "How can the software provider's liability towards the home and its residents be allocated and capped, given that the home keeps the professional organisation and supervision of care?" If the partner is public-law, add IDG BS/BL.
