# D-30 — Correcting a visit report when its author has left the team

## Decision summary
Who may correct a final visit report, and sign that correction, once the nurse who performed the visit and signed the report is no longer a member of the visit's team; and what the corrected record then shows about authorship. **No recommendation yet.** Today the correction is refused (strict default), so the final version stays in force and a wrong record cannot be corrected at all. Expected route: `clinical` (the named Clinical Owner, G-1) for who may sign; `lca` for the documentation-duty and authorship question.

## Context and problem statement
**Blocks:** the PROVISIONAL label of F6's correction path (CR-048). **Findings:** CGR-19 (closed with the interim refusal, provisional); PS-15 (S2, mitigated-in-code, interim); H-12; related CGR-18 (removal while reports are unsigned, CR-036).

**What NAVORA does today (IMPLEMENTED, read in the repository by the PO):**
- CR-012: the performer (first `started` event) alone drafts and signs a report, including a correction version (0016; 0041 `sign_report` requires the performer).
- CR-043: a signed or final report locks the visit's times and observations; they reopen only through a correction draft (`correct_report`, any team member, with a reason).
- CR-048 (interim, D-30): `correct_report` is refused with `performer_not_in_team` while the report's performer is not a member of the report's team; nothing is written; the final version stays in force (0044 §1, pgTAP 044:143).
- CR-036: removal from a team is refused while she is performer of a completed team visit without a signed or final report (so unsigned reports cannot be orphaned by removal).
- Residual (PS-15): a correction draft opened **before** she left stays open and can never be signed; it raises `report_correction_open` (CR-053, P3, not closable by hand).
- CR-045: billing and working-time facts follow the final report version; a correction is a new version and a new fact.

**Safe default in force:** no correction of a departed author's report; the final version stays the record; a stranded draft is visible as an exception.

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Keep the refusal (today) | no correction after the author left | no authorship question | a known error stays in the record; the stranded draft stays forever (PS-15) |
| B A registered nurse of the team corrects and signs as **corrector** | the correction is signed by a team RN who states she did not perform the visit; the record shows original author and corrector separately; the original version stays readable | the team owns its record (autonomy); errors can be fixed | the corrector attests to something she did not see; what she may change must be limited (UNKNOWN: `clinical`) |
| C B, limited to non-clinical fields | only times, category split or payer data may be corrected by another RN; observations and care given only by the author | protects clinical authorship | the author is gone, so clinical errors stay; needs a field classification (`clinical`) |
| D Addendum instead of correction | the final version is never changed; a dated addendum by a team RN is attached and shown with it | strongest integrity; the original stays untouched | billing facts follow the final version, so a billing error cannot be fixed by an addendum |
| E Contact the former author | she signs the correction with a time-limited access | the author signs | depends on her availability and on access for a non-member (D-3, privacy) |
