# D-2 — Staging hosting provider and region

## Decision summary
Where does NAVORA's protected staging environment run (T12): a Supabase project in an EU or Swiss region, plus a protected host for the Next.js app, with migrations deployed from CI and synthetic data only? The PO's first triage: the **design** (region, synthetic data only, protected access, deploy from CI) is a reversible product and technical choice the PO may decide; a **paid contract** is the owner's (financial); creating the projects and the deploy secret is an owner action (agents have no account access). **Preliminary route: po for the design, `owner` for the contract and execution.** Inputs requested: Engineering, Legal & Compliance. No clinical input needed (synthetic data only).

## Context and problem statement
- **BLOCKED today (BACKLOG T12):** "Supabase project (EU/CH region) + protected Next host, migrations from CI, synthetic seed + staging-only users", waiting for this decision; ADR-011 (separate staging from production hosting) PROPOSED.
- **What it blocks (accepted work that waits for a hosted run):** PLAN2's hosted daily job and COVER1's `navora-cover1-events` pg_cron job are unverified until staging exists (both accepted with that deviation); SIG1's `private.evaluate_signals` runs from pg_cron every minute, which CI cannot show on a hosted project; SIG2 needs the VAPID key pair as a staging/production secret set by the owner; F0's live reproducibility (M5); every e2e on a real host; the pilot itself.
- **Care-model relevance:** the safety signals (SIG1/SIG2) and planning jobs only protect clients when they run on a hosted clock. A staging environment is where that is shown before any real client.
- **Safe default in force:** no hosted environment; CI is the only runner (ADR-012); no real data anywhere.

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Supabase project in an EU region (or Swiss if offered) + Vercel (EU function region), deploy from CI, password- or SSO-protected | matches the stack; fastest | US-headquartered providers; plan cost and region availability to confirm (`eng`) |
| B | Supabase (as A) + Render Frankfurt for the app | same database; one more vendor with an EU region | plan cost; Render's protection features to confirm |
| C | Swiss-hosted Postgres and app (self-managed Supabase or plain Postgres) | data residency in CH from day one | much more operations work; pg_cron and Auth to rebuild; slow |
| D | No staging until the pilot partner is chosen (D-11, D-12) | no cost | hosted jobs and push stay unproven; safety signals untested on a clock |

**What staging processes (on the design in the PO working view)**
- Client data: synthetic only. Data that relate to no identifiable person are not personal data (DSG Art. 5 lit. a, read for D-1), so the DSG does not apply to them. The condition is that the seed is invented, not derived from or resembling real clients (the seed header and test names indicate invented data; IMPLEMENTED as far as read).
- Staff accounts: in the repository seed, `*.test@navora.test` addresses (reserved, undeliverable). If testers use **real personal e-mail addresses** or real names, those are personal data of the testers (not sensitive). If SIG2 push is tested on testers' real devices, the push subscription is personal data too and the D-32/D-33 transfer questions apply to staging.
- The owner's own customer account with each provider: the provider processes it as its own controller under its terms; not a NAVORA processing question.

**What is settled**
- An EU/EEA region (Frankfurt, Paris, Stockholm, Ireland) or Zurich is either in Switzerland or in a state on the binding adequacy list [H1], so storing data there is not a disclosure to a state without adequate protection (DSG Art. 16 Abs. 1 [P1]).
- Supabase processes outside the region for support, account, usage and delivery, under SCCs to Singapore and the US [H3], with a Swiss addendum [H4]. Singapore is not on the list [H1]; the US is only for DPF-certified organisations, and Supabase's DPA does not rely on the DPF [H4]. So for **real personal data** in the database, support access or incident handling from Singapore or the US would be a disclosure abroad resting on Art. 16 Abs. 2 (SCCs), which needs the exporter's own transfer assessment [H7], not only the vendor's statement.
- Vercel by default runs functions in the US and may transfer data worldwide [H5]; an EU function region narrows where code runs but does not by itself keep request data and logs in the EU on the sources read.
- A processor may process only as the controller itself may, the controller must ensure data security, and sub-processors need its approval (DSG Art. 9 [P1]); Supabase's general authorisation with notice and objection [H4] is the usual way to give that approval.

**What is UNKNOWN**
- Whether Vercel's DPF listing includes the Swiss-U.S. DPF (owner or `eng`, from the DPF list; [H5] is the vendor's claim).
- Which data Vercel and Render process in the US for an EU-region app (request logs, build logs, analytics) and under which safeguard (Vercel DPA not read).
- For production with health data: whether professional secrecy (StGB Art. 321) and the pilot canton's law permit a provider whose contracting entity is in Singapore with US access paths, under SCCs; whether a Swiss region is required by the canton or the payers; whether the remote-access and foreign-law risk named in [H7] is acceptable for health data (D-11, D-12, a legal adviser).

**Legal risks of each option for staging**
- **A (Supabase EU/CH + Vercel EU):** with synthetic data and test-domain accounts, no personal data of third parties are processed, so no transfer, processor or DPIA duty arises from staging. With real tester e-mail addresses: low-risk personal data; the providers' DPAs and SCCs/DPF are the safeguard; testers should be told (Art. 19). Vercel's US default must be overridden.
- **B (Supabase + Render Frankfurt):** same as A; Render states Swiss-U.S. DPF certification [H6] (list not checked).
- **C (Swiss self-hosted):** the strongest residency, but staging data are synthetic, so it buys no compliance benefit now; its benefit is for production.
- **D (no staging):** no legal risk from staging itself; the risk moves elsewhere: the hosted safety jobs (SIG1, PLAN2, COVER1) would first run on a clock with real clients. That is a patient-safety and accountability concern rather than a statutory one, and it supports the PO's rejection of D.
- **Lock-in:** choosing a staging provider creates no legal obligation for production. A paid plan is a contract the owner signs and can end (Supabase DPA: deletion after 30 days [H4]). The practical lock-in (schema, pg_cron, Auth on Supabase) is technical. Production needs its own `lca` check (ADR-011), and the facts above (Singapore contracting entity; US and Singapore access paths under SCCs) are exactly what that check must answer before health data.

**Verdict on the PO working view** (one staging Supabase project in a specific EU or Zurich region, a protected app host in an EU region, deploy from CI, synthetic client data only, staging-only staff accounts): **compliant under every plausible interpretation** of the sources read, **provided** (i) the region is chosen as a *specific* region (Zurich or an EU state on the list), not the general "Europe" region (which may deploy to London; London is also listed, so this is a residency choice, not a breach); (ii) the seed stays invented and no real client data, copies or exports from the live project are loaded (D-1, SR-26: nothing from `jwmfxrmougneeceldhsa` goes to staging); (iii) staff accounts use addresses on a test or NAVORA-controlled domain without real names, **or**, where a tester uses a personal address, the tester is told what is stored and where; (iv) the app host's function region is set to the EU (Vercel's default is the US [H5]); (v) SIG2 push on staging uses only devices whose push service is covered by D-32's decision (Google, Microsoft) until D-33 is answered. Under (i)-(v) the only personal data are those of testers who have been told, held by processors with DPAs.
**Production with real data: unresolved: needs legal adviser and owner** (D-11, D-12), not decided by this package.
