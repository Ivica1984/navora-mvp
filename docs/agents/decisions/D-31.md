# D-31 — Documentation, follow-up and billing of a broken-off visit

## Decision summary
How NAVORA treats a visit that was started and then broken off before completion (client taken to hospital during the visit, client declined to continue, nurse called away or unwell, started by mistake): which reasons are allowed, whether a report of the care already given is required, whether the minutes already given may be billed, who may record the end, what follow-up must be decided after it (CGR-25), how an end recorded in error is corrected (CGR-26, PS-22), and the residual questions (c)-(e) of the 2026-10-23 13:20 row. **No recommendation yet.** The documentation duty and the follow-up rule are clinical rules (Clinical Owner, D-27); billing of partial minutes is a tariff and legal question (with D-18). The PO proposes none of these rules. The PO can decide the non-clinical workflow parts (who sees what, how a correction is recorded technically) once `cra` confirms they carry no clinical rule. Expected route: split, `clinical` for (1) documentation and (2) follow-up, `legal` for (3) billing, `po` for (4) the correction workflow if `cra` and `lca` raise nothing.

## Context and problem statement
**Blocks:** the PROVISIONAL label of OPS1's interim rule (CGR-20) and of CSR1's follow-up default (CGR-25); any billing of an interrupted visit. **Findings:** CGR-35 (open, advisory S3: whether missed care must be made up and whether a move counts); CGR-20, CGR-25, CGR-26 (closed with interim rules); PS-22 (mitigated in code).

**What NAVORA does today (IMPLEMENTED, read in the repository by the PO; the PO did not run the tests):**
- Terminal status `interrupted` only through `end_visit_not_completed` with a reason from `client_transferred`, `client_declined`, `nurse_unavailable`, `started_by_mistake`, `other` (with a note); actor and timeline event; audited, idempotent, row-locked (0042:50, 99-135). A direct update to `interrupted` is refused (pgTAP 042:112-113).
- Who may end it: the performer, any member of the visit's team whatever the qualification, or a coordination role (0042; pgTAP 042:130).
- No report draft, no report obligation, no billable fact, no working-time fact, no `report_not_final`, no assessment marker (pgTAP 042).
- After the end, `visit_interrupted` (P2, not escalating) stays for the team and Operations until one follow-up decision is recorded: *Folgeeinsatz geplant*, *nicht mehr nötig*, or *other* with a note; not closable by hand; one decision per visit; for `client_transferred` the detail names the case's next planned visit (0045:49, 93-140, 161-197; pgTAP 045).
- The follow-up decision cannot be corrected; nothing checks that a follow-up visit exists; a care assistant may record *nicht mehr nötig* (CGR-25 residual).
- `/field` shows the performer who ended her visit ("Beendet von …", actor kind me/team/coordination/former, no user id) (0047 §3; `visit-lifecycle.tsx:81`; pgTAP 047). An end recorded in error cannot be undone (PS-22).
- AN2 (active) measures 2 and 3 will count follow-up decisions without a planned visit within 48 h and coordinator ends while the visit was in progress.

**Safe default in force:** the interim rules above (not billable, no report obligation, follow-up decision required, not reversible). They stay until this package is decided.

## Options considered
Sub-questions: **(1)** documentation of care already given; **(2)** follow-up; **(3)** billing; **(4)** who may end and how an erroneous end is corrected.

| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1a No report (today) | the reason code and timeline are the record | least administration | care given (e.g. a medication administered before the transfer) is not documented as a report |
| 1b Short report of care given, required when any care was given | performer documents what was done before the break | complete record; basis for any billing | one more step for the nurse; needs a rule for `started_by_mistake` (nothing given) |
| 1c Short report optional | the performer may document | flexible | the record depends on the person |
| 2a One follow-up decision by any team member (today) | as built in 0045 | team autonomy, no coordinator | unchecked statement; care assistant decides *nicht mehr nötig* |
| 2b As 2a, but *Folgeeinsatz geplant* must name a planned visit of the same case, and *nicht mehr nötig* needs a registered nurse | server checks the visit; qualification rule | removes the unchecked statement (CGR-25 residual) | the qualification part is a clinical rule |
| 2c Follow-up decided by a coordination role | central | one accountable person | central disposition; contradicts team autonomy |
| 3a Not billable (today) | interrupted visits never produce a billable fact | cannot over-bill | may under-bill care actually given |
| 3b Minutes given until the end are billable when 1b documents care given | billable fact from start to the end, with the reason | pays care given | tariff rules (KLV Art. 7a units and minimum, D-18) apply; legal check needed |
| 4a Performer, team or coordination ends; irreversible (today) | as built | simple | PS-22: a coordinator's erroneous end cannot be undone |
| 4b As 4a, plus a correction within the same Zurich day by the performer or the person who ended it: `interrupted` back to the previous status with a reason, audited, original end kept in history | reversible mistake | adds a state transition; must not reopen billing facts |
| 4c Only the performer may end a visit she started; others may end it only after the stuck threshold (D-28) | performer controls her visit | unwell nurse may be unable to end it |
