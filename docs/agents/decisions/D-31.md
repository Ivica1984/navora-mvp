# D-31 — Documentation, follow-up and billing of a broken-off visit

## Decision summary
How NAVORA treats a visit that was started and then broken off before completion (client taken to hospital during the visit, client declined to continue, nurse called away or unwell, started by mistake): which reasons are allowed, whether a report of the care already given is required, whether the minutes already given may be billed, who may record the end, what follow-up must be decided after it (CGR-25), how an end recorded in error is corrected (CGR-26, PS-22), and the residual questions (c)-(e) of the 2026-10-23 13:20 row. **No recommendation yet.** The documentation duty and the follow-up rule are clinical rules (Clinical Owner, D-27); billing of partial minutes is a tariff and legal question (with D-18). The PO proposes none of these rules. The PO can decide the non-clinical workflow parts (who sees what, how a correction is recorded technically) once `cra` confirms they carry no clinical rule. Expected route: split, `clinical` for (1) documentation and (2) follow-up, `legal` for (3) billing, `po` for (4) the correction workflow if `cra` and `lca` raise nothing.

## Context and problem statement
**Blocks:** the PROVISIONAL label of OPS1's interim rule (CGR-20) and of CSR1's follow-up default (CGR-25); any billing of an interrupted visit. **Findings:** CGR-35 (open, advisory S3: whether missed care must be made up and whether a move counts); CGR-20, CGR-25, CGR-26 (closed with interim rules); PS-22 (mitigated in code).

**What NAVORA does today (IMPLEMENTED, read in the repository by the PO; the PO did not run the tests):**
- Terminal status `interrupted` only through `end_visit_not_completed` with a reason from `client_transferred`, `client_declined`, `nurse_unavailable`, `started_by_mistake`, `other` (with a note); actor and timeline event; audited, idempotent, row-locked (0042:50, 99-135). A direct update to `interrupted` is refused (pgTAP 042:112-113).
- Who may end it: the performer, any member of the visit's team whatever the qualification, or a coordination role (0042; pgTAP 042:130).
- No report draft, no report obligation, no billable fact, no working-time fact, no `report_not_final`, no assessment marker (pgTAP 042).
- After the end, `visit_interrupted` (P2, not escalating) stays for the team and Operations until one follow-up decision is recorded: *Folgeeinsatz geplant*, *nicht mehr nötig*, or *other* with a note; not closable by hand; one decision per visit; for `client_transferred` the detail names the case's next planned visit (0045:49, 93-140, 161-197; pgTAP 045).
- The follow-up decision cannot be corrected; nothing checks that a follow-up visit exists; a care assistant may record *nicht mehr nötig* (CGR-25 residual).
- `/field` shows the performer who ended her visit ("Beendet von …", actor kind me/team/coordination/former, no user id) (0047 §3; `visit-lifecycle.tsx:81`; pgTAP 047). An end recorded in error cannot be undone (PS-22).
- AN2 (active) measures 2 and 3 will count follow-up decisions without a planned visit within 48 h and coordinator ends while the visit was in progress.

**Safe default in force:** the interim rules above (not billable, no report obligation, follow-up decision required, not reversible). They stay until this package is decided.

## Options considered
Sub-questions: **(1)** documentation of care already given; **(2)** follow-up; **(3)** billing; **(4)** who may end and how an erroneous end is corrected.

| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1a No report (today) | the reason code and timeline are the record | least administration | care given (e.g. a medication administered before the transfer) is not documented as a report |
| 1b Short report of care given, required when any care was given | performer documents what was done before the break | complete record; basis for any billing | one more step for the nurse; needs a rule for `started_by_mistake` (nothing given) |
| 1c Short report optional | the performer may document | flexible | the record depends on the person |
| 2a One follow-up decision by any team member (today) | as built in 0045 | team autonomy, no coordinator | unchecked statement; care assistant decides *nicht mehr nötig* |
| 2b As 2a, but *Folgeeinsatz geplant* must name a planned visit of the same case, and *nicht mehr nötig* needs a registered nurse | server checks the visit; qualification rule | removes the unchecked statement (CGR-25 residual) | the qualification part is a clinical rule |
| 2c Follow-up decided by a coordination role | central | one accountable person | central disposition; contradicts team autonomy |
| 3a Not billable (today) | interrupted visits never produce a billable fact | cannot over-bill | may under-bill care actually given |

| 4a Performer, team or coordination ends; irreversible (today) | as built | simple | PS-22: a coordinator's erroneous end cannot be undone |

**(3) Billing of minutes given before the break-off**
- Settled (federal, [S1]): OKP pays the services of KLV Art. 7 Abs. 2 by time, in 5-minute units, at least 10 minutes (Art. 7a Abs. 2).
- Settled (contracts read, [S3] Art. 8, 9.1; [S4] Ziff. 7.2, 8.1.5): billing follows the services actually given, stated as minutes per category and day. Neither contract read says a visit must be completed to be billed, and neither mentions interrupted visits.
- Reading, not a determination: nothing read excludes billing care that was actually given before a break-off. Nothing read allows billing time in which no KLV Art. 7 Abs. 2 service was given either (e.g. `started_by_mistake`, or waiting for an ambulance unless that is coordination under lit. a).
- UNKNOWN: (i) whether a payer requires a record of the care given (a report) as evidence for those minutes (documentation clauses of [S3]/[S4] not read for this; KVG Art. 42 not read); (ii) how the 5-minute units and the 10-minute minimum apply to a short interrupted visit (same open question as D-18 question 1); (iii) which category the minutes fall into when the visit was planned as several categories.
- **3a (not billable, today): compliant under every plausible interpretation.** It cannot over-bill. No source read creates a duty to bill. The only effect is that care actually given goes unpaid, and the client's cost share is lower to match.
- **3b: unresolved: needs legal adviser** (questions 1-2 below) and the contract the organisation signs (D-18, D-12). If 3b is ever chosen, the protective pairing is 3b only together with 1b (minutes billed only when a report of the care given exists) and never for `started_by_mistake`.

**(1) Documentation of care already given**
- Settled at federal level only in part: KLV Art. 8 and 8a [S1] require a documented needs assessment and controls; they set no duty to report on each visit. The GesBG Art. 16 duties as listed in [L4] include "Sie üben ihren Beruf sorgfältig und gewissenhaft aus" and "Sie wahren die Rechte der Patientinnen und Patienten"; the list read names no documentation duty. [L4] says these duties apply to work "in eigener fachlicher Verantwortung".
- Settled for one canton (example, [L4]): SO GesG § 18 says "Es ist über jede Patientin eine laufend nachzuführende Patientendokumentation anzulegen". Entries show their author and date. The duty applies to all health professionals, not only licence holders. Records are kept 20 years after the last treatment.
- UNKNOWN: the pilot canton's rule (D-11); whether NAVORA's reason code and timeline count as patient documentation for care actually given (e.g. a medication given before a transfer). Per CGR-26 evidence, `record_observation` does not accept `interrupted` (0040:144), so NAVORA has no place today to document that care.
- **1a (no report, today): unresolved: needs legal adviser and D-11.** A cantonal duty like SO § 18 plausibly covers care that was actually given, whether or not the visit was completed. It is covered while the data is synthetic. With real clients it holds only if the organisation documents that care somewhere else. **1c** has the same exposure, because the record then depends on the person.
- **1b (short report required when any care was given; none for `started_by_mistake`): compliant under every plausible interpretation on the sources read** as far as a documentation duty goes. Documenting care actually given cannot breach a documentation duty, and it is the patient record, so DSG proportionality is met. The report's content and form are a clinical rule (Clinical Owner, D-27), not set here.

**(2) Follow-up (legal remarks only; the rule is clinical)**
- No source read sets a follow-up rule. Two GesBG Art. 16 duties as listed in [L4] are relevant to who decides *nicht mehr nötig*: "sorgfältig und gewissenhaft", and "Sie halten sich an die Grenzen der Kompetenzen, die sie im Rahmen der Aus-, Weiter- und Fortbildung erworben haben". Whether a care assistant may decide that a nursing follow-up is unnecessary is a clinical and competence question, so its legal side is UNKNOWN.
- DSG Art. 6 Abs. 5 [L1]: whoever processes personal data "muss sich über deren Richtigkeit vergewissern". *Folgeeinsatz geplant* without a planned visit is an unchecked and possibly wrong record. 2b's server check is the more protective option on this point.

**(4) Who may end, and correcting an erroneous end**
- Settled [L1]: DSG Art. 6 Abs. 5 requires accuracy and "angemessene Massnahmen" to correct or delete inaccurate data. Settled for SO [L4]: entries show their author and date.
- **4a (irreversible, today): unresolved: needs legal adviser.** A status known to be false (care was given, the visit was not broken off) that cannot be corrected conflicts with the accuracy duty. The audit row shows the error but does not correct the record.
- **4b (audited correction; original end, actor, time and reason kept; no deletion): compliant under every plausible interpretation on the sources read**, provided that it never deletes or overwrites the original entry, that the correction is attributed and dated, and that it reopens no billing fact. The same-day window is a product choice and not a legal requirement. After that window, a correction could still be possible as an attributed addendum. UNKNOWN: whether the pilot canton prescribes a form for corrections to patient records (D-11).
- 4c: no legal question identified; clinical and workflow choice.

(3) Billing (3b) waits for D-18 and D-12 in any case.
