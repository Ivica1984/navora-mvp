# D-39 — Sensors in a client's home (IoT inbound) and sensor-based risk predictions

**Owner freeze (directive 2026-11-03 14:00, post-PR #822): specification only.** No implementation, no database table, no migration, no production architecture until the owner approves this package. Allowed: requirements analysis, specification, decision support. Source: owner issue #817 (draft specification of `iot_sensor_streams` and `ki_prediction_audit_logs`).

## Decision summary
May NAVORA receive measurements from devices in a client's home and record AI risk predictions derived from them? If so, on which legal basis, with whose consent, under which device-law route, and with which retention? **Recommendation: not before the owner approves it (frozen).** The package prepares the decision. The specification in #817 stays specification. Approval: `owner` (regulatory liability, a new data category, a possible medical-device function).

## Context and problem statement
- **Why the question exists:** owner direction #817 (2026-11-03): the data model should be ready in phase 1 for automated KVG billing in phase 2. Owner directive 14:00: frozen, specification only.
- **What NAVORA does today (IMPLEMENTED):**
  - no sensor data and no prediction;
  - the AIG1 suggestion ledger is append-only (0097) and is the place where any prediction would be written (AI-4, *AI-First Architecture Update*);
  - billable facts come only from a signed report without an open hold (J5).
- **Safe default in force:**
  - no sensor ingestion, no prediction, no table;
  - D-35 A (no data to an AI service in the pilot);
  - D-1 (no real data);
  - D-34 A plus C (interpreting clinical content only in the qualified module).
- **Blocks:** AI domain 4 *Check-In Services* with sensors; AI capability roadmap stage 3 sensor-based risk signals.

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | No sensors in the pilot; specification kept | no new data category, no device question | domain 4 sensor part waits |
| B | Sensors only for logistics facts (e.g. "door opened", device present), no health measurement, no prediction | possibly level L; may reduce no-show uncertainty | still data in a private home; household consent; UNKNOWN value |
| C | Health measurements recorded and shown to the team as facts, no interpretation | continuity information between visits | health data in a home; device-law question if values are shown for care decisions |
| D | Health measurements plus AI risk predictions (as #817) | earliest risk signal | level H: clinical module, conformity route, Clinical Owner, D-35 provider; highest legal exposure |

## Engineering impact
*requested 2026-11-03 (po → eng)*, **specification impact only** (owner freeze):
- what each option would need (ingestion endpoint, device credential, AIG1 ledger extension instead of a parallel log);
- what it would cost, and how it could be reversed.
- No code, no table, no migration.

## Clinical impact
*requested 2026-11-03 (po → cra).*
- Hazards of each option: false reassurance, alarm fatigue, an unwatched value read as "watched", stale data.
- Whether option C or D is clinical decision support (record it as a regulatory finding if so).
- What only the Clinical Owner can approve: measurement types, bands, who acts.

## Legal/compliance impact
*requested 2026-11-03 (po → lca).*
- **DSG:** legal basis and information duties for sensors in a private home, including the consent of other persons living there; professional secrecy.
- **Medical-device qualification (MepV)** per option, and the EU AI Act where relevant (with D-34).
- **Retention** (with D-4).
- **KVG/KLV question from #817:** may any billable service rest on sensor data or a prediction without a nurse's signed service record?

## Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| A data model built "for phase 2" shapes the product before the decision | owner freeze: no table, no migration (directive 2026-11-03) |
| Sensor values read as clinical monitoring that NAVORA does not provide | option B or C wording; `cra` finding route |

## Recommended default for NAVORA
**PROVISIONAL: option A** (no sensors in the pilot; specification kept) until the inputs are in and the owner approves.
