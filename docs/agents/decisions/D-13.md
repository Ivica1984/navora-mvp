# D-13 — Assessment instrument for home care

## Decision summary
Which needs-assessment instrument does NAVORA's home-care product work with, and in what way: does NAVORA integrate a licensed instrument (the row names interRAI HC for home care, interRAI LTCF only for a nursing-home offer, D-24), or does it stay **instrument-neutral**, recording that an assessment was done, with which instrument, by whom and with which resulting minutes per KLV category, while the assessment itself is done in the provider's licensed tool? **PO working view, not a decision:** instrument-neutral now (option B); an integration (A) only once the pilot provider (D-12) and its licensed tool are known; never NAVORA's own instrument (C). Licence cost and any partnership are `owner`. Inputs requested: Engineering, Clinical & Regulatory, Legal & Compliance.

## Context and problem statement
- **IMPLEMENTED today (J3, migration 20261021000014):** a needs assessment is a visit flag (`care_visits.is_needs_assessment`); completing it sets `cases.needs_assessment_done_at` once (`coalesce`); planned minutes per category A/B/C are entered on the care plan; the KLV category mapping is D-15 (`clinical`). NAVORA stores **no instrument, no items, no scores** and computes nothing clinical from an assessment. Read 2026-10-24, not tested.
- **Why it matters:** the needs assessment is the billing basis (KLV Art. 8a as read by `lca`, advisory) and the start of continuity (the care plan). Re-typing an assessment from another tool into NAVORA is duplicate documentation (value 5); missing it blocks billing.
- **Constraints already in force:** no invention of assessment, PAG, MQI or LTCF logic (CLAUDE.md, `DOMAIN_RULES.md`); D-34 safe default: no feature that interprets a client's clinical information to suggest an action; interRAI content is licensed and is not reproduced (`NURSING_HOME_TRANSFORMATION.md`, "licence" items).
- **What it blocks:** nothing built; it shapes a future assessment brief, ORD0 question 6 (sending the assessment result to the GP, D-22) and the nursing-home track (D-24 NH2-NH5).
- **Safe default in force:** as built (no instrument in NAVORA).

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Integrate a licensed home-care instrument (e.g. interRAI HC as the row names): import or link the assessment result from the licensed system into the case | no re-typing; assessment and plan stay in step | licence and integration rights needed (cost, partnership); the instrument the pilot provider uses is UNKNOWN; scores inside NAVORA move it toward D-34's grey zone |
| B | Instrument-neutral: NAVORA records date, instrument name and version (from a short reference list), the assessing registered nurse, a document reference and the resulting minutes per category; the assessment runs in the provider's licensed tool | no licence cost; works with any provider (D-12 B); keeps NAVORA an organisation and documentation tool (D-34) | the result is typed once (minutes per category); no item-level data for quality learning |
| C | NAVORA builds its own assessment form | full control | invents clinical rules (forbidden); not accepted by payers without validation; medical-device risk (D-34) |
| D | interRAI LTCF for everything | one instrument for home and nursing home | built for residential care; nursing homes are a later horizon (D-24) |

## Engineering impact
*requested 2026-10-24 (po).* Asked: (1) what option B adds to the schema (instrument and version as reference data, assessor, document reference, per-assessment minutes) and whether a second and later assessment can be stored per case (today `needs_assessment_done_at` keeps only the first; relevant to Q12 and ORD0-C2); (2) for A, which interfaces are realistic (file export/import, an API of the licensed system), what is UNKNOWN until a vendor is named; (3) reversibility: B's records stay valid under a later A.

## Clinical impact
*requested 2026-10-24 (cra).* Asked: (1) which instruments are in use for home-care needs assessment in Switzerland and what NAVORA must know about the one used (name, version, assessor qualification), as information for the owner, not as a rule; (2) whether B's record (instrument, date, assessor, minutes per category) is enough for continuity and for ORD0 question 6, or whether the care plan needs the assessment's problem list (which would be clinical content); (3) the hazards of re-typing the result (transcription errors in minutes or category) and how to guard them; (4) what only the Clinical Owner can approve (the instrument choice for the pilot provider, the reassessment interval).

## Legal/compliance impact
*requested 2026-10-24 (lca).* Involved because the instrument is the basis of OKP billing and touches licences and health data. Asked, with sources: (1) does KLV Art. 8a or the tariff contracts read for D-18 and D-22 require a specific instrument or only a uniform, documented assessment; (2) what a licence for a licensed instrument covers if results are stored or displayed in a third-party system (A); (3) data-protection position of importing item-level results versus storing only the result (minimisation, D-3, D-9); (4) facts that depend on the canton (D-11), the provider (D-12) or the payer contract (D-18).

## Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| NAVORA adopts an instrument no payer or canton accepts | B records whatever the provider uses; `lca` question 1 |
| Licensed content copied into NAVORA | B stores no items or texts; A only under a written licence |
| Transcription error in minutes per category | J3's plan shows the source assessment; `cra` question 3 |
| Second assessment overwrites the first | `eng` question 1; ORD0-C2 (b) reads completed assessment visits, not the case field |

## Recommended default for NAVORA
Pending inputs. **PO working view, not a decision:** B now (instrument-neutral record, no items, no scores); A as a later integration once D-12 names the provider and its licensed tool, if the integration removes re-typing and `lca` finds the licence covers it; C rejected; D only within D-24. **Default until decided:** as built.

## Recommendation confidence
Low: all three inputs missing; the pilot provider's instrument is UNKNOWN (D-12).

## Owner approval required?
**yes: `owner`** for any licence or integration partnership (escalation criterion "funding, pricing or partnership"). **Preliminary:** B is reversible, costs no licence and approves no clinical rule; the PO may decide B as the interim design once `cra` confirms it is not a clinical rule and `lca` finds it compliant under every plausible interpretation. **Expected ask (later):** "approve an integration with <the provider's licensed instrument> at <cost>, or stay with B".
