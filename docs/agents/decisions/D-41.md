# D-41 — Approved NAVORA AI path for real data

## Decision summary
The founder (2026-11-06, point 10; FE-024) keeps **D-35 rule A in force**: no client or staff data reaches any AI service until a new, formal founder decision. The founder also wants AI to take over administrative work on real documentation and coordination later (FE-023, FE-028). This package prepares that later decision. It compares an **explicitly reviewed and approved NAVORA AI path** with keeping rule A, a self-hosted model, and the risk of unapproved external AI tools. **Nothing changes until the owner decides.** Preliminary route: `owner` (provider, contract, country, regulatory liability), with `lca` on the legal basis and `cra` on clinical documentation.

## Context and problem statement
- **IMPLEMENTED (synthetic data only):** AIG1 ledger, agent register, value-by-value confirmation, stop rule and look-back (migrations 0097, 0103, 0104); PLAN3 proposals (rule-based, no AI service) in open PRs.
- **PLANNED:** AIO1 orchestrator, agent contract and evaluation harness (M8); no AI provider is configured.
- **In force:** D-35 rule A (stage 0); the staged path 0-3 approved by the owner on 2026-10-27, where stage 1 (administrative L agents with AI on minimised real data) starts **after three stable pilot months** and needs a new owner package naming provider, region and contract. D-34 A plus C (core outside medical-device scope; clinical intelligence only in a separate module).
- **Tension (founder point 10):** AI drafting of real care documentation (founder point 6) is level G (D-34) and stage 2 (dictation and drafting) in the approved path, later than stage 1. Using it in the first real pilot (Phase B, FE-021) would need an earlier, stricter entry than the path foresees.
- **Related question, not decided here:** whether an AI provider may be used **with synthetic data only** in Phase A, so that the documentation and billing-check workflows can be tested for real (founder report, question O-4).

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Keep rule A beyond the first real pilot; AI only on synthetic data; real-data workflows run as rules and human work | no processor, no transfer, no secrecy question | the main AI value (documentation, billing checks) does not reach real users |
| B | **Approved NAVORA AI path:** every AI call through the NAVORA orchestrator only; one contracted provider in CH or EU (processor agreement, no training on NAVORA data, region and sub-processor control, deletion, audit and incident duties); minimisation or pseudonymisation per agent; AIG1 ledger for every proposal; human confirmation and signature unchanged; per agent an entry gate (evaluation on synthetic cases, `cra` gate, `lca` verdict, DPIA, licence holder's approval) | real value with bounded, auditable risk; one controlled path instead of many | contract and cost; legal basis per agent and professional secrecy (StGB 321) UNKNOWN until `lca`; DPIA needed |
| C | Self-hosted model inside NAVORA's own CH/EU hosting | no third-party AI processor | operating cost and model quality UNKNOWN; NAVORA carries model operations |
| D | No NAVORA path; staff use external AI tools on their own | none for patient safety or privacy | uncontrolled disclosure of health data, no ledger, no confirmation; **not acceptable**: whichever option is chosen, NAVORA states that unapproved external AI tools must not receive client or staff data, and designs so that no workflow needs them |

**Settled (on the sources read)**
1. Nothing in the founder direction changes the D-35 analysis: the ordinary DSG, professional secrecy and contract govern AI processing; there is no Swiss AI statute [A1], [M9].
2. **In BS there are two secrecy duties, federal and cantonal**, and the cantonal one expressly covers every health professional **and their Hilfspersonen** [K1] Ziff. 2.3. An AI provider (or NAVORA) acting for a home or a Spitex organisation in BS is therefore either a *Hilfsperson* bound by both duties, or a third party to whom disclosure needs consent or a release [K1] Ziff. 3, 5. BL: UNKNOWN (no source read).
3. The controller is the care provider (D-12 B as analysed earlier); an AI provider is NAVORA's sub-processor and needs the provider's prior approval ([P1] Art. 9 Abs. 3).
4. "No training, no own purpose" remains the condition on which the *Hilfsperson* reading rests ([E7] p. 29), and [K1]'s broad definition ("in irgendeiner Form … unterstützt") fits a provider that only performs the task; it does not fit one that uses inputs for itself.
5. A public-law nursing home or Spitex (a possible Phase B partner) would add cantonal data-protection law (IDG BS / IDG BL) [K1] Ziff. 8.2; the partner's legal form is UNKNOWN until Phase B.
