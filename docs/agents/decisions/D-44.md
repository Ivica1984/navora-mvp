# D-44 — Contract, legal form and billing path per order constellation

## Decision summary
For each constellation of [`BUSINESS_MODELS_AND_CONSTELLATIONS.md`](../../product/BUSINESS_MODELS_AND_CONSTELLATIONS.md) (KON-1 team for a nursing home, KON-3 team for a hospital stay or a transition of care, KON-4 private person; KON-2 and KON-5 B2B software), which contract form, which legal form of the team, which licence and which billing path are lawful in Basel-Stadt and Basel-Landschaft, and how NAVORA's fee is kept separate from the care revenue. **No recommendation yet** (inputs requested 2026-11-06). Approval: owner (commercial and regulatory liability), after a signed `lca` section.

## Context and problem statement
Founder decision FE-040 (2026-11-06) separates two business models: the team business (GM-1: a self-organised team takes orders directly; NAVORA earns a transparent fee) and the B2B software business (GM-2: a home or hospital licenses NAVORA for its own staff; KI-Planung core module, documentation add-on). It forbids assuming that a team's work in a home or hospital can be billed to a health insurer in addition. Today (IMPLEMENTED): billing preparation from confirmed facts (J5), no AI value in billing tables (AIR1); D-12 (owner, 2026-10-27) chose an operating-model partnership with a licensed provider (written for a Zurich provider; the pilot region is now Basel-Stadt/Basel-Landschaft, D-11). Safe default in force: Phase A only, synthetic data, no contract, no invoice to anyone real.

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Personalverleih | the team's legal entity lends its members to the home/hospital (KON-1, KON-3a) with an AVG licence | clear, established path; home keeps instruction right and responsibility | licence, deposit, GAV Personalverleih; the team is integrated into the home's hierarchy (tension with FE-032) |
| B Service contract (Auftrag) | the team delivers a defined service under its own professional responsibility (e.g. a whole shift or unit with its own responsible nurse) | keeps self-organisation; no lending | must meet the delimitation criteria (instruction right, integration, billing form, risk); home's licence duties (PDL) still apply; UNKNOWN whether permissible for direct resident care |
| C Licensed-provider partnership (D-12) | team members are employed by a licensed provider that runs on NAVORA; the provider contracts with homes and bills KVG ambulatory care | admission and billing exist; fastest for KON-3b/KON-4 | income gain depends on the partner's margin; partner needed in BS/BL |
| D Team-owned provider | the team founds its own licensed organisation (Art. 51 KVV) or members bill as freelance nurses (Art. 49 KVV) | maximum share for the nurses | licence, admission, management duties; small-team fixed costs; Scheinselbständigkeit risk for freelancers |
| E B2B only (KON-2, KON-5) | licence contract and data-processing agreement; no care service by NAVORA or a team | no care liability for NAVORA | D-41 (AI on real data), D-34 (MDR scope of the documentation add-on) |

## Engineering impact
*requested 2026-11-06* (`eng`, issue #928) — which contract and billing data the options need (order with activities, price basis per hour/shift/flat, invoice recipient per constellation, separation of U-T/U-N1/U-N2 in data and reports).

**Drafted by po, not confirmed by eng** (two-run rule: requested 2026-11-06 on #928; the `eng` runs of 2026-11-06 22:40, 23:30, 23:45 and 2026-11-07 00:20 did not answer; read on `recovery/main` `48cdb0c`, nothing tested).
1. **What exists:** billing preparation models only the KVG home-care path: `billable_facts` (0022: per visit, KLV A/B/C planned minutes, order basis, payer chain insurer/insurance type/residence, tariff versions), `tariff_rates` (payer `insurer`/`patient`/`residual`, unit `per_hour`/`per_day_cap`), `billing_rules` (minimum minutes per region). There is **no** order or engagement entity, no client institution as invoice recipient, no price per shift or flat rate, no team revenue or NAVORA fee anywhere (D-14 open), and no report that separates U-T, U-N1, U-N2.
2. **What each option needs in data:** A (Personalverleih) and B (Auftrag): an `engagement` per order (ordering institution, constellation KON-1/KON-3, activities from the QAM catalogue, price basis per hour/shift/flat, invoice recipient = institution, start/end, the responsible function per side), and an invoice line built from the performed visits/shifts of that engagement, **not** from `billable_facts` (no insurer path, FE-040). C (licensed-provider partnership): the existing KVG path under the partner's organisation, plus the engagement entity for KON-1. D (team-owned provider): as C with the team's own organisation as the provider. E (B2B only): no care invoice; a licence record per institution, outside the care data.
3. **Revenue separation (all options):** U-T (care revenue, the team's), U-N1 (NAVORA fee to the team) and U-N2 (B2B licence) must be separate tables or at least separate ledgers with no shared amount column, so no report can show a fee as a share of care revenue unless L-10 allows it. Reports per team and month only (as AIG1-7).
4. **Effort:** medium; nothing is built before the owner decides. Phase A can simulate KON-1 with a synthetic engagement and no invoice to a real party (the package's provisional default).

## Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| unlicensed staff lending (Art. 39 AVG fines for lender and hirer) | no real order before `lca`; KON-1 simulated only |
| double billing to an insurer | billing preparation refuses an insurer invoice for a visit in a home or hospital setting until L-8 is answered (rule for `eng` once D-44 is decided) |
| income promise not met | economics shown as scenarios with assumptions (`ECONOMICS_COMPARISON.md`) |

## Recommended default for NAVORA
PROVISIONAL until the inputs are in: Phase A simulates KON-1 and KON-2 without any contract or invoice to a real party; no insurer invoice for home or hospital settings; NAVORA's fee is billed only to the team (GM-1) or to the institution (GM-2), never as a share of care revenue (L-10 open).
