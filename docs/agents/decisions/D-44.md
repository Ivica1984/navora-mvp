# D-44 — Contract, legal form and billing path per order constellation

## Decision summary
For each constellation of [`BUSINESS_MODELS_AND_CONSTELLATIONS.md`](../../product/BUSINESS_MODELS_AND_CONSTELLATIONS.md) (KON-1 team for a nursing home, KON-3 team for a hospital stay or a transition of care, KON-4 private person; KON-2 and KON-5 B2B software), which contract form, which legal form of the team, which licence and which billing path are lawful in Basel-Stadt and Basel-Landschaft, and how NAVORA's fee is kept separate from the care revenue. **No recommendation yet** (inputs requested 2026-11-06). Approval: owner (commercial and regulatory liability), after a signed `lca` section.

## Context and problem statement
Founder decision FE-040 (2026-11-06) separates two business models: the team business (GM-1: a self-organised team takes orders directly; NAVORA earns a transparent fee) and the B2B software business (GM-2: a home or hospital licenses NAVORA for its own staff; KI-Planung core module, documentation add-on). It forbids assuming that a team's work in a home or hospital can be billed to a health insurer in addition. Today (IMPLEMENTED): billing preparation from confirmed facts (J5), no AI value in billing tables (AIR1); D-12 (owner, 2026-10-27) chose an operating-model partnership with a licensed provider (written for a Zurich provider; the pilot region is now Basel-Stadt/Basel-Landschaft, D-11). Safe default in force: Phase A only, synthetic data, no contract, no invoice to anyone real.

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Personalverleih | the team's legal entity lends its members to the home/hospital (KON-1, KON-3a) with an AVG licence | clear, established path; home keeps instruction right and responsibility | licence, deposit, GAV Personalverleih; the team is integrated into the home's hierarchy (tension with FE-032) |
| B Service contract (Auftrag) | the team delivers a defined service under its own professional responsibility (e.g. a whole shift or unit with its own responsible nurse) | keeps self-organisation; no lending | must meet the delimitation criteria (instruction right, integration, billing form, risk); home's licence duties (PDL) still apply; UNKNOWN whether permissible for direct resident care |
| C Licensed-provider partnership (D-12) | team members are employed by a licensed provider that runs on NAVORA; the provider contracts with homes and bills KVG ambulatory care | admission and billing exist; fastest for KON-3b/KON-4 | income gain depends on the partner's margin; partner needed in BS/BL |
| D Team-owned provider | the team founds its own licensed organisation (Art. 51 KVV) or members bill as freelance nurses (Art. 49 KVV) | maximum share for the nurses | licence, admission, management duties; small-team fixed costs; Scheinselbständigkeit risk for freelancers |
| E B2B only (KON-2, KON-5) | licence contract and data-processing agreement; no care service by NAVORA or a team | no care liability for NAVORA | D-41 (AI on real data), D-34 (MDR scope of the documentation add-on) |

**L-7: staff lending (V-1) or service contract (V-2)?**
- *Settled (secondary, [A1]):* a lender is whoever leaves workers to a host and cedes to it "wesentliche Weisungsbefugnisse"; further indicators are that the worker is "in persönlicher, organisatorischer, sachlicher und zeitlicher Hinsicht in die Arbeitsorganisation des Einsatzbetriebes eingebunden", works with the host's tools and material, and that the host bears the risk of poor performance. The right to instruct is the main criterion; it may be split, but the host must not hold its essential part; framework requirements and statutory duties alone do not count; the client's wishes in person-centred services are not instructions; the test is the contract **and the actual work situation**, case by case.
- *Application (my reading, not a determination):* KON-1 with R3-c (the home's PDL supervises the team's work, the team works in the home's shifts with the home's material, priced per hour) shows most indicators of lending. V-2 needs the opposite: a defined service (a unit or shift) under the team's own responsible nurse who holds the instruction right, the team's own planning, the team carrying the risk of poor performance, a flat or unit price.
- *The structural conflict:* the home cannot give up the professional organisation and supervision of care for its own residents ([BS1] § 36 Abs. 1; [BL1] §§ 1, 4; see D-43). Every instruction the home's PDL must keep for licence reasons pushes KON-1 towards lending. V-2 inside a home is therefore plausible only if the team's entity is itself a licensed provider with its own responsible function, and whether BS/BL allow such a provider to deliver care to a home's residents is **UNKNOWN** (L-8).
- *UNKNOWN:* the threshold of *Gewerbsmässigkeit* (Art. 29 AVV) and the licence conditions (deposit, GAV Personalverleih): not readable this run.

**L-8: can an external team bill an insurer for care in a home or hospital?** KLV Art. 7 Abs. 1, as quoted in [A3], lists nursing homes (Art. 39 Abs. 3 KVG) as providers of their own; the home bills its residents' care by level (D-18, D-22). I found **no source** on whether a second admitted provider may also bill an insurer for a home resident's care: **UNKNOWN**. The package's protective default (no insurer invoice for any visit in a home or hospital setting; the team invoices only the institution) cannot breach KVG billing rules, because it bills no insurer at all.

**L-9: legal form and admission for ambulatory KVG billing (KON-3b, KON-4).** *Settled:* a self-employed nurse's OKP admission in BS needs proof of "2-jährigen praktischen Tätigkeit" under an admitted nurse (Art. 49 KVV as cited) and the quality requirements of Art. 58g KVV ([BS3]); a practice run in the name of an AG or GmbH needs an operating licence ([BS3]); a Spitex organisation needs an operating licence with the functions listed in D-43 ([BS2]) and an OKP admission ([BS4]). BL: operating licence under [BL1] §§ 2, 4 (≥ 80 Stellenprozente for lead and deputy). *UNKNOWN:* whether BS's *Verordnung über die Zulassung und Höchstzahlen von ambulanten Leistungserbringern* (17 June 2025, named in [BS4], not read) caps admissions for nurses or Spitex organisations; whether BS/BL residual financing is paid to a private or team-owned provider on the same terms as to public Spitex (BL: Normkosten, D-18).

**L-10: percentage fee or payment pass-through.** *Settled ([A2] p. 13-15):* a licence is needed by whoever "regelmässig und gegen Entgelt Arbeit vermittelt"; an internet platform that matches or lets parties find each other is "immer auch Arbeitsvermittlung im Sinne des AVG". That reading concerns placement of **employment**; whether matching a home with a team entity under a service contract (V-2) is placement in the AVG sense is **UNKNOWN** (AVV Art. 1 not read). Fees from jobseekers are restricted (Art. 9 AVG; details not readable in [A2]); a fee charged to nurses per placed order risks falling there. Payment flows through NAVORA: GwG/FINMA not read, **UNKNOWN**.

**L-11: self-employment.** *Settled ([S3]):* self-employment generally means working "unter eigenem Namen und auf eigene Rechnung", organising one's work freely, carrying one's own economic risk, with own infrastructure for several clients as a further indicator; "Die Kasse entscheidet". A nurse integrated into one home's shifts, paid by the hour, fits these criteria poorly. Members **employed by the team's own entity** avoid the status question for the members (the entity is then the employer, and the L-7 question moves to it).

**Proposal for *k* and the rule around it (all PLANNED; tests named for `eng`):**
- **K-1 · Count persons who contributed, not members on the list:** a team row is shown for a period only when at least **k = 5** distinct persons contributed to the counted events (for BK-8: distinct signers of the reports counted), not when five members exist. Reason: a team of six where two sign every report is a team of two for this measure. Five is above the minimum of 3 used in official statistics [K3] because the readers of a team row know the team (colleagues, coordinators) and so hold «weitere Daten» [K1].
- **K-2 · Dominance:** the team row is also withheld when one person accounts for **50 % or more** of the counted events in the period (the [K3] threshold for small groups). The check runs on the server; the actor never leaves the function (AUT-122 (a)).
- **K-3 · No recalculation:** when team rows are withheld, the organisation figure is shown only if the withheld remainder itself meets K-1 and K-2; otherwise the organisation figure is withheld too, or the next smallest team row is withheld as well, until no hidden figure can be found by subtraction.
- **K-4 · Fixed periods:** calendar month (or a longer fixed period), never a free date range and never a sliding window, so a reader cannot subtract two overlapping periods to isolate one shift or one person who joined or left.
- **K-5 · Who sees it:** the team sees its own row; an organisation administrator sees team rows only under K-1 to K-4; nobody outside the organisation (no client, no home of KON-1) sees a team row until UNKNOWN (2) is answered. No ranking of teams on one screen while a team has fewer than k contributors.
- **K-6 · Reach changes (AUT-117):** a count of reach changes per team is a team setting, not a behaviour judgement, and the audit already holds the actor for the setting act (section 00:20, G-4 (b)). It may stay per team without K-1 to K-3, provided the measure view carries no actor column (AUT-122 (a)) and shows no text of the change.
- **K-7 · Value in one place:** *k* = 5 and the 50 % share as one configured constant read by every measure function, so the owner can raise them without a code hunt; test: changing the constant in a pgTAP run changes which rows appear.
- Tests (BK-b/BK-g, for `eng` and `po` to place): seed team with 4 contributors → no team row, organisation figure only if K-3 holds; team with 5 contributors, one holding 3 of 5 events → no team row (K-2); two teams, one withheld, organisation total shown → assertion fails (K-3); catalogue: no measure function takes a free start and end date (K-4); no measure function returns an actor or member column (AUT-122 (a)).
- Inside the team, colleagues may still guess who discarded a segment from their own knowledge of who worked; no threshold removes that. It is acceptable only because the screen shows no person and no ranking and its purpose is team learning; it is a reason to keep the measure off any appraisal or pay (to state in the team privacy notice before real use [D1 Art. 6 Abs. 3]).
