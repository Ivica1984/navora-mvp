# D-14 — Platform fee and team surplus model

## Decision summary
How does NAVORA earn its money from a team's work (the platform fee), and what happens to what is left after pay and costs (the team surplus)? **PO working view, not a decision:** a fixed, published platform fee per billable hour (not a share of revenue), versioned and visible to every team member; the surplus is split by a written rule into a part distributed to the team's nurses and a part the team reinvests, and the distributed part follows **direct care delivered with quality held**, never hours present, visit count, category mix or (in nursing homes) the care-effort group. The **amounts** (fee level, distributed share) are pricing: `owner`. What the PO can fix within product authority is only the set of **design constraints** in *Recommended default*, which bind J7 whatever the owner chooses. Inputs requested: Engineering, Legal & Compliance (pay, profit share, KVG financing), Clinical & Regulatory on one point (perverse incentives on care).

## Context and problem statement
- **What it blocks:** J7 (team economics transparency) stays a draft (`briefs/J7.md`); without a model, the promise that recruits nurses ("higher income for more direct care") cannot be shown or checked. Value 3 (nurse income) of the owner's order, and the lever behind value 1 if income follows direct care.
- **IMPLEMENTED today:** nothing for fees or pay. J5 derives billable visit facts and a monthly revenue **estimate** (reference rows `validated = false`, no invoice, D-18). AN1 shows a within-visit care share; the North Star (care ÷ paid working time) is not measurable until D-23. No payroll data, no fee parameter, no surplus calculation exists in `supabase/migrations/` or `src/` (searched 2026-10-24 for `platform_fee`, `surplus`: no match).
- **Who pays whom is not settled:** the employer of the nurses and the holder of the OKP admission are D-12 (owner). Under D-12 option B (licensed partner organisation), NAVORA is a software and operating-model supplier to that organisation, and the "platform fee" is a contract price between two companies; the team's pay is the partner's employment matter. Under C (own entity), NAVORA is the employer. The model must work in both, or it must be decided after D-12.
- **Earlier PO notes (row):** pay more for more direct care, not more hours (2026-10-22); in nursing homes the care-effort group sets the tariff, so income must never follow the classification level (2026-10-22 15:05).
- **Safe default in force:** no fee or surplus is computed or shown; J7 draft; nobody's pay depends on NAVORA data.

## Options considered
**Fee (how NAVORA is paid):**
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Fixed fee per billable hour, published, versioned | predictable; the team keeps every efficiency gain; easy to check against J5 facts | NAVORA's income rises with volume, so it has a reason to favour more billed hours |
| F2 | Percentage of revenue | simple; NAVORA shares tariff risk | NAVORA gains from higher categories and higher rates; weakens the "income follows care, not classification" rule; opaque when residual financing differs by municipality |
| F3 | Fixed fee per team member per month (subscription) | no link to volume or category at all; strongest neutrality | small or part-time teams pay relatively more; NAVORA's revenue does not scale with care delivered |
| F4 | Fixed fee per client under care per month | follows clients, not hours; neutral to visit length | a team with heavy clients pays the same as one with light clients |

**Surplus (what the team does with what is left):**
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | All reinvested (training, equipment, reserve) | simplest; Buurtzorg-like | no direct income effect; value 3 unmet |
| S2 | A fixed share distributed equally per paid hour | simple, perceived as fair | rewards hours present, not care delivered (against the North Star) |
| S3 | A fixed share distributed by the team's own written rule, within guard rails set by NAVORA (no rule may use visit count, category mix or classification level) | team autonomy (value 4); the team owns fairness | needs a rule editor and an audit; risk of conflict inside a team |
| S4 | A share distributed in proportion to the team's **direct care share** above a floor, paid equally to members, only while quality and continuity guards hold | ties income to value 1; equal split avoids competition inside the team | the North Star needs D-23 first; guard thresholds are partly clinical |

## Engineering impact
*requested 2026-10-24 (po).* Asked: (1) which J5 and AN1 facts a fee per billable hour (F1), per member (F3) or per client (F4) would read, and whether each is computable today without new recording; (2) the effort for versioned fee and surplus parameters (reference data with validity, as proposed for D-18 option A) and a J7 view that shows source and assumptions per figure; (3) how S4's guards (visit-length distribution, quality events, continuity) could be computed from AN2 measures already built; (4) what separates billing facts, pay facts and team economics as data types (J7 criterion 5) and who may read each (no individual pay visible to colleagues, J7 criterion 4); (5) reversibility: a parameter change applies only from its validity date, never to a closed month.

## Clinical impact
*requested 2026-10-24 (cra), on one point only.* The fee and pay amounts are not clinical. Asked: which incentive hazards any of F1-F4 and S2-S4 create for care (shorter visits to fit more, avoiding heavy or complex clients, delaying a needs reassessment, classifying upward, under-reporting quality events to keep a surplus guard), and which guard measures `cra` would want before any income depends on NAVORA data. Any guard **threshold** that is a care-quality level is `clinical` (Clinical Owner); the PO sets none.

## Legal/compliance impact
*requested 2026-10-24 (lca).* Involved because the model touches pay, profit sharing, employment, the use of OKP and residual financing, and the contract between NAVORA and a licensed provider (D-12). Asked, each with sources and whether the working view is compliant under every plausible interpretation: (1) may part of the pay of employed nurses depend on a team result (profit share or bonus under the Code of Obligations), and what must the written rule contain; (2) may a provider financed by OKP contributions and cantonal or municipal residual financing distribute a surplus to its staff, or do service mandates (D-11) or the residual-financing rules require reinvestment or non-profit status; (3) does a fee per billable hour (F1) or a share of revenue (F2) paid to a software supplier raise a question under the KVG (economic efficiency, Art. 56; transparency of costs in the provider's cost accounting); (4) data protection of pay data inside a team (who may see what; J7 criterion 4); (5) which answers depend on the canton (D-11), the legal entity (D-12) or a collective agreement, named as missing facts. No verdict is drafted by the PO.

## Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Income rewards shorter visits or more visits | no option counts visits; S4 pays on direct care share with a visit-length guard; `cra` names further guards |
| Income rewards upward classification (category mix at home, care-effort group in nursing homes) | design constraint 2: no fee or pay rule reads category mix or classification level |
| NAVORA's fee pushes volume | F3 or F4 are volume-neutral; F1 is published and capped by the team's own planning (PLAN2 plans from the needs assessment only, D-18 product rule) |
| Pay depends on an estimate that later changes | constraint 4: nothing is paid from an estimate; J5 stays an estimate until D-18; J7 shows estimates only |
| A team rule (S3) treats a colleague unfairly | guard rails, an audited rule history, and the rule visible to every member |
| A surplus distribution is not allowed for a financed provider | `lca` question 2; S1 stays the fallback until answered |

## Recommended default for NAVORA
Pending inputs. **PO working view, not a decision.**
- **Design constraints (PO, product authority; they bind J7 and any later pay feature whatever the owner decides; PROVISIONAL until the inputs are in):**
  1. Every fee and pay parameter is published to the team, versioned with a validity date, and never applied to a closed month.
  2. No fee, pay or surplus rule reads visit count, KLV category mix, care-effort group or any classification level.
  3. A distributed surplus follows care delivered (the North Star once D-23 makes it measurable), shared equally inside the team or by the team's own audited rule, and only while guard measures hold; guard thresholds that are care-quality levels come from the Clinical Owner.
  4. Nothing is paid from an estimate: no pay effect before real invoicing (D-18) and recorded working time (D-23).
  5. No individual's pay is visible to colleagues (J7 criterion 4).
- **For the owner, later:** fee **F3 or F4** (volume-neutral) is the PO's lean, because NAVORA should not profit from more billed hours or higher categories; F1 is acceptable only with constraint 2 and a published cap. Surplus **S4**, with S3 as the team-autonomy variant, if `lca` finds a distribution permissible; S1 until then.
- **Default until decided:** nothing computed or shown; J7 draft.
