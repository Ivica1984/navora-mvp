# D-16 — Quality event types, severities, quality hold, and who lifts it

## Decision summary
Which quality event types NAVORA offers, what makes one serious, which ones set a quality hold, what a hold stops (today only billing), and who may lift it. **No recommendation yet.** The PO's earlier proposal (2026-10-22 16:15) stands as an option: a second registered nurse of the same team, not the author, lifts a hold with a reason; serious events are additionally reviewed at network level afterwards. Expected route: `clinical` (the named Clinical Owner, G-1).

## Context and problem statement
**Blocks:** the relaxation of QG1 (the lift role) and the clinical meaning of a hold. **Findings:** CGR-13 (closed for the wording "Abrechnungssperre"; the clinical meaning stays here); PS-5 (S1, open: a hold does not stop care); H-06.

**What NAVORA does today (IMPLEMENTED, read in the repository by the PO):**
- Event types come from a table with a `serious_default` per type; the provisional short list from the decision row (fall, wound, medication, acute decline, social situation) is engineering's seed, not a clinical rule (0020 header).
- Severity `low`, `medium`, `high`, `critical`; serious = `high` or `critical` (0020 `private.is_serious`).
- Visibility: the assigned team; serious events also coach and admin (0020:58-65).
- CR-018: a hold attaches to one visit and blocks only billing; CR-047: a hold appends a not-billable fact, lifting the last hold restores it (0043 §5).
- CR-055: a hold is called "Abrechnungssperre" everywhere, with the sentence that it does not govern care.
- Lift: coach or admin only, with a reason, once (0020:281, provisional until D-16): a management step outside the team.
- CR-020 / CR-041: a serious event is an OPS1 exception, P2 → P1 after 4 h, and keeps escalating while `in_review`.

**Safe default in force:** the provisional types and severities; a hold blocks billing only and is lifted by a coach or admin; serious events escalate and cannot be closed by hand.

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Confirm today's model | types, severities, coach/admin lift as built | no build | a hierarchy step outside the team; types not clinically chosen |

**(1) Must a provider have a named person or function that reviews serious quality events? May a team RN be that function?**
- Settled [Q1], [Q2]: basic quality requirements are a condition of OKP admission (KVV Art. 58g, as quoted). As quoted in [Q2], they cover qualified staff, a suitable quality-management system, **a suitable internal reporting and learning system**, membership in a national network for reporting undesirable events, and equipment for national quality measurements. The quality-management system must fit the size and complexity of the organisation ([Q1] on lit. b). If no national network exists, lit. c is not required for admission ([Q1], 2022).
- Settled [Q2] (Thurgau example): the canton approves a *Bereichsleitung Pflege* and a deputy who meet KVV Art. 49 lit. a and b and GesBG Art. 12. They sign that the requirements are met from licensing onward. The form assigns no named individual to incident handling.
- Settled [Q1]: compliance with the quality contracts under KVG Art. 58a is a condition for working at the expense of OKP, for members and non-members of the associations alike. [Q3]: the home-care quality contract (Spitex Schweiz, ASPS, curacasa, prio.swiss) has been negotiated. It has **not** been submitted to or approved by the Federal Council, and its content is not public.
- UNKNOWN: (i) whether the pilot canton requires a named function (for example the Bereichsleitung Pflege) to review or approve the handling of serious events (D-11); (ii) the content of the home-care quality contract ([Q3]); (iii) whether a national reporting network for home care exists today. The [Q1] statement dates from 2022.
- Reading: no source read requires a particular person to **lift a billing hold**. A billing hold is an internal billing control, and none of the sources regulates it. The sources require that an organisation-level reporting and learning system exists and that a cantonally approved nursing management is responsible for meeting the requirements. Under option B the lift happens in the team. That is compatible with this only if the after-the-fact review of serious events is part of the organisation's reporting and learning system and reaches the responsible nursing management. Whether a team RN may also be that management is a question for the pilot canton's licence. It cannot be answered from these sources.

**(2) Reporting duties for certain events**
- Settled [Q4]: anyone *may* report to the adult-protection authority (KESB) when a person appears to need help (Art. 443 Abs. 1). A person who learns of this "in amtlicher Tätigkeit" and cannot remedy it *must* report (Abs. 2). Both paragraphs reserve professional secrecy. **The cantons may add further reporting duties** (Abs. 3). Relevant to the seed types "social situation" and "acute decline".
- Settled [Q5]: serious or previously unknown adverse drug reactions "nach Art. 59 des Heilmittelgesetzes (HMG) meldepflichtig sind". The page addresses doctors and pharmacists. Relevant to the type "medication".
- UNKNOWN: (i) whether nurses or home-care organisations are themselves obliged under HMG Art. 59 (primary text not read); (ii) whether staff of a public-law home-care organisation act "in amtlicher Tätigkeit" (Art. 443 Abs. 2; depends on D-12 and the canton); (iii) cantonal reporting duties under Art. 443 Abs. 3, cantonal health law (for example unusual deaths, communicable diseases) and licence conditions (D-11); (iv) how professional secrecy (StGB Art. 321, not read) limits a report.
- Consequence for the product (advisory): NAVORA cannot list the reporting duties as rules today. A protective step that invents no rule would be a field on a serious event that records "reporting duty checked: yes, reported to <authority> / not applicable / open", with the person and the time. Which events require which report stays UNKNOWN until D-11 and a legal adviser answer.
