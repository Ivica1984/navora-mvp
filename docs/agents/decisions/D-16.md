# D-16 — Quality event types, severities, quality hold, and who lifts it

## Decision summary
Which quality event types NAVORA offers, what makes one serious, which ones set a quality hold, what a hold stops (today only billing), and who may lift it. **No recommendation yet.** The PO's earlier proposal (2026-10-22 16:15) stands as an option: a second registered nurse of the same team, not the author, lifts a hold with a reason; serious events are additionally reviewed at network level afterwards. Expected route: `clinical` (the named Clinical Owner, G-1).

## Context and problem statement
**Blocks:** the relaxation of QG1 (the lift role) and the clinical meaning of a hold. **Findings:** CGR-13 (closed for the wording "Abrechnungssperre"; the clinical meaning stays here); PS-5 (S1, open: a hold does not stop care); H-06.

**What NAVORA does today (IMPLEMENTED, read in the repository by the PO):**
- Event types come from a table with a `serious_default` per type; the provisional short list from the decision row (fall, wound, medication, acute decline, social situation) is engineering's seed, not a clinical rule (0020 header).
- Severity `low`, `medium`, `high`, `critical`; serious = `high` or `critical` (0020 `private.is_serious`).
- Visibility: the assigned team; serious events also coach and admin (0020:58-65).
- CR-018: a hold attaches to one visit and blocks only billing; CR-047: a hold appends a not-billable fact, lifting the last hold restores it (0043 §5).
- CR-055: a hold is called "Abrechnungssperre" everywhere, with the sentence that it does not govern care.
- Lift: coach or admin only, with a reason, once (0020:281, provisional until D-16): a management step outside the team.
- CR-020 / CR-041: a serious event is an OPS1 exception, P2 → P1 after 4 h, and keeps escalating while `in_review`.

**Safe default in force:** the provisional types and severities; a hold blocks billing only and is lifted by a coach or admin; serious events escalate and cannot be closed by hand.

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Confirm today's model | types, severities, coach/admin lift as built | no build | a hierarchy step outside the team; types not clinically chosen |
| B **Team lift by a second RN (PO proposal 2026-10-22)** | a registered nurse of the same team who is not the event's author lifts with a reason; serious events get a network-level review afterwards (coach or quality specialist), not a prior approval | team autonomy; no waiting for a coach; four-eyes principle kept | needs a rule on the review after the fact (`clinical`) |
| C Hold also governs care | a hold on a serious event stops or changes planning of the affected service until reviewed | addresses PS-5 (care continues as normal) | a clinical rule with a risk of stopping necessary care; needs the Clinical Owner |
| D Types from a recognised taxonomy | event types and severities follow a published classification the Clinical Owner names | comparable data; less invention | which taxonomy is UNKNOWN (`clinical`) |
