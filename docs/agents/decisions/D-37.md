# D-37 — interRAI HC as NAVORA's clinical foundation

## Decision summary
Owner direction 2026-10-27: *interRAI should become an important clinical foundation for care planning, continuity, reassessment and governance, within licensing and regulatory limits.*

D-13 (po-decided 2026-10-25, option B) made NAVORA **instrument-neutral**:
- the provider chooses the instrument;
- NAVORA records only that an assessment happened;
- integration (option A) was left as a later owner decision.

This package asks whether NAVORA designates **interRAI HC** as its clinical foundation in home care, and through which route.

## Context and problem statement
- **IMPLEMENTED:** nothing. D-13 B is decided but not built. NAVORA stores no interRAI item, scale, CAP or score (Clinical Rule Register, *Not in the code*).
- **Related decisions:**
  - **D-36:** licence scope; `inputs-requested`; option A in force.
  - **D-34:** intended purpose. Statement v2 excludes computed interRAI outputs from the core; computed outputs need a separately qualified module (C).
  - **D-12:** which provider and tool.
  - **D-24:** nursing homes, interRAI LTCF; a different instrument.
- **interRAI reference sections** in D-15, D-16, D-17, D-28, D-30 and D-31 (2026-10-27): interRAI informs D-15, D-16, D-17 and D-31 substantially or partly. It removes no clinical approval. All lines are UNKNOWN until verified.
- **What the owner's direction changes:** interRAI stops being "one instrument a provider might use". It becomes the instrument NAVORA's care planning, continuity, reassessment and governance features are designed around. Choosing an assessment instrument is not the same as computing its outputs: the computation (scales, CAPs) stays behind the licence and the D-34 module rule.

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Stay instrument-neutral (D-13 B only) | record that an assessment happened; no instrument preference | lowest licence and device risk; any provider fits | contradicts the owner's direction; care planning cannot build on assessment content |
| **B interRAI HC as the designated target instrument, integrated through the provider's licensed tool** | NAVORA's features are designed for interRAI HC. Assessment results enter NAVORA as **signed facts from the licensed tool**: date, assessor, the problem areas the tool reports, the assessor's own care-planning conclusions. NAVORA computes nothing until the module and licence allow it | follows the direction; no NAVORA computation (stays out of device level H); continuity and reassessment become possible; the governance aggregates come later | needs an integration agreement with the tool vendor or provider (UNKNOWN who); providers on another instrument fit less well |
| C B plus NAVORA computes scales and CAPs in a qualified clinical module | as B, and NAVORA's own module derives outputs under its own licence | full clinical foundation; independent of the provider's tool | needs an interRAI licence for NAVORA as software vendor (UNKNOWN whether granted); medical-device conformity (D-34 C); highest cost; not before horizon 2 |
| D Own instrument | NAVORA writes its own assessment | — | rejected in D-13 (no compliant route; invents clinical content) |

## Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| interRAI content copied or computed outside the licence | B imports signed facts only; nothing computed (D-34 statement v2) until C |
| Reported problem areas read as NAVORA's clinical judgement | they are shown as "aus Bedarfsabklärung vom <Datum>, <Name>"; the assessor's signature, not NAVORA, stands behind them |
| A provider without interRAI HC cannot use NAVORA | the D-13 B record stays instrument-neutral as the fallback for other instruments |
| Integration depends on one tool vendor | the import format is specified by NAVORA; any licensed tool can deliver it |

## Recommended default for NAVORA
**PO preliminary recommendation 2026-10-27 (Low until D-36 and the inputs):** **B.**
- interRAI HC is NAVORA's designated target instrument for home care.
- Results are integrated as signed facts from the provider's licensed tool.
- NAVORA computes no scale or CAP until D-34's clinical module (C) and a licence allow it. C becomes the horizon-2 target, through a new package.

**First build, inside B and needed in every option:** brief **ASM1**, the D-13 B record with its five acceptance criteria, interRAI HC listed as the designated instrument. The reassessment due display waits for the Clinical Owner's interval.

**Until decided:** D-13 B as decided; option A of D-36 (names only).
