# D-11 — Pilot canton and municipalities

## Decision summary
In which canton, and in which municipalities, does the first NAVORA pilot run? **No recommendation of a canton:** the choice depends on facts only the owner has (where the first teams and partners are, where an entity can be licensed, D-12) and it is strategic. The package names what the choice decides, which legal work each candidate already has, and the product criteria, so the owner answers one line: "canton X, municipalities Y". It stays with the `owner` whatever the inputs say.

## Context and problem statement
**Blocks (directly or as a missing fact named by `lca`):** D-18 (residual financing and the tariff contract; CGR-31 residence key), D-22 (cantonal rules for care without an order), D-3 and D-30 (cantonal secrecy, patient-record and data-protection law), D-12 (the cantonal operating licence), D-16 (cantonal quality obligations), D-25 (nursing-home leadership, Horizon 3), the re-seeding of cantonal reference data in J5 (CGR-07, CGR-08), and the statutory statistics hypothesis in `docs/product/CARE_OPERATING_MODEL.md`. Of 30 INDEX rows, at least nine name D-11 as missing information.

**What NAVORA does today (IMPLEMENTED):** cases store municipality and canton of residence (J1); J5 reference data hold federal insurer rates 2026 with source and no cantonal shares (nothing invented, `validated = false`); the user interface is German. **Safe default in force:** synthetic data only; no cantonal rule is assumed anywhere.

## Options considered
The PO does not choose a canton. Criteria the owner can apply (product view, PO):

| Criterion | Why it matters for the care model | Who supplies it |
|---|---|---|
| Where the first self-organised team(s) and a design-partner organisation are | a pilot needs real teams; nothing else matters if none are there | `owner` |
| Where an entity can hold the licence and OKP admission fastest (D-12) | without it nothing is billed | `owner` with `lca` |
| Residual-financing model of the canton and its municipalities | decides whether a small team is paid like an established Spitex organisation (nurse income, value 3) | `lca` |
| Cantonal law already read by `lca` (ZH GesG § 13, BE PatV Art. 4, SO GesG § 18 and guidance) | a canton already read shortens every open package | `lca` |
| German-speaking | the app is German today; French or Italian adds translation before the pilot | `eng` |
| Whether the canton or its municipalities give service mandates to new or small providers (`lca` 05:45: ZH Pflegegesetz § 5, § 13; BE Tarifblatt) | a mandate changes residual financing, so it decides whether a self-organised team earns like an established provider (value 3) and whether it must accept every client (continuity) | `lca`, then `owner` |
| A few municipalities close together | short travel keeps direct care share high (North Star) and makes COVER1 cover between teams possible | `owner` |

**What the canton decides for NAVORA (settled on the sources read, per topic)**
- *Admission and licence.* OKP admission is granted by the canton where the care is given, and a further canton needs its own procedure (KVG Art. 36, D-12 [E1]). The operating licence and its conditions are cantonal (KVV Art. 51, D-12 [E1], [E2]). ZH applies its health-law duties to Spitex institutions (GesG ZH § 39 with § 35 Abs. 2 lit. c, D-30 [S1]). **UNKNOWN for ZH, BE and SO:** the licence conditions and any staffing thresholds like Thurgau's (D-12 [E2]). This is the largest gap, because thresholds decide whether a small team can be licensed (D-12).
- *Residual financing: who pays and how much.* The cantons regulate it (KVG Art. 25a Abs. 5, D-18 [S2]).
  - ZH: the municipality of the client's civil-law residence pays the residual costs (§ 9 Abs. 4 [K1]); municipalities may run their own services or commission Spitex organisations or self-employed nurses (§ 5 Abs. 1 [K1]); subsidies for non-nursing services only for providers run or commissioned by the municipality (§ 13 Abs. 4 [K1]). The Normdefizit figures were not read; the canton collects data to set it [K2].
  - BE: the residual rate depends on the provider type: reference costs 100 % with a Leistungsvertrag, 85 % without, 80 % for independent nurses; residual financing for KLV a/b/c (CHF; the unit is not stated in [K3], most likely per hour): CHF 28.40/43.00/43.50 (with Leistungsvertrag, mobility excluded), 24.80/39.30/41.30 (without, mobility included), 18.80/33.30/35.80 (independent nurses, mobility included) [K3]. Who pays (canton or municipality) is not stated in [K3] (UNKNOWN).
  - SO: the residence municipality pays, settled through a Clearingstelle; providers without a basic-care mandate and independent nurses received fixed rates (2019: CHF 13.98/12.42/11.52 for a/b/c) [K4]. Current values UNKNOWN.
- *Patient contribution.* Federal cap CHF 15.35 per day for ambulatory care (BAG, 12 June 2026, D-22 [S9]); ZH charges half of the maximum (§ 9 Abs. 2 [K1]); BE and SO not read.
- *Patient record and secrecy.* Read for ZH, BE, SO (D-30): visible authorship and an unchanged original in all three; retention 10 years (ZH, BE minimum) and 20 years (SO guidance). These are the facts D-3, D-4 and D-30 need from the canton.
- *Data-protection law.* The federal DSG applies to processing by private persons (Art. 2 Abs. 1 lit. a [K5]); a private provider, and NAVORA as its processor, are under the DSG. Cantonal data-protection law applies to cantonal and municipal bodies. **UNKNOWN:** whether a private provider acting under a municipal mandate (ZH § 5 [K1], BE Leistungsvertrag [K3], SO basic-care mandate [K4]) falls under cantonal data-protection law for that task. It matters for D-3, D-4 and D-9.
- *Quality, reporting and statistics.* The basic quality requirements are federal (KVV Art. 58g, D-16 [Q1]); cantons may add reporting duties to the adult-protection authority (ZGB Art. 443 Abs. 3, D-16 [Q4]), not read for any canton. ZH requires yearly Spitex data including cantonal additional data [K2]; BE and SO not read. This is evidence for the statistics hypothesis in `CARE_OPERATING_MODEL.md` for ZH only.

**What [Z1] settles for Zurich (on the guidance)**
- *Who needs the licence:* care provided on behalf of a legal entity, rather than by a nurse with her own practice licence (Vorbemerkungen).
