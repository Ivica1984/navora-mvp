# D-11 — Pilot canton and municipalities

## Decision summary
In which canton, and in which municipalities, does the first NAVORA pilot run? **No recommendation of a canton:** the choice depends on facts only the owner has (where the first teams and partners are, where an entity can be licensed, D-12) and it is strategic. The package names what the choice decides, which legal work each candidate already has, and the product criteria, so the owner answers one line: "canton X, municipalities Y". It stays with the `owner` whatever the inputs say.

## Context and problem statement
**Blocks (directly or as a missing fact named by `lca`):** D-18 (residual financing and the tariff contract; CGR-31 residence key), D-22 (cantonal rules for care without an order), D-3 and D-30 (cantonal secrecy, patient-record and data-protection law), D-12 (the cantonal operating licence), D-16 (cantonal quality obligations), D-25 (nursing-home leadership, Horizon 3), the re-seeding of cantonal reference data in J5 (CGR-07, CGR-08), and the statutory statistics hypothesis in `docs/product/CARE_OPERATING_MODEL.md`. Of 30 INDEX rows, at least nine name D-11 as missing information.

**What NAVORA does today (IMPLEMENTED):** cases store municipality and canton of residence (J1); J5 reference data hold federal insurer rates 2026 with source and no cantonal shares (nothing invented, `validated = false`); the user interface is German. **Safe default in force:** synthetic data only; no cantonal rule is assumed anywhere.

## Options considered
The PO does not choose a canton. Criteria the owner can apply (product view, PO):

| Criterion | Why it matters for the care model | Who supplies it |
|---|---|---|
| Where the first self-organised team(s) and a design-partner organisation are | a pilot needs real teams; nothing else matters if none are there | `owner` |
| Where an entity can hold the licence and OKP admission fastest (D-12) | without it nothing is billed | `owner` with `lca` |
| Residual-financing model of the canton and its municipalities | decides whether a small team is paid like an established Spitex organisation (nurse income, value 3) | `lca` |
| Cantonal law already read by `lca` (ZH GesG § 13, BE PatV Art. 4, SO GesG § 18 and guidance) | a canton already read shortens every open package | `lca` |
| German-speaking | the app is German today; French or Italian adds translation before the pilot | `eng` |
| A few municipalities close together | short travel keeps direct care share high (North Star) and makes COVER1 cover between teams possible | `owner` |

## Engineering impact
*requested 2026-10-24 (po → eng).* Please state: what in the data model and reference data is canton-dependent today (residence, J5 reference rows, operating rules); what changes when a canton is set (re-seeding J5 cantonal rows, region for J2 and COVER1); cost of a second language; whether anything assumes one canton per organisation.

## Clinical impact
Not needed for the choice of canton (no clinical rule). Cantonal clinical obligations reach `cra` through the packages that depend on D-11 once it is answered.

## Legal/compliance impact
*requested 2026-10-24 (po → lca).* For ZH, BE and SO (the cantons whose law `lca` has already read) and any canton the owner names: what the canton decides for NAVORA (operating licence for home care, residual financing and who pays, quality and reporting duties, patient-record and secrecy rules, data-protection law for a private provider); which of the open packages (D-3, D-12, D-16, D-18, D-22, D-30) would become answerable; what remains UNKNOWN. Advisory: this informs the owner's choice, it does not make it.

## Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Packages stay Low because the canton is unknown | this package asks the owner once, with criteria |
| A canton is chosen for contacts while its financing pays small teams poorly | `lca` compares residual financing for the candidates |
| Assumptions about cantonal rules creep into code | no cantonal rule is built before D-11 and the dependent package |

## Recommended default for NAVORA
None for the canton (owner's choice). Until it is answered: synthetic data only; no cantonal rule in the code; packages that need the canton name it as missing.

## Recommendation confidence
Low: by design (strategic, facts only the owner has).

## Owner approval required?
**Yes: `owner`** (strategic choice; regulatory consequences). Asked once the `lca` comparison is in: "name the pilot canton and two to five municipalities". Until then nothing changes.
