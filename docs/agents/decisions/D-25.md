# D-25 — Nursing-home leadership licensing rules

## Decision summary
What do the pilot canton's operating-licence rules require of nursing-home leadership (a named home director, a nursing-care lead, ward leadership, their qualifications, staffing ratios or a qualification mix), and does a self-organised ward team without a ward leader, with a network clinical lead, satisfy them? The PO assumes no rule. The PO's first triage: this is a question of cantonal law for a horizon that is not being built (D-24 working view A: not now), so it is tier 4 and cannot be answered before D-11 names the canton. The package fixes the design question so that the answer, when it comes, can be applied without a new round: which roles NAVORA treats as **accountable roles the licence requires** (kept, named, never removed by software) and which as **operational layers** the teams can absorb. **Preliminary route: `legal`** (cantonal law; no legal adviser named), then `owner` if a pilot needs a cantonal permit. Inputs requested: Engineering, Clinical & Regulatory, Legal & Compliance.

## Context and problem statement
- **Source in the repository:** `docs/product/NURSING_HOME_TRANSFORMATION.md` (po, 2026-10-22, PLANNED). It reads the interRAI LTCF CH handbook as an organisation chart: up to five levels above the nurse at the bedside in a 96-bed home; the home director (*Heimleitung*) kept "as an accountable licence holder" and the head of care (*Leitung Betreuung und Pflege*) kept as a function, both with "cantonal requirements UNKNOWN, D-25"; ward leadership is UNKNOWN in the source. Proposals NH0 (ward-team pilot), NH1 (shift self-planning) and NH8 (network expert pool) are gated by D-25.
- **What it blocks:** NH0, NH1 and NH8; stage "1. Organise" of the transformation path (one ward self-organised, the ward-leader role suspended for the pilot). Nothing that is built or approved.
- **Related decisions:** D-24 (enter nursing homes at all; working view A now, B one ward after D-11, D-12, one pilot month and D-25), D-11 (canton), D-12 (who holds the licence), D-23 (working-time record; 24-hour rosters raise rest-period and night-work questions), D-13 (instrument).
- **What NAVORA does today (IMPLEMENTED):** nothing nursing-home specific. Teams, members with a qualification label (D-29 verification open), PLAN2 recurring planning, COVER1 cover offers (switched off), QG1 quality events. No ward, shift, resident or roster entity.
- **The home-care analogue already read by `lca`** (D-10, 11:55, ZH Spitex leaflet of November 2023): one responsible head of nursing with a practice licence and management experience carries responsibility for nursing matters; staff who are not professionally independent are supervised. That is a home-care source and says nothing about nursing homes; it shows the shape of the question only.
- **Safe default in force:** no nursing-home feature is built; any role the licence may require is treated as required until `lca` and, if needed, a legal adviser say otherwise.

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | No ward pilot until the pilot canton's rules are known (today) | assumes nothing | Horizon 3 waits on a legal answer that itself waits on D-11 |
| B | **Accountable roles kept, operational layer removed:** every role the licence names (licence holder or home director, nursing-care lead, any qualification it requires) stays named and accountable; only ward leadership, if the canton does not name it, is replaced by the self-organised ward team, with a named RN per shift; any staffing ratio or qualification mix becomes a hard constraint of the NH1 roster | keeps the licence intact; removes the layer with the most coordination work; the roster enforces the mix instead of a ward leader | needs the canton's text; if ward leadership is named, B shrinks to "the ward leader as a team member with the accountability, not the scheduling" |
| C | B plus a **network nursing-care lead** shared across homes instead of one per home | removes a second layer; scales | only if the canton allows a shared or part-time nursing lead; the heaviest legal question |
| D | Ask the canton for a pilot permit or conditions for one ward (*Bewilligung mit Auflagen* or a pilot arrangement) | the canton itself answers; evidence for others | a commercial and regulatory step (owner); time |

**Sources (read 2026-10-24):**
- [L8] Gesundheitsdirektion Kanton Zürich, "Merkblatt Betriebsbewilligung Pflegeinstitution", November 2023: Ziff. 2, 3.1, 5.1, 5.2, 5.3, 7.3, 11, Anhang C. **This is guidance of the licensing authority, not the law.** The bases it cites (GesG ZH, LS 810.1, §§ 35-37, 40; Pflegegesetz, LS 855.1; Verordnung über die Pflegeversorgung, LS 855.11) were **not read**.
- **BE:** the GSI licence application form for homes (January 2025) could not be read (host blocked for this session). **SO:** not read in this run. So only **ZH** is covered, as orientation until D-11 names the canton.

**1. Leadership roles named (ZH, [L8] 5.1):** an overall management, "eine kompetente und vertrauenswürdige Person", working at the operational level; a **nursing lead**: a diploma nurse HF/FH with at least two years in a nursing leadership role; a **deputy for both**, with equivalent qualification; the nursing lead "kann nicht im Job-Sharing ausgeübt werden" and is held by one person; a **medical lead**, a physician licensed in ZH. **Ward leadership (Stationsleitung, Abteilungsleitung) is not named.** The application includes an organisation chart "zum Nachweis einer zweckmässigen Führungsorganisation", showing supervisory and directive authority (7.3). Changes in the overall, nursing or medical leadership need prior approval (11).

**2. Staffing ratio and qualification mix (ZH, [L8] 5.2, 3.1), in guidance, not in the law read:** at least 50 % of the nursing staffing plan held by nursing professionals and EFZ-qualified staff; at least 25 % by diploma nurses (HF, FH or DN I); assistants and helpers at most 50 %; helpers with the SRK course; a Pflegefachperson covers at least one shift (the day shift) per ward (*Abteilung*) every day; around the clock a Pflegefachperson HF/FH/DN I is present in the house or on the ward, or reachable "innert nützlicher Frist" (usually 20 minutes); care is guaranteed 24 hours. Exceptions "auf begründetes Gesuch hin" (5.2). General "Kompetenzerweiterungen" for assistants are not allowed (5.3). These figures are concrete enough to be a versioned hard roster constraint (Engineering question 2), with [L8] recorded as the source and ZH as the scope.

**3. Shared or part-time nursing lead (C):** not addressed. What the text does say: one person, no job-sharing, a qualified deputy, and prior approval of any change. A lead shared across homes is not excluded by the words read, but each licence names an approved person, so the canton decides. UNKNOWN.

**4. A ward without a ward leader, a named RN per shift (B):** in ZH nothing read requires a ward leader, and B keeps every role named. The per-shift RN fits the day-shift and 24-hour rules (2). Two points keep it from being certain: the organisation chart must convince the authority that the leadership organisation is "zweckmässig" with clear directive authority (7.3), which is the authority's judgement; and the law behind the guidance was not read. Whether reorganising one ward must be reported is UNKNOWN: Ziff. 11 lists the overall, nursing and medical leadership, not ward roles.

**5. Pilot permit (D):** [L8] knows exceptions to the staffing requirements on a reasoned application (5.2) and prior approval of leadership changes (11). A specific pilot arrangement for an organisational model is not mentioned: UNKNOWN. Asking the Gesundheitsdirektion in advance is a known route in form, not a known outcome.

**Labour law** (24-hour team rosters, rest periods, night work) stays with D-23 or a new decision, as the package says.

**Verdicts:**
- **A** (no ward pilot, nothing built): compliant under every plausible interpretation.
- **B: unresolved: needs legal adviser, or the canton through D, after D-11.** Question: "Under the pilot canton's health law and licensing practice, does a nursing home whose organisation chart shows the roles the licence requires (overall management, nursing lead and deputy, medical lead) but no ward leaders, with one ward run by a self-organised team (a named registered nurse per shift, staffing mix enforced by the roster), meet the requirement of an appropriate leadership organisation, and does the change need prior approval or notice?"
- **C: unresolved: needs legal adviser and the canton.** Question: "May one person hold the nursing lead (*Leitung Pflege*) of several licensed homes, or hold it part-time, and with what deputy and presence?"
- **D:** an owner step (regulatory and partnership); not a legal verdict.

The accountable roles to protect from team commands (Engineering question 3) are, for ZH: overall management, nursing lead, both deputies and medical lead. BE and SO stay open.
