# D-35 — AI services: provider, basis and governance

## Decision summary
Before any agent in the [AI Operating Model Review](../../product/AI_OPERATING_MODEL_REVIEW.md) touches real data, NAVORA needs answers to two questions:
- whether client or staff data may be sent to an AI service at all;
- if so, to which provider and hosting country, on which legal basis, minimised how, recorded how, and for which agent levels (L, G, H; D-34).

**PO first triage:** the choice of provider, contract and country is `owner` (funding, partnership, possibly regulatory liability). The legal basis is a legal question for `lca` and, if unresolved, a legal adviser. Which agents may assist clinical documentation is `clinical` and D-34.

**Safe default in force:**
- no client or staff data reaches any AI service;
- agents run only on synthetic data;
- agents that need no AI are built as deterministic rules.

## Context and problem statement
- **Today (IMPLEMENTED):**
  - no AI service is called anywhere in the repository (`ARCHITECTURE.md`: "KI-Orchestrator … Anbieter und Betriebsort OPEN");
  - all data is synthetic;
  - push delivery already showed the pattern of a cross-border processor question (D-32, D-33).
- **Blocks:**
  - agents A2, A7 to A12 of the review;
  - the AI drafting steps in the Handbook's data flow;
  - NH4 and the NH report's agents.

  Agents A1, A3 to A6 are not blocked while they run as rules without an AI service.
- **Related:**
  - D-34 (module rule; levels G and H);
  - D-9 (legal basis at intake);
  - D-12 (processor and confidentiality terms, StGB Art. 321 *Hilfsperson* question);
  - D-2 (hosting);
  - D-4 (retention of ledger rows);
  - adviser question 8 in D-34 (AI Act and Swiss AI rules).

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | No AI service with real data until the pilot has run; AI only on synthetic data | no new processor, no transfer question | the edge-of-graph drafting gains wait |
| B | AI service in Switzerland or the EU, processor contract, no training on NAVORA data, minimised payloads, ledger; L and G agents first, H only as a D-34 module | earliest real value with a bounded risk | contract and cost; the legal basis per agent is UNKNOWN |
| C | Self-hosted model in the NAVORA hosting region | no third-party processor | hosting cost and operations; model quality UNKNOWN |
| D | Any provider, including outside the EU and CH | widest choice | transfer and secrecy questions as in D-33; not recommended |

## Engineering impact
*requested 2026-10-26 (po).* Asked of `eng`:

- how a provider call would be minimised and isolated (server-side only, no client-side keys; D-26 pattern);
- the cost of a stop rule per agent;
- which of A1, A3 to A6 can be pure rules.

## Clinical impact
*requested 2026-10-26 (po).* Asked of `cra`:
- the hazards of G and H agents (automation bias, H-03; omission in extraction; summarising away a safety fact);
- which forced-attention design is the minimum;
- which agents are clinical rules in substance (for the Clinical Owner).

## Legal/compliance impact
*requested 2026-10-26 (po).* Involved because the decision touches data protection, professional secrecy, processor contracts, cross-border transfer and regulatory compliance. Asked of `lca`:
- the legal basis for sending client health data to an AI processor (DSG; StGB Art. 321; cantonal health law, D-11);
- staff data in prompts;
- whether a Swiss or EU provider with a processor contract is compliant under every plausible interpretation;
- what the information duties are;
- whether the EU AI Act or Swiss rules apply to NAVORA (D-34 question 8);
- the retention of ledger rows (D-4).

## Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Client data leaves the scope through a prompt | default A; minimisation and scope inheritance in any later option |
| A confirmed draft carries an error into the record | forced attention, source per claim, correction-rate stop rule (review §8) |
| An H agent ships in the core | D-34 module rule; input-class control in every brief |
| The provider changes model or region silently | stop rule on provider, region or model change |

## Recommended default for NAVORA
**PO working view, not a decision:** A now. B as the target for L and G agents once `lca` finds it compliant under every plausible interpretation, the owner has chosen a provider and contract, and the ledger and stop rule are built. H agents only as a qualified module under D-34. Until decided: A.
