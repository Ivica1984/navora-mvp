# D-9 — Legal basis for processing client data at intake

## Decision summary
On which legal basis NAVORA (and the provider using it) may receive, store and pass on a client's data from the moment a referral arrives: the client's explicit consent, the care mandate (treatment contract) with professional secrecy, a cantonal health-law duty, or a combination; who must capture that basis (the referrer, intake, the team) and in which form; and what may be stored before it exists. **No recommendation yet:** the package waits for Engineering and Legal & Compliance input. The provisional consent gate stays in force. Expected route: `legal` (no legal adviser named → `owner`) unless `lca` finds one option compliant under every plausible interpretation.

## Context and problem statement
**Blocks:** REF2 (hospital referral without an intake coordinator, draft: "consent captured by the referrer in the form required by D-9"), any real client data (with D-3, D-29, D-12). **Findings:** none open; CGR-21 and CGR-28 concern D-3 (read scope), not the basis.

**What NAVORA does today (IMPLEMENTED, read in the repository by the PO):**
- Consent gate (PROVISIONAL, ADR-026; migration 0010:14-17, 130-146): converting a referral into a case requires `p_consent_confirmed = true`, otherwise the command is refused; who confirmed and when is stored (`intake_consent_confirmed_at`, `_by`, 0010:35-36) and audited (0010:44). The gate "only prevents silent creation"; its legal sufficiency is explicitly open (0010:16-17).
- J1 readiness counts consent as a blocking fact (0013:9).
- What is collected before conversion: the partner request with name, region and needs (row default: "collect no more than name, region and needs").
- The audit log records allowlisted columns only, no free text (0004:5-9, 0010:19-21).
- Only synthetic data exists (no real client data, D-1 open for the live database).

**Safe default in force:** keep the provisional consent gate; collect no more than name, region and needs before conversion; synthetic data only.

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Consent as the basis (today's gate) | explicit consent of the client (or her representative) before a case exists; recorded who, when, how | clear, visible to the client | consent can be withdrawn while care must continue; a person without capacity cannot consent; a hospital discharge on a Friday waits for a signature |
| B Care mandate / treatment contract | processing is necessary to perform the care the client asked for; consent only for what goes beyond it | matches how care works; no consent loop | needs a mandate before data flows; what exactly "necessary" covers is legal |
| C Statutory duty of the provider (cantonal health law, documentation duty) | the provider must keep a record once it treats | strongest for the record itself | does not cover the referral before the provider accepts; canton-specific (D-11) |
| D Combination: referral transfer on the referrer's basis (hospital discharge duty), the provider's processing on B/C, consent only for sharing beyond the team (e.g. COVER1, network) | each step on its own basis | fits REF2 and the care network | most to explain and build |

## Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Real client data processed without a valid basis | synthetic data only until D-9, D-3, D-12 are answered (D-1 for the live database) |
| A consent-only model delays care after discharge | option D is analysed; REF2 stays draft until then |
| A boolean "consent confirmed" is read as proof of more than it is | the gate stays labelled PROVISIONAL (ADR-026) |
