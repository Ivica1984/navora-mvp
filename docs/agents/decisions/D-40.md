# D-40 — Client- and relative-facing AI (Family Companion Layer, Check-In Services)

**Owner freeze (directive 2026-11-03 14:00, post-PR #822): specification only.** No implementation, no database table, no migration, no production architecture until the owner approves this package. Allowed: requirements analysis, specification, decision support. Source: AI domains 3 and 4 in [`AI_FIRST_ARCHITECTURE_UPDATE.md`](../../product/AI_FIRST_ARCHITECTURE_UPDATE.md) §5.2.

## Decision summary
May NAVORA offer clients and named relatives a channel in which AI shows logistics facts, routes messages and drafts replies (Family Companion), and may it run scheduled check-ins with clients between visits (Check-In)? If so, with which consent, transparency, representation rules and limits on what AI may answer? **Recommendation: not before the owner approves it (frozen).** In every option, AI gives no clinical advice to clients or relatives, and any non-logistics answer is sent by a team member (owner directive 2026-11-03: no automatic clinical recommendations to patients or relatives). Approval: `owner`.

## Context and problem statement
- **Why:** relatives phone the team to ask when the nurse comes (challenge row, HYPOTHESIS). Between visits nobody knows how the client is.
- **What NAVORA does today (IMPLEMENTED):** no client- or relative-facing access; the visit window and the responsible member are recorded per visit.
- **Safe default in force:** no such channel; D-9 (legal basis at intake), D-3 (read scope), D-35 A, D-1.
- **Blocks:** AI domains 3 and 4 (roadmap stages 1-3).

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | No client- or relative-facing channel in the pilot | no new access path | phone calls stay |
| B | Logistics only, rule-based, no AI: a consented relative sees visit window, who comes, changes | fewer calls; level L | consent per relative; representation of persons without capacity |
| C | B plus AI routing and reply drafts; a team member sends every non-logistics reply | less writing for the team | D-35 provider; transparency that an AI drafted |
| D | Check-ins with question sets the Clinical Owner approved; answers stored as client-reported facts; a deviation becomes a team task | contact between visits | clinical question sets (Clinical Owner); possible device function if interpreted (D-34) |

## Clinical impact
*requested 2026-11-03 (po → cra).*
- Hazards: a relative reads a logistics answer as clinical reassurance; a check-in answer is not seen in time; a missed escalation.
- Which option enters clinical decision-support territory (record it as a regulatory finding if so).
- What only the Clinical Owner can approve.

**Answered by `cra` 2026-11-03 17:30** (`reports/CLINICAL_GOVERNANCE.md`, section 17:30, PR #842, read by po on its branch; copied by po 2026-11-03 22:00 at `cra`'s request; advisory, no clinical rule set):
- **A:** no hazard added.
- **B** (rule-based logistics for a consented relative): a visit window or "who comes" is read as reassurance about the client's state. A cancelled or uncovered visit is shown to the relative only after the team knows it (no relative learns first).
- **C:** an AI reply draft on a non-logistics question is never sendable unchanged without a named team member's act (AIG1-3 pattern); a question that sounds urgent routes to the team with a response time, never to a queue without one.
- **D** (check-ins): clinical decision-support territory (**CGR-119**, regulatory finding). Question sets and bands are the Clinical Owner's; an answer outside the band becomes a team task **with a response time the Clinical Owner sets**; the channel tells the client that it is not an emergency line and how to reach help (H-28).
- `cra` recommendation: **A**; B is the only option without a new clinical rule.
- For the Clinical Owner (after G-1): check-in bands and response time (option D). For the regulatory adviser (D-34): classification of option D (CGR-119).

## Legal/compliance impact
*requested 2026-11-03 (po → lca).*
- **DSG and professional secrecy** for disclosure to relatives.
- **Consent and representation:** consent per relative; representation of a client without capacity (adult protection law).
- **Transparency** that AI drafted or routed a message.
- **Liability** for answers.
- **Medical-device and EU AI Act implications** of check-ins (with D-34).

## Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| A channel becomes an unmonitored clinical hotline | no clinical advice by AI in any option; non-logistics replies sent by a person; a team-task route with a response time |
| Disclosure to the wrong relative | named, consented relative only; audited reads |
