# D-43 — Mandate for clinical rule approval and the Qualification and Activity Matrix

## Decision summary
Founder decision FE-039 (2026-11-06) replaces the open request "name a Clinical Owner" (G-1, FD-02) with a package of **options and a defined mandate**. This package asks: which NAVORA product rules need a qualified clinical approval; whether a named professional or a review board carries it; what formal responsibilities a later real provider or nursing-home partner must hold; how that responsibility stays separate from the team's daily self-organisation; and which rules may run as provisional in Phase A (synthetic) and which must be binding before Phase B. It also frames the **Qualification and Activity Matrix (QAM)** that `cra` prepares and `lca` checks. The PO's preliminary option is B + D (below), confidence Low until the `cra` and `lca` sections are in. Approval: owner (governance mandate, regulatory liability).

## Context and problem statement
- **Three questions kept apart (FE-039, Blueprint §1.4):** team organisation (the team, by its rules); professional authority per activity and case (a verified QAM rule, never derived by the PO from a job title); formal responsibility required by law for a provider, home or visit (`lca` plus a qualified clinical professional; not a dispatcher). Terms *Pflegeleitung*, *klinische Fachverantwortung* and *Verantwortung für die Freigabe einer Produktregel* are not interchangeable. This package is about the **third term** (approval of product rules) and names the formal-responsibility question for `lca`.
- **IMPLEMENTED today:** no rule in the Clinical Rule Register is `APPROVED` (D-27); every clinical rule is `UNVALIDATED` or `PROVISIONAL`. The qualification gate is coarse: two values (`registered_nurse`, `care_assistant`, D-5, CR-009), recorded, not verified (D-29, CGR-16); a registered nurse is required for KLV-B minutes and needs assessments (CR-007, CR-052, CR-164). PLAN3 never proposes a member without a recorded qualification; "kein Vorschlag" names the failed limit; nothing is assigned without a named member's confirmation.
- **Rule families that, on the register, encode a clinical or care-organisation judgement** (candidates for the approval mandate; the list is the PO's reading of the register, `cra` to confirm): who may perform which activity (CR-007, CR-009, CR-051, CR-052, CR-164 and future QAM rows); safety-signal recipients and thresholds (CR-067, CR-068, D-28); overlap of visits for one member (CR-164, PLAN3-14, PLAN3-16, CGR-137, CGR-144); documentation, correction and countersignature (D-10, D-30, D-31); quality events and holds (D-16); readiness checklist, care-plan fields and KLV mapping (D-15); on-site protocol and pre-visit alerts (D-19, D-17).
- **Safe default in force:** provisional rules run on synthetic data only and are shown as provisional; no real client (FE-021, D-1); no rule `APPROVED`.
- **QAM v0.1 (`cra`, 2026-11-06 21:50, PR #923):** `docs/agents/clinical/QUALIFICATION_MATRIX.md`, every cell PROPOSED; CGR-145 (matrix loosens CR-007 for FaGe; not built before a database check, a delegation record and D-29 evidence) and CGR-146 (the licensed nursing-responsible role BS/BL require in a nursing home, modelled without line authority) bear directly on this package; `lca` questions L-1 to L-6 are part of the requested legal section (issue #924).
- **Related, not reopened here:** D-27 (role defined), D-29 (how a qualification is verified; frozen, awaiting owner), D-25 (nursing-home leadership licensing; `lca` re-analysis requested), D-15.

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **One named qualified clinical professional** (and a deputy) under a written mandate limited to approving product rules (register rows, QAM rows); no say over daily team planning | simple; one accountable signature | single point of failure and bias; one person may not cover home care, nursing home and hospital contexts; capacity |
| B | **Clinical review board** (for example 3 qualified persons covering home care, long-term care, and one with regulatory or physician background), written mandate, quorum, conflict rule, minutes per decision; approves product rules only | covers several contexts; four-eyes by design; survives one member leaving | slower; needs a chair and a procedure; members and terms are a commercial choice (owner) |
| C | **External qualified service** (contracted expert body) approves rules | independence; capacity on demand | contract and price (owner); availability UNKNOWN; distance from the product |
| D | **Split by phase and by party (combinable with A, B or C):** Phase A: the approval function marks rules *tested provisionally* on synthetic data; Phase B: rules applied to a real provider's clients additionally need the partner's legally required responsible functions to adopt them for their organisation | keeps product-rule approval separate from the partner's legal duties; matches FE-021 phases | needs `lca` to name the partner's formal functions (D-25); two signatures before Phase B |

**(1) Formal responsible functions in BS and BL, and whether they carry daily dispatching.**
- *Settled (on the texts read):*
  - **BS, Spitex:** at least one "pflegeverantwortliche Fachperson" must be named; a Pflegedienstleitung (PDL) and a deputy PDL are named to meet the presence duty of § 22 Bewilligungsverordnung; PDL and deputy need a practice licence and a tertiary nursing degree "auf Niveau FH / HF"; a change of PDL or deputy is reported two months in advance ([BS2]).
  - **BS, every licensed operation:** "Die fachliche Organisation und Aufsicht eines Betriebs" lies with its medical or professional management, which meets the practice-licence requirements ([BS1] § 36 Abs. 1-2); the permit holder or a member of that management, or a deputy meeting the same requirements, is "in der Regel anwesend" during opening hours ([BS1] §§ 22 Abs. 1, 28 Abs. 1); the application carries a *Betriebskonzept* showing "Führungsorganisation, Verantwortlichkeiten" and a *Stellenplan* that secures the staff needed ([BS1] §§ 12 Abs. 1, 37 Abs. 2).
  - **BS, nursing homes:** PDL and deputy need a practice licence from the Bereich Gesundheitsversorgung ([BS3]).
  - **BL, homes and Spitex:** the application names "die für die Pflege fachlich verantwortliche Person sowie deren Stellvertretung" with qualifications ([BL1] §§ 1, 2); both must meet the requirements to practise "in eigener fachlicher Verantwortung" ([BL1] § 4 Abs. 1); **together at least 150 Stellenprozente in a home and 80 in a Spitex organisation** (exceptions by the Direktion, [BL1] § 4 Abs. 2); in homes at least 40 % of care staff with an EFZ or higher ([BL1] § 5); changes of licence-relevant facts are reported at once and unasked ([BL1] § 8). Their practice licence is granted inside the operating-licence procedure ([BL2]).
- *Dispatching:* **no text read requires the formal function to plan or dispatch daily work**, and neither BS nor BL, in what I read, requires an organisation chart with directive authority or a written delegation outside the management (the ZH leaflet does, D-25). But the function is not advisory either: in BS it carries "fachliche Organisation und Aufsicht" and a presence duty, in BL a minimum workload. Whether "fachliche Organisation" includes the organisation of work (who works when) or only professional standards is **UNKNOWN**; that is the core of L-2 and CGR-146.
- *Consequence for the matrix:* F5 as proposed (adopts the matrix version and delegation rules, reads and revokes delegations, last escalation, plans nobody; matrix §3.1) is **consistent with every text read**, but the BL minimum workload (150 % for lead and deputy in a home) means F5 is a staffed function with real time, not a signature. Whether "Aufsicht" in BS § 36 is met when F5 acts through rules and delegations instead of rosters: **unresolved**.

**(2) What service form and billing require of the performer, beyond the KLV category.**
- *Settled:* in BS anyone working "fachlich eigenverantwortlich" in nursing needs a practice licence ([BS3]); "Fachliche Tätigkeiten dürfen an Fachpersonen delegiert werden, welche dafür hinreichend ausgebildet sind" ([BS1] § 23 Abs. 4); supervision of persons in training is by a professional who meets the licensing requirements for that activity ([BS1] § 35 Abs. 3), which bears on **L-6**: a FaGe in training works on B activities, if at all, only under such supervision. Billable KLV services are those of admitted providers (nurses under Art. 49 KVV, organisations under Art. 51 KVV, nursing homes under Art. 39 Abs. 3 KVG), as quoted from KLV Art. 7 Abs. 1 in a commentary (read for D-44 this run, secondary).
- *UNKNOWN:* **L-1** (consolidated KLV Art. 7, 7a, 8, 8a; whether B11/B12 are Bst. b or c) and **L-5** (whether payment of a Bst. b activity depends on the performer's grade): still not read in the ordinance; carried. **L-3:** BS § 23 Abs. 4 permits delegation to adequately trained persons but does not say who delegates; a team nurse delegating without a line manager fits the words, whether it meets the organisation's Art. 51 KVV duty is UNKNOWN. **L-4** not researched this run.

**(3) Does a product-rule approval by A, B or C create a liability position for NAVORA; what must a mandate contain?**
- *Settled by the cantonal texts:* the professional organisation and supervision of a licensed operation lie with **its own** management ([BS1] § 36 Abs. 1; [BL1] §§ 1, 4). A NAVORA approval function (A, B or C) therefore **cannot replace** the licence holder's adoption of a rule for its residents or clients. This is what option D adds, and on these texts **D is necessary for Phase B, whichever of A, B or C is chosen**.
- *UNKNOWN (not read):* whether an approval by NAVORA's board creates contractual (towards the partner) or non-contractual liability (towards a client or resident) for NAVORA, and whether software counts as a product under product-liability law. I did not read the Code of Obligations or the PrHG this run and do not assess it.
- *Minimum content of the mandate (drafting checklist from the package and the texts above, not a legal rule):* scope limited to product rules (register and QAM rows) per context, canton and validity; express exclusion of daily planning, individual care decisions and the partner's F5 duties; the approvers' qualifications per rule family (`cra` 22:35 (4)); quorum, conflict rule, minutes and versioned record per decision; the status reached (CR-179 ladder) and that nothing below `freigegeben` binds; revocation and re-review triggers (source change, canton, incident); that any rule applies to real clients only after the licence holder's F5 adopted it (D); the approvers' insurance and NAVORA's (commercial, owner).

**(4) May a team member check another member's evidence (D-29)?**
- *Settled:* GesReg is public since 1 February 2022 and serves "dem Schutz und der Information von Patientinnen und Patienten"; nursing is among the professions recorded ([G1]); which data fields are public was not shown. The duty to have the needed qualified staff is the licence holder's ([BS1] § 37 Abs. 2; [BL1] § 5).
- *Assessment:* a check is the licence holder's task; a member can perform it **on its behalf** if the licence holder's policy says so, the check is recorded with checker, source (register entry or original document) and date, and the result is visible only to those who need it (data minimisation, DSG as cited in D-9, not re-read). A self-check never counts (Blueprint §1.4). Whether the authority accepts a peer check as the licence holder's evidence is the licence holder's policy question (D-29), not a legal gap I can close.
