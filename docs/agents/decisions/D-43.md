# D-43 — Mandate for clinical rule approval and the Qualification and Activity Matrix

## Decision summary
Founder decision FE-039 (2026-11-06) replaces the open request "name a Clinical Owner" (G-1, FD-02) with a package of **options and a defined mandate**. This package asks: which NAVORA product rules need a qualified clinical approval; whether a named professional or a review board carries it; what formal responsibilities a later real provider or nursing-home partner must hold; how that responsibility stays separate from the team's daily self-organisation; and which rules may run as provisional in Phase A (synthetic) and which must be binding before Phase B. It also frames the **Qualification and Activity Matrix (QAM)** that `cra` prepares and `lca` checks. The PO's preliminary option is B + D (below), confidence Low until the `cra` and `lca` sections are in. Approval: owner (governance mandate, regulatory liability).

## Context and problem statement
- **Three questions kept apart (FE-039, Blueprint §1.4):** team organisation (the team, by its rules); professional authority per activity and case (a verified QAM rule, never derived by the PO from a job title); formal responsibility required by law for a provider, home or visit (`lca` plus a qualified clinical professional; not a dispatcher). Terms *Pflegeleitung*, *klinische Fachverantwortung* and *Verantwortung für die Freigabe einer Produktregel* are not interchangeable. This package is about the **third term** (approval of product rules) and names the formal-responsibility question for `lca`.
- **IMPLEMENTED today:** no rule in the Clinical Rule Register is `APPROVED` (D-27); every clinical rule is `UNVALIDATED` or `PROVISIONAL`. The qualification gate is coarse: two values (`registered_nurse`, `care_assistant`, D-5, CR-009), recorded, not verified (D-29, CGR-16); a registered nurse is required for KLV-B minutes and needs assessments (CR-007, CR-052, CR-164). PLAN3 never proposes a member without a recorded qualification; "kein Vorschlag" names the failed limit; nothing is assigned without a named member's confirmation.
- **Rule families that, on the register, encode a clinical or care-organisation judgement** (candidates for the approval mandate; the list is the PO's reading of the register, `cra` to confirm): who may perform which activity (CR-007, CR-009, CR-051, CR-052, CR-164 and future QAM rows); safety-signal recipients and thresholds (CR-067, CR-068, D-28); overlap of visits for one member (CR-164, PLAN3-14, PLAN3-16, CGR-137, CGR-144); documentation, correction and countersignature (D-10, D-30, D-31); quality events and holds (D-16); readiness checklist, care-plan fields and KLV mapping (D-15); on-site protocol and pre-visit alerts (D-19, D-17).
- **Safe default in force:** provisional rules run on synthetic data only and are shown as provisional; no real client (FE-021, D-1); no rule `APPROVED`.
- **QAM v0.1 (`cra`, 2026-11-06 21:50, PR #923):** `docs/agents/clinical/QUALIFICATION_MATRIX.md`, every cell PROPOSED; CGR-145 (matrix loosens CR-007 for FaGe; not built before a database check, a delegation record and D-29 evidence) and CGR-146 (the licensed nursing-responsible role BS/BL require in a nursing home, modelled without line authority) bear directly on this package; `lca` questions L-1 to L-6 are part of the requested legal section (issue #924).
- **Related, not reopened here:** D-27 (role defined), D-29 (how a qualification is verified; frozen, awaiting owner), D-25 (nursing-home leadership licensing; `lca` re-analysis requested), D-15.

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **One named qualified clinical professional** (and a deputy) under a written mandate limited to approving product rules (register rows, QAM rows); no say over daily team planning | simple; one accountable signature | single point of failure and bias; one person may not cover home care, nursing home and hospital contexts; capacity |
| B | **Clinical review board** (for example 3 qualified persons covering home care, long-term care, and one with regulatory or physician background), written mandate, quorum, conflict rule, minutes per decision; approves product rules only | covers several contexts; four-eyes by design; survives one member leaving | slower; needs a chair and a procedure; members and terms are a commercial choice (owner) |
| C | **External qualified service** (contracted expert body) approves rules | independence; capacity on demand | contract and price (owner); availability UNKNOWN; distance from the product |
| D | **Split by phase and by party (combinable with A, B or C):** Phase A: the approval function marks rules *tested provisionally* on synthetic data; Phase B: rules applied to a real provider's clients additionally need the partner's legally required responsible functions to adopt them for their organisation | keeps product-rule approval separate from the partner's legal duties; matches FE-021 phases | needs `lca` to name the partner's formal functions (D-25); two signatures before Phase B |

## Legal/compliance impact

Sources required; UNKNOWN where not settled.

## Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| A provisional gate is read as an approved prerequisite for real visits | every provisional rule shown as provisional (PLAN3-18, PLANNED); no real clients before Phase B; D option requires approval before Phase B |
| The approval function turns into a hierarchical line management | mandate limited to approving product rules; no access to daily planning decisions; separate from any partner function (Blueprint §1.4) |
| Job-title shortcuts become rules | QAM rows only with sources; `cra` researches limits; the PO derives nothing from a job title (FE-039) |
| Self-confirmation of a profile is taken as evidence | three evidence states; *selbst bestätigt* never equals *nachgewiesen* (Blueprint §1.4 step 4) |

## Recommended default for NAVORA
**PRELIMINARY (po, 2026-11-06, before inputs): B + D.** A small clinical review board with a written mandate limited to approving product rules (register and QAM rows) per context and canton; in Phase A it marks rules *tested provisionally on synthetic data*; before Phase B every rule in the families above that applies to real clients is `APPROVED` by the board and adopted by the partner's legally required responsible functions. Until the owner answers: no rule `APPROVED`; provisional rules on synthetic data only, shown as provisional; no new binding assignment rule to `eng` (FE-039).
