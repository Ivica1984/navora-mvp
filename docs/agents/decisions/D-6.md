# D-6 — Visit assignment (team / nurse / both) and full state list

## Decision summary
Who a visit is assigned to (the team, one nurse, or both) and which states a visit can be in. NAVORA ships **team plus an optional responsible member** and the states `planned`, `on_the_way`, `started`, `paused`, `completed`, `cancelled`, `no_show` and (since 0042) `interrupted` (CR-013). The row asks `clinical` to confirm. PO working view: the **assignment model is a workflow and product choice** (team autonomy, J3, J6, PLAN2, ABS1, COVER1 and SIG1 all build on it), which the PO may decide once `cra` confirms that it approves no clinical rule; the **clinical content inside the states** (when a state raises a safety signal, D-28; what a no-show requires on site, D-19; what an interrupted visit must document, D-31) stays with those packages. Inputs requested: Engineering and `cra`. No `lca` input: no legal, privacy, billing or contract question is involved (step 4 below).

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A (as built) | Every visit belongs to a team; one responsible member is optional; states as CR-013 | team autonomy (the team, not a dispatcher, owns the visit); every downstream item already uses it; an unassigned visit is visible (`visit_uncovered`, CGR-22) | an unassigned visit depends on the team noticing it (covered by the P1/P2 signal) |
| B | Every visit must have a responsible member at planning | no unowned visit | brings back a dispatcher-like step at planning; ABS1 release would create invalid visits |
| C | Assign to a nurse only, team implicit | simpler UI | loses team ownership, cover and the SIG1 team fallback |
| D | A plus an explicit `accepted` state (the member confirms her visit) | a positive "I will do it" | an extra tap per visit for no hazard found yet; `cra` to say whether it closes a hazard |
