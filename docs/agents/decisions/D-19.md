# D-19 — On-site protocol when the client does not open the door

## Decision summary
What a nurse must do on site when a client does not open: whom to call, in what order, how long to wait, when to call emergency services, and whether a care assistant may handle it alone. This is a **clinical protocol**; only the named Clinical Owner can approve it (D-27). NAVORA invents none of it. The package prepares the decision: what is built, which parts are product (how NAVORA records and shows the steps), and which legal questions sit inside (disclosure to an emergency contact, entering the home, who is reachable). Inputs requested: Engineering, `cra`, `lca`.

## Context and problem statement
- IMPLEMENTED (C1, 0024): `record_no_show` from `on_the_way` only, with a note; `record_no_show_outcome` with `client_reached`, `emergency_contact_informed`, `emergency_services_called` or `other` plus a note; a no-show without an outcome is a P1 exception (0024:534-536); since 0060 a P1 `no_show` safety signal goes to the responsible member and the present team members, then Operations (SIG1). CR-014 (UNVALIDATED, owner D-19). PS-4 (S1, open): "no protocol".
- **CGR-43 (`cra` 14:27, PR #294):** any recipient, a care assistant included, can take a P1 no-show signal and stop the step to Operations. The PO decided a protective interim on 2026-10-24 15:10 (SIG1-C1: a take by a member who is not a registered nurse does not stop the step for `no_show` and `visit_interrupted`) and put the question to the Clinical Owner in D-28 and here (question 4).
- PO 2026-10-22 14:30 (row): please confirm that a no-show may be recorded once the nurse has set off (`on_the_way`), not only after arrival; C1 allows that because earlier escalation is the safe side.
- Safe default in force: C1 records and signals only; NAVORA gives no instruction of its own; the provider's existing on-site practice applies (UNKNOWN to NAVORA).

**Legal check (step 4, po 2026-10-24):** yes. (a) Telling an emergency contact or a neighbour that the client did not open is a disclosure of health-related data and touches professional secrecy (StGB Art. 321) and the DSG; (b) entering the home (key safe, locksmith, police) touches the client's rights; (c) whether staff must remain reachable for the follow-up after working time is the Pikett question of D-32 (`lca` 10:47). So `lca` is asked.

## Options considered
| Option | What it means | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A (as built) | record + outcome code + P1 signal; no protocol in NAVORA | no invented rule | each nurse improvises; PS-4 stays S1 |
| B | the Clinical Owner writes a short protocol (steps, order, waiting time, emergency trigger); NAVORA shows it on the no-show screen and records each step done | consistent, auditable; the team needs no phone call to a coordinator | needs the Clinical Owner (G-1); content must not be invented by agents |
| C | B plus per-client data recorded at intake (key safe, emergency contact and consent to inform them, known habits) shown on the card | the nurse has what she needs at the door; overlaps D-17 (safety alerts before the first visit) | needs D-9 (legal basis at intake) and consent handling |

## Engineering impact
*requested 2026-10-24 (eng)*. Questions: (1) confirm the C1 build above and what an extra outcome step (B) costs (a step list as reference data, a per-step record); (2) whether C's per-client fields can reuse D-15/D-17 readiness fields; (3) SIG1-C1 effect on the no-show path (the step to Operations after a care assistant's take).

## Clinical impact
*requested 2026-10-24 (cra)*. Questions: (1) the hazards of a no-show (fall, deterioration behind a closed door) and the time-critical steps, as a structure for the Clinical Owner, not as a rule; (2) is recording from `on_the_way` (before arrival) safe (row question)? (3) which parts of B are clinical (steps, waiting time, emergency trigger) and which are product (how steps are shown and recorded)? (4) CGR-43: may a care assistant handle a no-show alone, or must a registered nurse be involved, and by when?

## Legal/compliance impact
*requested 2026-10-24 (lca)*. Questions: (1) under which conditions may the team tell an emergency contact or a neighbour that the client did not open (consent recorded at intake; presumed consent in an emergency; StGB Art. 321; DSG)? (2) who may decide to enter the home or call the police or emergency services, and what must be documented? (3) whether a follow-up duty after working time is Pikett (D-32 question 3). Name the facts that depend on the canton (D-11) or the provider's policy (D-12).

## Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| A client lies injured and the nurse leaves after one ring | P1 signal (SIG1) until an outcome is recorded; Operations step not stopped by a non-RN take (SIG1-C1) |
| An agent writes the protocol | forbidden (D-27); B needs the Clinical Owner's text |
| An emergency contact is informed without a basis | C only with D-9 and recorded consent |

## Recommended default for NAVORA
PROVISIONAL, until the inputs are in: **A as built, plus SIG1-C1**; target **B** with the Clinical Owner's text, then **C** once D-9 and D-17 allow the intake fields.
