# Remediation plan: CGR-102 (a correction of the assessment a care plan was written against leaves no marker)

**Finding:** `cra` CGR-102, Clinical Governance Report 2026-10-30 09:30 (PR #737), **advisory (S2)**, patient-safety relevant. Placed by the PO as **ASM1-C5** (`briefs/ASM1.md`, PO review 2026-10-30 10:15, `changes-requested`), built with ASM1-C4. Engineering: ADR-125, migration 0101.

**Risk (IMPLEMENTED fact before 0101):** the plan's basis was the **head** of each active item's supersede chain (`private.assessment_head`, 0100). A supersede is a correction that may keep the date and change minutes, period, instrument or assessor. The head is then the current record, so no "Neuere Bedarfsabklärung" marker is returned, the basis line keeps saying "Geschrieben gegen die Bedarfsabklärung vom <Datum>", and the minutes shown beside the items are no longer the ones the plan was written against. A team is not prompted to re-read its plan when its basis changed (D-13 criterion 3, H-08). pgTAP 100 wrote the plan after the correction, so the path was untested.

**Affected code and data:** new `public.care_plan_assessment_reviews` (ASM1-C4 act; append-only; not selectable through the table), `private.plan_item_basis`, `private.plan_item_bases`, `public.review_care_plan_against_assessment`, `public.team_case_assessments` (re-created with `corrected_basis_date`, `corrected_on`, `plan_review`); app: care-plan block on `/team` (`plan-assessment-basis.tsx`, `plan-review-button.tsx`), `features/assessment/rules.ts`. No data migration; `care_plan_items` rows are not changed by anything in 0101.

**Steps (IMPLEMENTED in the ASM1-C1 to -C5 PR, CI decides):**
1. CGR-102 (1): the reader returns, per case, whether an active item's basis record differs from the head of its supersede chain, with the basis's assessment date and the correction's entry date (Zurich).
2. CGR-102 (2): the care-plan block shows, at `info`, no block, no colour above `info`: "Die Bedarfsabklärung vom <Datum>, gegen die der Pflegeplan geschrieben ist, wurde am <Datum> korrigiert." (`uxa` may word it).
3. CGR-102 (3): the ASM1-C4 review act clears it: an item's basis becomes the record of the latest act that covered it, which is the current (head) record.
4. CGR-102 (5): the sentence sits in the care-plan block, outside the closed history of the assessment block (ASM1-C2).
5. Nothing is blocked, derived, signalled or reminded; PLAN2 generation, visit minutes and J5 facts unchanged (`cra` (c)).

**Tests:** pgTAP `101_asm1_c4_c5_plan_review_act.test.sql` (33): item written against record 2, record 2 superseded with the same date and other minutes → the fact is returned (and no "Neuere" marker, the CGR-102 gap); after the act → not returned; a correction of a record no active item rests on → not returned; every column of every `care_plan_items` row of the case identical before and after the acts; billing preview, billable facts and visits identical. vitest `asm1-conditions` (sentence text, `info` tone, no warning class). pgTAP 100 unchanged (74).

**Needs a decision (not coded):** none for CGR-102 itself. Related and unchanged: who may record the review act is a provisional default (a registered nurse of the case's team; `cra` no objection, `lca` compliant on the sources read); whether the act may rest on a record still "nicht bestätigt" is the **Clinical Owner's** (after G-1), allowed until then because the state stays visible.
