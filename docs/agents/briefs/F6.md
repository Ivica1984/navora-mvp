## Problem
`ARCHITECTURE.md` puts a human-approved report between the visit and everything that follows: *freigegebener Bericht → Quality Gate → billable_facts*. Without a signed report there is no documentation the client can inspect, nothing an insurer query can rely on, and no basis for billing. Operations already shows "report missing" with nothing to resolve it.

## Users and journey step
The nurse who performed the visit; a registered nurse of the team where a countersignature is required. Journey: Einsatz → Dokumentationsfreigabe.

## Outcome
Every completed visit ends with a report that the performing nurse signs; until then it is a draft that never counts as final or billable.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A completed visit produces a report draft with times, services performed per category, observation and handover, taken from F1–F4 data (no re-entry).
- [ ] The performing nurse submits and signs the report; the signature records who and when.
- [ ] Countersignature: until `clinical` answers D-10, a report of a visit performed by a `care_assistant` is final only after a `registered_nurse` of the team countersigns (safe default, PO 22:20). The rule is configuration, so D-10 can relax it without a rebuild. Visits by a `registered_nurse` need no countersignature.
- [ ] An unsigned report stays a draft and appears nowhere as final or billable.
- [ ] A signed report cannot be changed; a correction creates a new version with reason and link to the previous one.
- [ ] Operations' "report missing" item closes when the report is final.
- [ ] Negative: nobody outside the assigned team can read, sign or correct the report.

## Out of scope
AI-generated drafts (Gate 4), client portal access, insurer export.

## Constraints and references
Q4 (PO answer: the performing nurse signs), D-10 (`clinical`: countersignature rule), D-3 (permission matrix), D-4 (audit retention).
