## PO review — 2026-10-22 12:00: changes-requested (one rule)

Everything else in the brief is met (#43, ADR-038, CI green): facts only from final reports without an open hold; payer chain, order basis and tariff version recorded; non-billable reasons; dated, versioned reference data with source, cantonal shares not invented; immutable facts with corrections; working-time facts separate; preview labelled as an estimate, no invoice.

**Change: which minutes count (answers Q11).** The provisional rule bills the planned minutes even when less care was measured. That overstates revenue, and billing time that was not provided is not acceptable. New rule:

- [ ] Billable minutes per category never exceed the **measured care minutes** of the visit. When the measured total is below the planned total, the planned minutes per category are **scaled down proportionally** to the measured total.
- [ ] Measured minutes above the planned total are **not billed**; the visit is flagged "above plan" so the team can review the care plan (J3).
- [ ] The minimum billable duration is checked on the billable minutes after this rule.
- [ ] Exact minutes, no rounding or time units, until D-18 (tariff-contract billing rules) is answered; the preview keeps its "estimate" label.
- [ ] Existing facts are not rewritten; recalculated facts follow the brief's correction rule (new fact referencing the old one).

Later, not now: letting the nurse confirm the split per category at signing (F6) once pilot nurses say the scaled split is often wrong.

## Problem
NAVORA delivers care but cannot yet show that the model pays. Revenue per hour is three payers (insurer contribution by KLV category, patient contribution, residual financing by canton/municipality), and each depends on facts captured at intake (J1) and planning (J3). Without billable facts, gaps are found only when an invoice is rejected, and nobody can prove the pilot's hourly economics.

## Users and journey step
Assigned team, NAVORA Operations, founders. Journey: Quality Gate → billable_facts → Tarif-/Kostenträgerabgleich.

## Outcome
Per month, a team sees billable minutes per category and expected revenue split by payer, plus a list of visits that are not billable, each with the reason; no invoice is produced.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A billable fact is created only from a final, signed report (F6) with no open quality hold (QG1).
- [ ] Each fact records category, actual and planned minutes, the case's payer chain, order basis (with order / without order A/C), and the tariff version used.
- [ ] Visits below the minimum billable duration, category B without a valid order, or cases without an insurer are flagged non-billable with a reason.
- [ ] Tariff and residual-financing values are dated, versioned reference data per canton; never hard-coded.
- [ ] Facts are immutable; a correction creates a new fact referencing the old one.
- [ ] Billing facts and working-time facts are separate types.
- [ ] The preview is labelled as an estimate and produces no invoice.

## Out of scope
Invoice generation and insurer e-billing (after OKP admission), payroll, tariff negotiation.

## Constraints and references
KLV Art. 7a; at least 10 minutes billed per visit, invoiced per calendar month (Basel-Stadt Merkblatt 2026). Pilot canton: D-11 (needed for residual-financing rates). Category mapping: D-15. Legal entity and admission: D-12 gates **real invoicing only**, which is out of J5 scope, so it does not block J5.
