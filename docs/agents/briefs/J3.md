Deviations accepted: the team chooses the KLV category per service until `clinical` signs off a mapping (D-15); assignment is team plus responsible member with the RN check at start (D-6). PO did not run the tests; evidence is the merged test suite and its green CI on #32 and `recovery/main`.

## Problem
Planning is where billing is set up correctly or lost. #25 lets the team plan, move and remove visits with date, time and services. It does not record the service category (KLV A, B or C), planned minutes per category, or whether a valid medical order covers the visit. Without those, billing becomes a second data entry after the fact, and category B visits can be planned without an order.

## Users and journey step
Members of the assigned team. NAVORA does not plan visits. Journey: Teamentscheid → Teamplanung → Einsatz.

## Outcome
The team turns an accepted case into dated visits that already carry what billing needs: category, planned minutes and legal basis.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Only the assigned team creates, moves or removes visits; every change keeps the original and shows who and why. *(delivered in #25, to confirm the "why")*
- [ ] The first visit of a new case is the needs-assessment visit, done by a registered nurse (category A). Until it is done, it is the only visit that can be planned.
- [ ] A care-plan summary per case stores, per agreed service: category A/B/C, planned minutes per visit, frequency and review date.
- [ ] Each planned visit stores planned minutes per category, never only a total.
- [ ] A category B visit is rejected without a valid medical order on the case, and flagged when it falls after the order's validity end.
- [ ] A category B visit cannot be assigned to a member without registered-nurse qualification.
- [ ] A/C visits without an order are allowed and create an "inform GP" task on the case.
- [ ] Visits after the care-plan review date are flagged for reassessment.
- [ ] The care goals from the care-plan summary appear on the nurse's visit card in `/field`. *(added 2026-10-21 from the F1-F4 acceptance)*
- [ ] An uncovered visit within the next 24 hours alerts the team and then appears as an Operations exception.

## Out of scope
Route optimisation, travel-time calculation, client self-booking, the full interRAI HC instrument (later; attaches to the care-plan summary).

## Constraints and references
KLV Art. 7 (categories A/B/C), Art. 8a (needs assessment). Category B by registered nurses; C may be done by trained care assistants. Visit assignment model: D-6 (`clinical`). Care-plan fields and category mapping: D-15 (`clinical`).
