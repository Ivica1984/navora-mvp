## Problem
No care journey can run with real partners or teams until every read and write is scoped to the organisation, team and case a person actually works on. Engineering reports T8 (SSR auth, #7), T9 (tenancy and RLS, #8) and T10 (audited idempotent commands, #9) as delivered. F0 is the PO's acceptance frame for that work across J1–F6: one access model, designed once from the journeys, not per screen.

## Users and journey step
All users: partner (referral), NAVORA operations (intake, exceptions), team members (decision, planning, visit), coach and quality (later). Cross-cutting for the whole `ARCHITECTURE.md` data flow.

## Outcome
A partner sees only its own referrals, an offered team only the minimal offer, an assigned team its case, and nobody else sees anything.

## PO update 2026-10-21 22:20 — remaining scope after the engineering report

Engineering's per-criterion report (`reports/F0.md`, #33) confirms the review. **Remaining for F0 acceptance: item 2 only.**

1. ~~**Team-scoped clinical read.**~~ **Done in #34, accepted 22:35.** A nurse reads clinical case fields (diagnosis tags, cognitive and mobility status, date of birth, notes, care notes, quality events) only for cases of her own teams. Coach and operations read organisation-wide **without** health fields. Negative pgTAP per legacy table. This is D-3 option B. Engineering and PO both recommend it, it only restricts access, and no real data is affected, so build it now. Widening access later needs the owner's D-3 answer.
2. **Mandatory reasons.** Product rule: a reason is mandatory when a command **changes or undoes something already recorded, or overrides a safety or billing rule**. It is not required for forward steps in the normal flow.
   - Mandatory: time corrections, plan move/remove, care-plan retirement, team decline (all delivered); case pause, close and reopen; visit cancellation and no-show; changing insurer, insurance type or order status on a case that is already ready for matching; qualification change; report correction (F6); lifting a quality hold (QG1).
   - Not mandatory: depart, start, pause, resume, complete, referral conversion, first entry of any fact. Who and when are still audited.
