# F0 — Shared access foundation for the care journey

## Problem
No care journey can run with real partners or teams until every read and write is scoped to the organisation, team and case a person actually works on. Engineering reports T8 (SSR auth, #7), T9 (tenancy and RLS, #8) and T10 (audited idempotent commands, #9) as delivered. F0 is the PO's acceptance frame for that work across J1–F6: one access model, designed once from the journeys, not per screen.

## Users and journey step
All users: partner (referral), NAVORA operations (intake, exceptions), team members (decision, planning, visit), coach and quality (later). Cross-cutting for the whole `ARCHITECTURE.md` data flow.

## Outcome
A partner sees only its own referrals, an offered team only the minimal offer, an assigned team its case, and nobody else sees anything.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] The schema is reproducible from the versioned migrations in the repository; drift to live is listed (TD-19, #18, ADR-022). Applying it to live stays an owner production decision (ADR-008, M5).
- [ ] Organisations, teams, memberships and member qualifications (registered nurse / care assistant) exist as data, not labels. *(organisations, organisation roles and team memberships delivered in #8; **qualification missing**)*
- [ ] Each team member has exactly one qualification: `registered_nurse` or `care_assistant` (D-5, decided by the owner 2026-10-21).
- [ ] Only NAVORA administrators set or change a qualification (D-5); every change is audited; the member's own team can read it; nobody outside the organisation can.
- [ ] Negative: a nurse cannot change her own or a colleague's qualification.
- [ ] J2 and J3 read qualification through one shared rule, so the category B checks live in one place.
- [x] Negative: anonymous → nothing; another organisation → nothing (pgTAP 004, 011, 012, 013).
- [ ] Negative: a nurse or team member not on the case's team cannot read that case's clinical data (case details, visits, care notes, observations, quality events). *(observations are team-only in #22; cases, care_visits, care_notes, quality_events are readable by every organisation member under the interim `org_members_read` policy, #8. Scope per role is owner decision **D-3**; PO supports option B)*
- [x] Before acceptance, an offered team sees no client identity (#24, pgTAP 013). Expected hours and required qualification follow with J2 changes.
- [x] Every state-changing command writes an audit entry and is idempotent under resend (T10 #9, pgTAP 005, 009).
- [x] Tests and seeds use synthetic data. Whether the live rows are real people is owner/legal decision D-1, outside F0.
- ~~Partner A cannot read partner B's referrals.~~ Moved out of scope (see below).
