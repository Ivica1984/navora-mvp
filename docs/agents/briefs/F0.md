# F0 — Shared access foundation for the care journey

## Problem
No care journey can run with real partners or teams until every read and write is scoped to the organisation, team and case a person actually works on. Engineering reports T8 (SSR auth, #7), T9 (tenancy and RLS, #8) and T10 (audited idempotent commands, #9) as delivered. F0 is the PO's acceptance frame for that work across J1–F6: one access model, designed once from the journeys, not per screen.

## Users and journey step
All users: partner (referral), NAVORA operations (intake, exceptions), team members (decision, planning, visit), coach and quality (later). Cross-cutting for the whole `ARCHITECTURE.md` data flow.

## Outcome
A partner sees only its own referrals, an offered team only the minimal offer, an assigned team its case, and nobody else sees anything.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The live schema is reproducible from the versioned migrations in the repository; any remaining drift is listed (TD-19 report).
- [ ] Organisations, teams, memberships and member qualifications (registered nurse / care assistant) exist as data, not labels.
- [ ] Negative tests exist and pass: anonymous → nothing; partner A cannot read partner B's referrals; team X cannot read team Y's offers or cases; a team member not on the case's team cannot read its clinical data.
- [ ] Before acceptance, an offered team sees only care need, region, start, expected hours and required qualification, never client identity.
- [ ] Every state-changing command writes an audit entry (who, when, what, why) and is idempotent under resend.
- [ ] Only synthetic data is used outside production; nothing in tests or seeds is a real person.

## Out of scope
Break-glass access, MFA rollout, organisation self-administration (D-5), production hosting (D-2).

## Constraints and references
