# RLS4 — Acts per record, assignments to the team (OBS1-7 (c) inventory decided)

## Problem
OBS1-C1 (#688, 0096) closed five authorship and signer columns. Its inventory (`docs/engineering/READ_PATHS.md`, *Remaining person-linked column grants*, OBS1-7 (c)) lists 19 care-record tables where a team member can still select, row by row through the table API, a column naming a person, and filter or count it per person: who decided a team decision, who did a case task, who changed a plan, who wrote a quality event, whose working time a fact is, who created a billable fact. That is the same gap RLS3-4 and OBS1-7 closed for visit events and observations (D-23 term b: never a list or total per person). The PO decides on that list (OBS1-7 (c)); this brief is that decision.

## Users and journey step
Every team member and coordination role reading care records; no new screen. Cross-cutting (Visit, Documentation, Quality, Billing).

## Outcome
A column that records **who did an act** (decided, wrote, changed, completed, set, lifted, returned, countersigned, withdrew, created, recorded, confirmed, took) is read only per record through the record's existing reader, as RLS3-4. A column that records **who is responsible now** (an assignment the team must see to coordinate care) stays readable by the team. Nobody can list a colleague's acts through the Data API.

## PO revisit — 2026-10-30 07:20 CET: ADR-122 deviation 4, condition RLS4-C2 (`lca` 06:41 D-23)

**Why revisited:** the 06:10 acceptance of deviation 4 rested on the premise that `member_open_reports` is shown "at access end". `lca` 06:41 (advisory) read the code: `/admin` (`src/features/admin/queries.ts`) calls it **for every member on every page load**, so the administrator holds a standing per-person list of open reports with visit dates; the code comment "listed before an access end" does not match what it does. `lca`: the registered nurses' view (`team_open_reports`, `member_open_reports`) and the as-built administrator view are both compliant under every plausible interpretation on terms (t1)-(t3); the end-only administrator variant is the most protective and also compliant. Which to keep is a product choice. The PO's acceptance said it would revisit if `lca` found otherwise; the premise was wrong, so it is revisited.

**Decision (product authority): end-only variant for administrators; registered nurses' view as built.** *Why:* the day-to-day follow-up of an open report is the team's task: the team's registered nurses countersign and return reports (D-10) and see each colleague's open reports on `/field`; the author sees her own. The administrator's task is the access end (LEAVE1-12, ADM2-12), and only there does she need the list. A standing per-employee view of documentation backlog held by the employer is a management-oversight layer the care model removes (value 4 team autonomy, value 5 less administration; the same reasoning as COND1-5a for the unexported-days count). Patient safety is not traded: no open report becomes invisible to anyone who must act on it (H-12, PS-15); the leaver's list still appears before her access ends.
