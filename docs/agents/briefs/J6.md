PO did not run the tests; evidence is the merged test suite and green CI on the merge commit.

## Problem
Continuity is NAVORA's quality promise and the core of the self-organised team model. Fewer different nurses per client means better observation, fewer errors and more trust. Without a rule, plans drift to whoever is free, and nobody can see it.

## Users and journey step
Assigned team; Operations (read). Journey: Teamplanung → Einsatz.

## Outcome
Each case has a named primary nurse and an optional deputy in the team; planning prefers them; the team sees, per client, how many different nurses visited this month.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] An active case has exactly one primary nurse from the assigned team; the deputy is optional.
- [ ] Planning flags a visit assigned to neither primary nor deputy; it does not block it.
- [ ] Changing the primary nurse is logged with a reason.
- [ ] A monthly measure shows distinct nurses per client and the share of visits by the primary nurse, visible to team and Operations.
- [ ] The measure is never shown as an individual performance ranking.

## Out of scope
Hard continuity limits (wait for pilot data), matching by continuity (Gate 5).

## Constraints and references
