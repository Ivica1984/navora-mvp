## Problem
Operations enters every referral in Phase A, and three gaps make that step unsafe or invisible (code read by `eng` 2026-11-12 14:22, recorded in `specs/B1.md`; the PO did not re-read the lines this run):
- **Region is free text** compared exactly (0086:205, 0034:217; IMPLEMENTED). A typo or a legacy value («Bern West», «Basel Pilot») ends as «Teamsuche: kein Team mehr verfügbar» (Δ1): the client waits and nobody learns why.
- **A referral has no end other than «in Fall überführt»** (Δ5). A duplicate, an out-of-region request or a withdrawn one stays open forever; open referrals show no age, and nothing signals one that has passed its response deadline.
- **After «Fall eröffnen» nothing says what happened and what comes next** (Δ7, UXR-165): ready or not ready, and why.
- **The requester and the person who typed it are one field**: the data model makes Operations the requester, which hard-codes the intermediary role NAVORA removes (Lieferung 1b §4).

Benefit: no referral is lost to a typo or left without an end; Operations' Phase A role is visible as a stand-in, not a dispatch office; the measure «share of referrals entered or touched by Operations» becomes possible.

## Outcome
Every referral ends in exactly one of two recorded ways, its age is visible, an overdue one reaches Operations as a P3 exception, a region can only be chosen from the list, and after «Fall eröffnen» Operations reads what happened and what comes next. The requester is the referring body; Operations is recorded only as the person who entered it.

## Rules and their input class
- Region list membership and mapping: *visit/schedule fact* (administrative); displays and routes only by region, as today.
- P3 exception after the response deadline: *visit/schedule fact*; existing deadline values; it routes to Operations (escalates). Not *clinical content*; not *care-context fact recorded by a person*. No new threshold.
- Rejection reasons: administrative facts; no clinical rule.

## AI leverage
1. **Remove:** the free-text region and the open-forever referral disappear; a typo can no longer silently end a search.
2. **Rules:** region list membership; the existing response deadline per type; one rejection reason per referral.
3. **Monitor:** the overdue referral is watched continuously (P3 exception) instead of someone scanning the list.
4. **AI prepares:** none. No agent in B1.1 (reason: the step is a structured choice; an AI suggestion of a region or a duplicate would be a guess on synthetic data with no ledger yet; AUT-102 places intake automation later).
5. **Human reviews:** Operations reviews the «Region nicht zugeordnet» list and maps by hand.
6. **Human decides:** Operations decides to reject or to open a case; the team decides on the case (B1.2), never Operations.
7. **Human signs:** no signature added or removed.
- Administrative step removed: chasing a lost referral by phone; baseline measure: open referrals older than their deadline (AN3 counts once built).
- Gate: D-35 rule A (no data to any AI service); synthetic data only.
- The AI must never assign a team, reject a referral or map a region in this item.

## Vision check (FE-070 (7))
The founder's eight questions are referred to in FE-070 (7) but not written out in any repository document read this run (UNKNOWN wording; the PO asks the founder to confirm or replace this working list in Lieferung 3):
1. *Does it remove administrative work?* Yes: no chasing of lost or forgotten referrals; one structured end.
2. *Does it recreate dispatching or a management layer?* No: Operations is recorded as a stand-in, «Team zuweisen» does not exist, and the measure makes the stand-in share visible so it can fall.
3. *Does it serve direct care time?* Indirectly: fewer phone rounds about referrals that went nowhere.
4. *Continuity of care?* A client is no longer lost between referral and team because of a typo.
5. *Team autonomy?* Unchanged; the team still decides alone (B1.2).
6. *Patient safety?* A referral can no longer disappear silently; the overdue signal is raised by rule.
7. *Separation of revenue streams?* Not touched (no billing data).
8. *Simpler than an agency or Spitex office?* The referring body is the requester from day one; the later referrer view (Lieferung 4) replaces the stand-in without a data migration.
