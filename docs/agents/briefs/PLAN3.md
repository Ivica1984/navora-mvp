# PLAN3 — Day-plan and continuity proposal

## Problem
PLAN2 generates the visits from the care plan, but nobody proposes who takes them. A team member still sets `responsible_user_id` visit by visit (J3, 0014), balancing who knows the client, who is qualified, who is absent (ABS1) and who already has a full day. In a traditional provider this is the scheduling administrator's work; in a self-organised team it is a daily coordination task of a nurse, done in the evening or by phone. It is the largest remaining manual coordination task after PLAN2 (Strategic Alignment Review dimension 3, *partial*: "no planning proposal"; AI Operating Model Review A3 "Roster and day plan", level L). Continuity (value 2) depends on it: an unassigned or randomly assigned visit is where the client meets a stranger.

## Users and journey step
The team (every member may ask for and confirm a proposal; the team decides, not a planner). Journey: Planning → Visit.

## Outcome
For a chosen day, the team sees a proposal of who takes each open visit, built from rules it can read (the client's primary nurse first, then her deputy, then a member who has visited the client before, within qualification and presence). The team confirms it, or changes single assignments, in one screen. Every proposal and every change is recorded through AIG1, so the team and Operations can see how often the proposal is right.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **PLAN3-1 Proposal for one team and one day.** A team member asks for a proposal for a date from today to 14 days ahead. It covers the team's planned visits of that day without a responsible member and, if she ticks "auch zugewiesene neu vorschlagen", those with one. It proposes one responsible member per visit or "kein Vorschlag" with the reason. It writes nothing to `care_visits` (pgTAP: the visit rows are unchanged after a proposal).
- [ ] **PLAN3-2 Hard limits, never broken.** A member is proposed only if: she is an active member of the case's team on that date (ADM1, LEAVE1: access not ended, contract end not passed); she has no recorded absence covering the visit (ABS1); she meets the existing qualification rule of the visit (J3: a registered nurse for category B minutes and for a needs assessment, 0014:244); and the visit does not overlap another visit already assigned or proposed to her that day by its planned times. pgTAP one case each: absent, removed, not RN for a B visit, overlapping → not proposed.
- [ ] **PLAN3-3 Continuity order, stated.** Among members who pass PLAN3-2 the proposal prefers, in this order: the case's primary nurse (J6), her deputy, a member with the most completed visits to the client in the last 90 days, then the member with the fewest planned minutes that day; ties broken by a stable key stated in the ADR (not random; pgTAP proves the same input gives the same proposal). Each proposed line shows its reason ("Bezugspflegende", "Stellvertretung", "kennt die Klientin (n Besuche)", "freie Kapazität").
- [ ] **PLAN3-4 Load is visible, not judged.** The proposal shows each member's planned minutes for that day before and after the proposal. NAVORA sets no maximum; the team sees and decides (no labour-law rule is built; D-23 and the working-time record stay with the employer).
- [ ] **PLAN3-5 Confirm in the team.** The team member confirms the whole proposal or changes single lines; confirming sets `responsible_user_id` through the existing J3 command path (every existing J3 check applies again at confirm time; a line that fails is reported and not set). Each confirmed or changed line is an AIG1 confirmation (AIG1-3): proposal kept in the ledger, `corrected` where she chose another member. She confirms line by line or by an explicit list of lines she ticked; there is no "alle übernehmen" without the list. The ticked list is a bulk confirm under AIG1-5 (amended 15:20 and 18:10): `responsible_user_id` is registered with class `schedule`; `cra` confirmed the class on 2026-10-27 17:35 (#543, CGR-78) on the conditions written into AIG1-5 and PLAN3-9. pgTAP: a ticked list of three lines writes three AIG1 confirmation rows, and a line that fails a J3 check at confirm time is reported and not set while the other lines are set.
- [ ] **PLAN3-6 Registered as an AIG1 agent.** PLAN3 is agent `plan3_day_proposal`, kind `rule`, level `L`, with its synthetic evaluation fixture (AIG1-9) and a stop threshold written by the administrator who switches it on (AIG1-8). Off by default. When stopped, the team sees "Vorschlag zurzeit nicht verfügbar" and plans by hand as today.
- [ ] **PLAN3-7 Measures, at team level only.** Through AIG1-7: per team and month, the share of lines confirmed unchanged and corrected. AN2 adds, per team and month, the share of visits performed by the client's primary nurse or deputy, before and after PLAN3 is switched on for that team. Also per team and month (AUT-1): confirm actions per day plan (a ticked list counts as one action) and the median minutes from proposal to the last confirmed line; balancing measure: the share of lines corrected. No per-person figure (pgTAP: no person column; small-team suppression).
- [ ] **PLAN3-8 Scope.** A proposal reads only the asking member's team's cases, visits and members (scope inheritance, AIG1-6); a member of another team, Operations and a coach can neither ask for nor read a proposal for this team (pgTAP).
- [ ] **PLAN3-9 Screen.** On `/team`, "Tag vorschlagen" for a date; a list per member with her visits, reasons and minutes; confirm per line or by ticked list. **Ticked list (amended 18:10, CGR-78 (b)):** every line starts unticked; each line is ticked by its own tap; no "select all"; the confirm button names its count ("3 Zuteilungen bestätigen"). Each line shows the visit time, the minute category, the proposed member and, where it applies, "ändert bisherige Zuteilung" or "nicht Bezugspflege/Stellvertretung". A line refused by a J3 check at confirm time shows its reason and the visit stays in the existing uncovered-visit path. e2e at 390 px: ask; the proposal renders with no line ticked; no single control ticks more than one line; change one line; tick three of five and confirm exactly three; see the responsible members set and the ledger showing one `corrected` line.
- [ ] **PLAN3-10 The team decides whether it uses the proposal** (added by po 2026-10-30 00:15 before build; challenge "Why does the administrator switch a team's planning help on?"). The AIG1 register switch stays the organisation's (AIG1-1: the administrator switches the agent on with its written threshold and is accountable for it). Under it, each team turns "Tagesvorschlag" on or off **for itself**: any active member of the team may change it on `/team`, the change is audited (actor, time, team) and shown to the team as "Tagesvorschlag eingeschaltet am <Datum>". Default **off** per team. A team with it off sees no "Tag vorschlagen" and gets no proposal; with the agent `off` or `stopped`, every team sees PLAN3-6's "Vorschlag zurzeit nicht verfügbar" whatever its own setting. The team's switch date is the "switched on for that team" date PLAN3-7 measures before and after. No other role sets it for a team (Operations, coach and administrator included). **Tests:** pgTAP: a member of the team switches it on and the audit row names the team; a member of another team, Operations and a coach are refused; with the team off, a proposal request is refused and writes no ledger row; with the agent stopped and the team on, the request is refused as `stopped`. e2e at 390 px: switch on, ask, see the proposal; switch off, no "Tag vorschlagen". **Actor (amended 2026-10-30 01:10, `lca` D-35 00:40 note 1):** the switch actor is stored only in the audit row; no team view, list, reader or export returns it (the team sees "Tagesvorschlag eingeschaltet am <Datum>" with no name), and it is readable only per record through `read_audit_record`. No per-person list of team switches exists for any role (Operations, coach, administrator and owner included; `lca`: unresolved, adviser question 8). **Test:** one pgTAP assertion that no reader granted to `authenticated` returns the switch actor, and that `read_audit_record` does.
- [ ] **Negative.** No route or travel time, no geodata and no client address is read (location features stay Gate 5, Q7). No visit is moved, added, cancelled or re-timed. Cover across teams stays COVER1. No proposal reaches a person outside the team. No AI service.

## Rules and their input class
- PLAN3-2 limits are existing rules (J3 qualification, ABS1, ADM1/LEAVE1 membership); input class *visit/schedule fact*. No new rule.
- PLAN3-3 order is a planning preference, input class *visit/schedule fact* (who is primary nurse, who visited before, planned minutes). It ranks members, never clients; it routes no client and escalates nothing. Not for the adviser.
- No labour-law limit (rest time, maximum daily hours) is built; that stays with the employer and `legal` (D-23). If the pilot shows teams need one, it comes through a decision package, not a default.

## Out of scope
- Day order and travel optimisation (needs client location, Q7, and a privacy review; later increment).
- Proposals across teams (COVER1) and for more than one day at a time.
- Any AI model; PLAN3 is a deterministic rule agent.

- Is "completed visits to the client in the last 90 days" readable for the team under RLS2 without a new read? If not, propose the narrowest read.

## PO note — 2026-10-30 00:15 CET: PLAN3-10 (team decides whether it uses the proposal)

*Why does an administrator decide when a team starts using a planning help?* In a traditional organisation, a central planning office rolls a tool out team by team. In NAVORA the organisation's switch exists for accountability (who answers for the agent, its threshold and its stop; AIG1-1, D-35), not for deciding how a team plans its day. PLAN3-7 already measures "before and after PLAN3 is switched on **for that team**", but AIG1's switch is per organisation, so the measure had no team date. PLAN3-10 gives it one and leaves the use to the team (value 4), without weakening any control: the agent, its threshold, its stop and the confirmation per value stay as AIG1 builds them; a team switch can only narrow use, never widen it. No data is read that the team could not read. Not a clinical rule; no `cra` gate on the switch itself. `lca` may comment; it adds no processing beyond what the organisation switched on.
