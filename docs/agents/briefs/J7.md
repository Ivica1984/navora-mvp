# J7 — Team economics transparency

## Problem
Fairer pay is the argument that recruits nurses to NAVORA, and it only convinces when a team can check it. Nobody can see today how billable minutes relate to paid hours, platform fee and team surplus.

## Users and journey step
Team members, founders. Journey: billable_facts and checked working time → Teamökonomie (`ARCHITECTURE.md`, parallel flow).

## Outcome
For a month, a team sees paid hours, billable minutes and share, estimated revenue, platform fee and the amount left for pay and team surplus, each labelled as an estimate with its assumptions.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Revenue comes only from J5 facts; working hours only from confirmed visit time (F1–F4).
- [ ] Platform fee and pay assumptions are versioned parameters set by NAVORA and visible to the team.
- [ ] Every figure shows its source and assumptions and is marked as an estimate.
- [ ] No individual nurse's pay is visible to other team members.
- [ ] Billing facts, payroll facts and team economics stay separate data types.

## Out of scope
Payroll processing, payslips, tax and social-insurance calculation.

## Constraints and references
Platform fee and surplus model: D-14 (`owner`). Employment structure: D-12 (`owner`).

**PO 2026-10-24 16:20 (D-14 package, product authority, PROVISIONAL):** five design constraints bind this brief whatever the owner decides on amounts: (1) every fee and pay parameter is published to the team, versioned with a validity date, never applied to a closed month; (2) no fee, pay or surplus figure reads visit count, KLV category mix or any classification level; (3) a distributed surplus follows care delivered (the North Star once D-23), shared equally or by the team's audited rule, only while guard measures hold (care-quality thresholds from the Clinical Owner); (4) nothing is paid from an estimate (D-18, D-23); (5) no individual pay visible to colleagues.
