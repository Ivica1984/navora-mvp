## Problem
The field tool is the product centre (`MASTER_CONTEXT.md`). Nurses lose most administrative time reconstructing times and notes after the visit. Engineering proposed and delivered F1–F4 (#19–#22): own-team day view, state machine with append-only events, reasoned time corrections, observations and handover. The PO adopts it as the "Verified Visit" capability and accepts it against the criteria below.

## Users and journey step
Nurse in the field; the assigned team. Journey: Teamplanung → Einsatz → Dokumentation.

## Outcome
A nurse completes a visit on the phone with automatic, checkable time, a short structured observation and a handover for whoever comes next.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A nurse sees only visits of her own team, with minimal case context: name, address, care goals, alerts, last handover.
- [ ] Arrival, start, pause, resume and end are recorded as events; a resend never creates a duplicate.
- [ ] Only allowed state transitions succeed; others are rejected with a reason.
- [ ] A time correction keeps the original, the new value, who changed it and why.
- [ ] Observation and handover are visible to the whole assigned team and to nobody else.
- [ ] An observation cannot be changed later without a trace.
- [ ] Negative: a nurse from another team, or an anonymous user, gets nothing back.

## Out of scope
Offline writes (v1.1, needs its own security review), AI drafts (Gate 4), signed visit report (F6).
