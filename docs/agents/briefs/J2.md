# J2 — Team decision workflow

## Problem
Self-organisation is NAVORA's core promise: teams accept or decline on their own authority. But no case may wait silently, and hospital partners need a fast, dependable answer. #24 delivers team pull and atomic acceptance; it has no response time, no escalation and no qualification check.

## Users and journey step
Members of eligible teams; NAVORA operations on escalation only. Journey: Matching → Teamentscheid.

## Outcome
A team says yes or no within an agreed response time; an unanswered or fully declined case escalates to Operations with the reasons attached and is never auto-assigned.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Before acceptance the offer shows only care need, region, start date, expected hours per week and required qualification; no client identity. *(delivered in #24, to confirm)*
- [ ] Accept and decline are atomic and audited; decline requires a reason from a configured list (v1: capacity, qualification, distance, other). *(partly delivered)*
- [ ] Two simultaneous accepts or a resend produce exactly one assignment, enforced in the database. *(delivered in #24, to confirm)*
- [ ] A team without a registered-nurse member cannot accept a case that requires category B work (needs qualification data from F0).
- [ ] Each open request has a response deadline set per referral type (configurable, not hard-coded); after the deadline it shows as an Operations exception.
- [ ] When every eligible team has declined or let the deadline pass, the request escalates to Operations with all decline reasons; no automatic assignment.
- [ ] Negative: a team sees only offers for its region and eligibility; other teams' decisions stay invisible.

## Out of scope
Ranking engine, geodistance, continuity scoring, partial or shared acceptance, negotiation chat.

## Constraints and references
`DOMAIN_RULES.md` Teamautonomie; `PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md` Operations without dispatch. Response-time values per referral type: Q8.
