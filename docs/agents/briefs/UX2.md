# UX2 — One app shell for every role

## Problem
UX review 2026-10-27, finding U1 with U16 and U18 (issue #510; screenshots of `recovery/main` at `6abf941`, synthetic seed). The PO checked the code on `6e1143f`:

- IMPLEMENTED: `/field`, `/team` and `/work` use a dark-green header, a narrow centred column and Tailwind, with no shared navigation. `/operations`, `/intake`, `/analytics`, `/admin`, `/settings` and the first-MVP pages `/teams`, `/visits`, `/partners` use the first MVP's `Sidebar.tsx` shell, partly with inline `style={{…}}` layout (`admin-client.tsx:64`, `settings-client.tsx:86`, `operations-client.tsx:66`, `teams-client.tsx:142`, `visits-client.tsx:104`, `partners/page.tsx:14`).
- On the phone a nurse cannot reach `/settings` (push opt-in for safety signals) from `/field`; `/work` is reachable only through a button in the content.

A nurse moving between her own screens changes layout, colour, navigation and vocabulary. The product reads as two products, which costs trust with pilot teams and buyers and makes every new screen pick one of two patterns.

UX1 fixes the severe symptoms (phone width, menu per role, `/field` order, codes in text). UX2 removes the cause.

## Users and journey step
Every signed-in role: nurses and care assistants (Visit, Documentation), team members and coaches (Team Decision, Planning), Operations, administrators, intake. No data-flow step changes.

## Outcome
Every protected screen is part of one NAVORA app: on a phone a tab bar (Heute, Team, Arbeitstag, Mehr), on a desktop a sidebar, the same tokens and components everywhere, and every screen a role may use is reachable from the navigation.

## PO notes on part 1 (#742, ADR-124), 2026-10-30 11:10 CET (not a review; UX2 is `in-progress`)
- **ADR-124 deviation 1, product question answered (po, product authority): lapsing does not pause while team admission is off; nothing to build.** Reasons: (a) a paused lapse would keep a candidate's name and data in NAVORA for as long as an organisation leaves the switch off, against ADM2-8 (candidate data deleted on lapse; `lca` 2026-10-27 03:50 read the 14-day lapse with immediate deletion as compliant) and data minimisation; (b) while the switch is off the administration admits (ADM1), so a team proposal that is left waiting has no path to an outcome, and the team may propose again after switching on; (c) one constant and one sweep stay one rule, the same with the switch on or off. The confirming step keeps the one off-sentence as built ("Danach nimmt nur die Administration neue Mitglieder auf."); the dropped second sentence stays dropped because it would be false. ADM2's lapse-share measure reads lapses during switched-off periods separately (the switch is audited with its date); no new measure is built for this.
- Deviations 2 (per team), 4 (no duplicated e2e for qualified members) and 5 (one selector step in `team-admission.spec`) are seen; the PO decides them, with deviation 3 (the cover section "Abdeckung" still offers a take the server refuses without a qualification), at the UX2 review. For deviation 3 the expected direction is the UX2-22 rule (offer only what she may do now); Engineering proposes the read in the shell ADR.
