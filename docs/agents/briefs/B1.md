## Problem
Referral → case → team decision (J1, J2, C1, RET1, J6) is built and runs green as steps 1-2 of the BD flow, but is tested item by item and has known gaps found in the code (specs/B1.md, confirmed by `eng` 14:22 with file:line):
- a case closed before any team accepted and later reopened can never be matched again, and no exception is raised (Δ11; 0034:187, 0024:300-323, 0031:43). The client is lost without any signal;
- the referrer's free-text need (`need_summary`) is not carried to the case, so a safety word Operations does not transcribe never reaches the team (`cra` CGR-193 (4b), 0013; H-03, H-09);
- the region is free text compared case-sensitively (0086:205, 0034:217); a typo ends as «Teamsuche: kein Team mehr verfügbar» (Δ1);
- after «Fall eröffnen» and after «Zusage» the user gets no line saying what happened and what comes next (UXR-165, Δ7, Δ12);
- «Fall abschliessen» cancels planned visits without a confirmation (Δ14); visits suspended before today stay out of sight on the case card after a resume (Δ17);
- the coach sees lifecycle controls she cannot use (Δ15); NV002/NV003 planning refusals show as a generic error (Δ16);
- there is no e2e for B1-UX-2 (390 px, double tap), B1-UX-3 (decline) and B1-UX-6 (safety notes, with the CGR-162 negative).

## Users and journey step
Operations (`admin`, `operations`) at `/intake`; team members at `/team` and `/field`; coach and Operations at `/operations`. Journey steps 1 (Anfrage) and 2 (Team übernimmt) of `PRODUCT_BLOCKS.md` §5, column *ambulant*. The nursing-home intake (NH-A2) is B9.

## Outcome
A referral reaches a team of its region or a visible exception, every time, including after close and reopen; the team reads what the referrer wrote; each hand-over tells the user what happened and what is next.

## Acceptance criteria

## Rules and their input class
B1 adds **no new care, tariff or eligibility rule** (BD-6 applies as well).
- B1-1 restores the existing rule «a ready, active case without a team has an open team search» after reopen: *visit/schedule fact*.
- B1-2 displays a *care-context fact recorded by a person* (the referrer); display only, it ranks, routes and escalates nothing.
- B1-3 changes the input form of an existing equality, not the rule: *care-context fact recorded by a person*.
- B1-4 to B1-7 are texts and controls.

## AI leverage
1. **Remove:** Operations no longer re-checks whether a reopened case found a team (B1-1) and no longer transcribes the referrer's words as the only channel (B1-2); typo hunting for regions disappears (B1-3). 2. **Rules:** the existing matching rules only. 3. **Monitor:** the exception list keeps showing cases without a team (M7). 4. **AI prepares:** no AI in this block (no client data to any AI service, D-35 rule A). 5-7. The team decides the offer; Operations decides the opening; no signature changes.

- Gates: `cra` (B1-1, B1-2, B1-5), `lca` (B1-2 before build, B1-3), `uxa` (B1-4 to B1-7 texts and 390 px).
- Data: synthetic seed only; the live project is never touched.
- The AI must never: read the referrer's text, match teams or decide an offer in this block.
